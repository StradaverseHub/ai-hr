# PCB 2(II) Pin. 2012 blank templates

These are the actual blank official specimens attached by the reporter to item
`72b100cd-c83d-498a-9072-b97e1691763a`, recovered from its original attachments for
approved item `963d4f56-f1cf-4496-8575-3d3b45530456`. Both were inspected as text
and rendered pixels before use. Each is one native Letter page, 612 × 792 points,
created in Microsoft Office Excel 2007 on 3 January 2012.

- `pcb2ii-en.pdf`: original attachment `d5c4892c-0e28-4d01-914a-4677ad306870`,
  `PCB2II_Pin2012_2 - Eng Version.pdf`;
  SHA-256 `70348977d822a7a64a655e88dcd6da52ed033b394dce0442599ea3df456c2056`.
- `pcb2ii-ms.pdf`: original attachment `6956b99e-c174-4c3a-8569-7e3ae134f14e`,
  `PCB2II_Pin2012_2 - BM Version.pdf`;
  SHA-256 `5a9307c68e83278ac83d2d1124b9ef97bd6796ca20d859484a651e36d08309e3`.

The English document's official published link is
https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/PCB2II_Pin2012_2.pdf, confirmed by
HASiL's current Other Forms listing (category 16). Direct retrieval returned HTTP
500 in this round; the original attachment supplies the verified document. The BM
attachment's content, rather than its `_2` filename, establishes its language.
The current 2026 calculation specification also contains a BM specimen, but its
updated employee tax-number label differs; these approved Pin. 2012 originals own
this form's labels and geometry. They contain no employee or payment records.

The renderer overlays complete values on these unchanged blank pages. It preserves
all captions, grouped columns, twelve months, totals, three blank preceding-year
rows, sign-off rules and page dimensions. The two languages have separate measured
field coordinates. Dynamic text prefers 8.5 pt and has the reviewed 6 pt floor; unsupported glyphs and
fields exceeding their real bounds refuse the entire request rather than silently
replacing characters, clipping an identifier or adding a continuation page.

Employer name and address use the original three footer lines, word wrapping first
and grapheme wrapping only for an unbroken word. The name starts its own line.
Available width is 200.05 points in English and 189.85 points in Bahasa Malaysia
after padding; the complete block prefers 8.5 pt and may reduce to 6 pt. There is
no fixed character limit because font and glyph widths differ. Content that cannot
fit all three lines at that floor refuses preparation. Telephone No. uses the
selected employer's existing company telephone, remaining blank when absent.
Receipt references and payment dates are centred horizontally and vertically
within their original monthly boxes. Preview and Download share this renderer.
