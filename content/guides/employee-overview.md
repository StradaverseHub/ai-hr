Start with [My Workspace](/me). Your available pages depend on your employment, permissions and the modules enabled for your company.

## First sign-in

Use your organization's sign-in link. If HR invited you, follow that invitation's activation steps. If HR gave you a username and temporary password, use the supported sign-in page and change the password when prompted. Complete any required identity verification or MFA. Do not share your account.

If the app says your login is not linked to an employee, ask HR to check the exact account/person link. Creating another account is not a fix. Check the company or employment context shown when you hold multiple employments.

## Find the right page

| Job | Page |
| --- | --- |
| Personal details and employment overview | [My Workspace](/me) |
| Request leave and check balances | [My Leave](/leave) |
| Review requests and leave credit | [Leave history](/leave/history), [Leave credit](/leave/credit) |
| Clock in/out | [Clocking](/attendance/clock) |
| Check punches and calculated attendance | [My records](/attendance/my-records) |
| Request attendance changes | [Attendance requests](/attendance/requests) |
| Apply for planned overtime | [Plan overtime](/attendance/plan-ot) |
| Submit expenses | [Claims](/claims) |
| Complete reviews and goals | [My performance](/me/performance) |
| Check onboarding tasks or request equipment | [Lifecycle](/lifecycle) |
| Read messages and set delivery preferences | [Notifications](/me/notifications) |

Payslips and annual tax statements are available through your personal workspace when the relevant records and permissions are available. The directory and organization chart do not grant access to other people's private payroll data.

## Personal preferences

Use the top-bar language and theme controls. Changing language does not change your company's payroll or leave rules. The Knowledge Base shows a current translation when one is available; refreshed articles may appear in English while translations are being updated.

## When something is missing

Check whether you are in the expected company/employment context, whether HR has completed your employment setup, and whether the module is enabled. A submitted request remains pending until the required decision is made. Use the request's history and notification link to follow progress.
