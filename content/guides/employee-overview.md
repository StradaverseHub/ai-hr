Welcome to **AI HR** — your company's HR home for everything from clocking in to requesting leave, checking your payslips, and keeping your details up to date. This guide is for **employees** opening the app for the first time. By the end you'll know how to log in, find your way around the **My Profile** page, use the everyday pages in the sidebar, and set the app to your preferred language and theme. No special role is needed — everything here is available to every employee.

## Logging in

Open AI HR in your browser using the link your HR team gave you and sign in. Once you're in, you land on the **Dashboard** — the company overview. The very first place to make your own, though, is **My Profile**, which is all about *you*.

**Note:** AI HR can hold more than one company under one roof, and you may even hold more than one position. The app handles that for you — you don't have to pick a company to see your own information.

## A quick tour: where everything is

The screen has three parts that stay put as you move around:

- **The sidebar** runs down the left. It's your main menu — every page is one click away here.
- **The top bar** runs across the top. On the right you'll find the **company switcher**, the **language picker**, and the **light/dark toggle**.
- **The main area** in the middle shows whichever page you've opened.

In the sidebar, the everyday pages every employee can use are grouped at the top:

| Page | Where it goes | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | `/` | Company-wide overview: headcount, upcoming public holidays, who's recently joined |
| **My Profile** | `/me` | Your personal home — attendance, leave, pay, details |
| **Personnel** | `/personnel` | The staff directory — search and browse colleagues |
| **Org chart** | `/org` | The reporting structure as a visual tree |
| **Leave** | `/leave` | The shared leave calendar and your requests |
| **Attendance** | `/attendance` | Attendance records |

**Tip:** You can shrink the sidebar to a thin strip of icons. Click **Collapse** at the very bottom of the sidebar; hover any icon to see its label, and click **Expand** to bring the text back. Your choice is remembered the next time you visit.

**Note:** Don't worry if a colleague's screen has extra sections like **Manage** or **Admin** — those only appear for managers and HR admins. As an employee you'll see the everyday pages above, and that's by design.

## My Profile — your home page

In the left sidebar, click **My Profile** (the person icon). At the top you'll see your name, employee code, position and department. Below that the page is laid out as a set of cards.

### Clock in and out (today's attendance)

The **Today** card shows today's date. To record your day:

1. Click **Check in** when you start. The card switches to show the time you checked in, with a status pill — usually `present`, or `late` if you're past the cut-off.
2. When you finish, click **Check out**. The card then shows your full day as `check-in → check-out`.

Underneath, a short **Recent** list shows your last few days, each with the hours worked and an **OT** badge if you logged overtime.

**Forgot to clock in?** Open **Fix a missed punch** at the bottom of the card. Pick the **Date**, enter the **Check in** (and **Check out**) times, add an optional **Reason**, and click **Request correction**. This doesn't change your record on the spot — it goes to your manager or HR to approve, and you'll see it listed with a `pending`, `approved`, or `rejected` status.

### Leave — balances and requests

The **Leave** card lists each leave type with how much you have left, shown as `available / accrued`, plus a usage bar (solid = taken, faded = pending approval).

To book time off:

1. Choose the leave **Type** from the dropdown.
2. Pick your **From** and **To** dates.
3. Click **Request leave**.

Your request appears under **Requests** with a `pending` pill until it's decided, then turns `approved` or `rejected`.

**Tip:** The faded part of the usage bar is leave you've asked for but that hasn't been approved yet — handy for seeing what's still in the pipeline.

### My positions

If you hold more than one role across the group, the **My positions** card lists each one with its company, position, department, and pay. It also shows the date you joined. Most people will see a single position here.

### My details — what you can change yourself

Further down, the **My details** card lets you keep your own contact information current. Update your **Phone**, **Address**, **Date of birth**, and **Emergency contact** name and phone, then click **Save details**. These changes take effect immediately.

**Bank & tax** sits beside it and works differently. Your bank, account number, **PCB category** and number of **dependent children** are shown for reference, but because they affect your pay they can't be edited directly. Open **Request a change**, fill in only the fields you want to change (leave the rest blank to keep them), and click **Submit for approval**. HR reviews it before anything updates — you'll see the request with a `pending` status until then.

**Note:** Your bank account number is masked (only the last four digits show) for your privacy.

### Payslips and tax statement

The **My payslips** table lists each pay period with its company, gross, deductions, net amount, and status. Click a **pay period** to open the full payslip.

At the bottom, the **Tax statement (EA / C.P.8A)** card gives you a yearly statement to download for your personal income-tax filing with LHDN. Click **View / download** next to the year of assessment.

**Performance reviews**, if you have any, appear in their own table with a link to open or acknowledge each one.

## The other everyday pages

- **Personnel** (`/personnel`) — the company directory. Search for colleagues and open a profile to see their role and contact details.
- **Org chart** (`/org`) — the reporting structure drawn as a tree, so you can see who reports to whom. You can expand, collapse, zoom, and search.
- **Leave** (`/leave`) — a shared view of who's off and when, alongside your own requests. Your personal balances stay private to you.
- **Attendance** (`/attendance`) — attendance records over time.

## Switching language

AI HR speaks six languages. In the **top bar**, click the **languages icon** (the speech-bubble "文A" style symbol). A small menu opens — pick any of:

- **English**
- **简体中文** (Simplified Chinese)
- **Bahasa Melayu**
- **தமிழ்** (Tamil)
- **ไทย** (Thai)
- **Tiếng Việt** (Vietnamese)

The interface switches straight away and a tick marks your current choice. Your selection sticks for next time.

**Note:** This changes only the *display language* of the app. It does not change your payroll or leave rules — those follow your company's country settings (Malaysian EPF/KWSP, SOCSO/PERKESO, EIS/SIP, PCB and so on) no matter which language you read in.

## Light or dark theme

Right next to the language picker in the **top bar** is the **theme toggle**. Click the **moon** to switch to dark mode, or the **sun** to switch back to light. Pick whichever is easier on your eyes — your choice is remembered.

## Troubleshooting

- **"My Profile says I'm not linked."** Your login isn't connected to an employee record yet. Ask your HR admin to link your account.
- **I clicked Check in but nothing else appears.** Refresh the page — the **Today** card should now show your check-in time and a **Check out** button.
- **My leave or correction still says `pending`.** That's normal: it's waiting on your manager or HR to approve. You'll see it flip to `approved` or `rejected` once they decide.
- **I can't edit my bank or tax details.** Those are approval-gated by design. Use **Request a change** under **Bank & tax** and submit it for HR to review.
- **I don't see Manage or Admin in the sidebar.** Those are for managers and admins only — nothing is missing from your view.
