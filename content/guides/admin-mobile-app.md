Open [Mobile App Preview](/mobile-preview). Group administrators can edit the tenant's design; other signed-in users see the published experience available to them. Company-only administration does not grant permission to change the entire tenant's presentation.

## Edit and publish

1. Review the current draft and published design.
2. Configure branding, supported colors, typography, navigation and registered sections/widgets.
3. Use the inspector and reorder controls to refine the layout. Keep required navigation and readable contrast.
4. Save the draft. Drafts are private to authorized administrators and do not change employees' published view.
5. Review supported screen sizes and light/dark appearance. Preview interactions demonstrate the design; they do not submit real HR requests or punches.
6. Publish when ready. Review the resulting revision and published experience.

If another administrator changed the draft, refresh after a revision conflict and reconcile your changes. Do not repeatedly submit a stale page.

## Restore a release

Choose an available historical release and use the restore/rollback action. Restoration publishes a new snapshot; it does not erase earlier release history. Recheck the result after restoring.

## What design can change

The builder changes presentation within supported components. It does not grant modules, change leave rules, bypass permissions, run custom code or create a separate app-store binary for each company. Some capabilities require a compatible native app version.

Open the explicitly identified live experience when testing real HR actions. For administrators, the live preview is available at [Mobile live experience](/mobile-preview?live=1). Treat its clocking and submission actions as real changes to records.

Publishing a design is not evidence of app-store distribution or physical-device acceptance. Confirm the installation/distribution route with your platform team before directing employees to install an app.


## Customer backgrounds

In **Mobile App → Design → App backgrounds**, upload a dark image and a light image for
your customer. Preview both appearances, save the draft, then review and publish.
**Use default city background** removes the selected draft override; it takes effect only
after saving and publishing. Version history can restore an earlier published design.

Use PNG or JPEG files smaller than 4 MB, with each side at most 6000 pixels. The system
creates a clean portrait image for phones. Crop the subject with room for controls and
check readability in the preview. Uploads stay within your customer; an unfinished draft
is visible only to tenant-wide administrators.

Employee phones need the compatible OneHR+ version and updated server. Published artwork
refreshes when the app reloads its design, including on return to the foreground. Before
sign-in and while locked, the app uses the shared city background.
