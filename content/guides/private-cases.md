Open [My private cases](/me/cases) to write to your company's HR case handlers about a grievance, harassment, a safety concern or another sensitive matter. It is also offered on My Profile and, where it is set up, in AI Centre's reply when you raise such a matter in chat. AI Centre never reads, stores or sees what you write in a private case.

## Start a private case

Choose [Start a private case](/me/cases/new). Pick the company, choose what it is about and write what happened in your own words (up to 8,000 characters). Under "Who can read it", tick anyone the case is about or anyone who must not see it. You can also send it to the group administrator instead of the case handlers. Then choose Send to HR.

Your cases stay listed on My private cases with their status: Received, In review or Closed. Where notifications are switched on, you get an in-app notice when the status changes.

Private cases are offered only once an administrator has named at least one case handler for the company, and only to someone with an employee record in that company. If they are not set up for your company, the page says so: contact your HR team directly.

## Who can read a case

- You, and the company's current case handlers, except anyone you ticked.
- The group administrators instead, again except anyone you ticked, if you send it to them or leave out every case handler.
- Nobody else. Other HR administrators, managers and AI Centre cannot read it, and naming a handler does not let the administrator who named them read cases.

Handlers see your name with the case: it is private, not anonymous. The text is stored encrypted, each time a case is opened it is recorded, and notices about a case never include its content. If you leave out everyone who could read it, the case is still kept, but nobody can read it until another reader is named; your case page tells you so.

## For case handlers

Open [Private cases](/cases) to see the cases written to you. Open a case to read it, add handler notes (only handlers see them) and mark it In review or Closed. Closed is final. If the person who wrote it asked for their data to be erased, the case is deleted when you close it.

## For administrators: naming case handlers

On Data privacy, open [Private case handlers](/admin/privacy/case-handlers). Choose a company, then add the people who receive its private cases or remove them; a removed handler loses access at once. You cannot name yourself: a group administrator, or another administrator of the company, designates you. Until a company has a handler, private cases are not offered there.
