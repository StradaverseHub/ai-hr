Use [AI Centre › Operations](/control-tower) to review operational readiness and exceptions. AI suggestions support a responsible reviewer; they are not authority to pay, terminate employment or change access.

## Coordinate outstanding work

The Overview shows readiness issues, the responsible party and the next action. **Check current records**
refreshes the queue from the HR engine without sending reminders. The scheduled review also refreshes
it and runs readiness and claims passes when their tenant switches are enabled. Readiness can use fixed
templates; scheduled model drafting additionally requires an admitted principal.

A verified closure means the missing field is now present or no longer required. HR still validates
the information itself. Ended employments are retired from the queue without being counted as fixes.
If a gap returns, its case reopens and the reopen count is shown. The recorded check time identifies
the observation behind the queue. Execution failures are shown separately from outstanding HR work.

Use the Operations tabs (Overview, Exceptions, Record readiness, Proposed actions and Opportunities) to
move between them; Administration holds the AI workers, setup and spending, and How it works explains each capability. Expand **Payroll and AI quality** on the Overview for the
existing quality and coverage metrics. The employee links open the authoritative personnel record.

## Work through an exception

1. Select the company and period where offered.
2. Open [Record readiness](/control-tower/readiness) or [Exceptions](/control-tower/exceptions).
3. Read the underlying facts and the affected record. Verify the source data before accepting an explanation or suggested action.
4. Use [Proposed actions](/control-tower/actions) for the actions available to your role and current workflow state.
5. Review the outcome in the authoritative HR/payroll record. A resolved suggestion is not proof that an external payment or submission occurred.

[How it works](/control-tower/how-it-works) describes the current model and, for administrators, what is switched on for your company. [Administration](/control-tower/agents) and [AI configuration](/admin/ai) expose the available configuration. Check provider readiness, permissions and the scope of any enabled behavior before changing it.

## Assistant

[Assistant](/assistant) answers with the data and tools allowed to the signed-in user. Use specific company, employee and period context. Check cited records and proposed actions. The model does not become the authoritative payroll calculator or gain another person's access because a prompt asks for it.

You can attach a supported spreadsheet or CSV to ask for import mapping help when that capability is available to your role. Review the proposed destination and column mapping, then open the relevant native import screen to validate its preview. An Assistant suggestion or file attachment does not save HR records: the native import screen retains its own permissions, validation and confirmation. Keep private workbooks out of public feedback and provide only the data needed for the selected task. If provider setup is unavailable, a Group HR administrator can review its configuration and evaluation status under AI configuration; a saved provider is not proof of a successful evaluation.

## AI quality

[AI quality](/assistant?view=insights#ai-quality), under AI Centre › Insights, is for HR and group administrators, for the companies they administer. It shows how people used AI Centre's work over the last 30 days, this quarter or this year:

- **Answers marked helpful**, out of the answers people marked.
- **Prepared actions confirmed unchanged**, with the share opened to edit, and **discarded**: cards prepared in chat and loop cards, out of those people decided.
- **Open gaps**: the kinds of question AI Centre could not answer or do whose status is still open.
- The confirmed-unchanged share by month, drawn from 0 to 100%.

**Biggest gaps** groups those questions by what was asked and counts them. Examples are redacted (names, ids, figures, dates and contact details are replaced by placeholders) and kept only as long as your company keeps chats. Mark each one planned, won't do or a duplicate of another; set it back to open at any time.

**Corrections to review** lists prepared actions opened to edit or discarded, recommendations not followed and answers marked not helpful, by role and date, never by name. Make one an **eval candidate** by writing the question with fictional details and what a good reply does: the text is redacted again when you save it. Read the saved text, approve it, then export it for a developer, who adds it to AI Centre's evaluation after review. Nothing leaves your company before you export it, and nothing changes AI Centre automatically.

**Proposals** lists what the loops propose from your own decisions, today the Salary payroll loop's net-swing threshold. A proposal applies only when an administrator approves it, from the next run, and **Revert** puts the earlier setting back as a new version. Each loop's corrections by month are shown beside it; promotion stays on the loop's own page.

A conversation about a sensitive topic stores no words and is never counted. Learning never changes calculations, permissions or what AI Centre may show.

Use [Reports](/reports) and [Analytics](/analytics) for authorized summaries. Confirm filters and totals before sharing an export.
