This guide is for **employees**. It walks you through finding your payslips in **My workspace**, opening a payslip to read exactly what you earned and what was deducted (EPF, SOCSO, EIS, PCB) before your money lands in the bank, and downloading both a single payslip and your annual **EA form** so you're ready when it's time to file your income tax with LHDN.

Everything here lives under your own self-service area — you don't need any special role to see your own pay. If you work for more than one company in the group, your payslips from all of them appear in one place.

## Finding your payslips

1. In the left sidebar, click **My workspace** (this opens the `/me` page).
2. At the top you'll see your name, employee code, position and department.
3. Scroll down past **Today**, **Leave**, **My positions** and **My details** to the **My payslips** section near the bottom. A small count next to the heading tells you how many payslips you have.

You'll see a table with one row per pay period:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Period** | The pay month, e.g. a month/year — this is a clickable link |
| **Company** | Which company paid you (useful if you hold more than one position) |
| **Gross** | Total earnings before deductions, shown as `RM …` |
| **Deductions** | Total taken off (statutory + any other), as `RM …` |
| **Net** | What actually reaches your bank, as `RM …` |
| **Status** | The payroll state for that run, e.g. `draft`, `approved`, `paid` |

**Note:** If the table shows *"no payslips"*, it simply means no pay run has been finalized for you yet. New payslips appear here automatically once payroll processes the period — there's nothing for you to request.

## Opening and reading a payslip

1. In the **My payslips** table, click the **Period** link for the month you want (this opens `/me/payslip/…`).
2. A clean, single-page payslip opens. At the top you'll see the pay period as the title, your name, the company, and a status pill (e.g. `paid`).
3. Use **← Back to my workspace** (top-left) to return to `/me` at any time.

### Earnings

The **Earnings** section lists what you were paid, each as a `RM` amount:

- **Basic salary**
- **Allowances**
- **Overtime**
- **Bonus**

These add up to **Gross pay**, shown in bold at the bottom of the section.

### Deductions

The **Deductions** section shows the statutory amounts taken off your pay. These are your *employee* contributions — the share that comes out of your earnings:

| Line | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **EPF** | Your KWSP (Employees Provident Fund) contribution toward retirement savings |
| **SOCSO** | Your PERKESO contribution for work-injury and invalidity protection |
| **EIS** | Your SIP contribution to the Employment Insurance System (job-loss support) |
| **PCB** | Monthly tax deduction (MTD) withheld and paid to LHDN on your behalf |

If anything extra was withheld, an **Other deductions** line appears too. The section ends with **Total deductions**, shown as a negative `−RM …`.

### Net pay

Below the deductions sits a highlighted **Net pay** box — the bold figure in the accent colour. **This is the amount that actually reaches your bank account.** In short:

> **Gross pay − Total deductions = Net pay**

### Employer contributions

The last section, **Employer contributions**, lists what your *company* pays on top of your salary — its **EPF**, **SOCSO** and **EIS** shares, plus the **Total employer cost**. These are shown in a muted colour because they are **not** deducted from you; they're informational, so you can see the full cost of employing you.

**Note:** If you joined or left part-way through the month, a small line at the bottom notes how many days were worked, since your pay was prorated accordingly.

## Downloading or printing a payslip

The payslip page is built to print and save cleanly — the navigation and buttons are automatically hidden from the printed copy.

1. On the open payslip, click **Print** (top-right corner).
2. Your browser's print dialog opens.
3. To save a PDF, choose **Save as PDF** (or *Microsoft Print to PDF*) as the destination instead of a physical printer, then **Save**.
4. To print on paper, pick your printer and click **Print**.

**Tip:** A saved PDF is handy for loan applications, visa paperwork, or rental references — anywhere you need to prove your income.

## Downloading your annual EA form

Your **EA form** (the *C.P.8A* statement of remuneration) summarizes a full year's pay and statutory contributions. You need it to file your personal income tax. AI HR generates it from your real pay records — you don't fill anything in.

1. Go to **My workspace** (`/me`) and scroll to the **Tax statement (EA)** section at the very bottom.
2. You'll see one row per company you work for, each labelled with the **year of assessment**.
3. Click **View / download** on the row for the company and year you need.
4. The EA statement opens. Click **Print** (top-right) and choose **Save as PDF** to keep a copy, or print it.

### What's on the EA form

The statement is laid out in the official EA sections:

- **Section A — Employee's particulars:** your name, income tax number, IC number, EPF number, SOCSO number, MTD/PCB category, number of children for tax relief, and your employment period.
- **Section B — Employment income:** gross salary, bonus, allowances/perquisites, and **Total gross remuneration**.
- **Section D — Total deductions:** your monthly tax deduction (**MTD/PCB**) for the year.
- **Section E — Contributions:** your **EPF/KWSP**, **SOCSO** and **EIS/SIP** totals.
- **Section F — Tax-exempt allowances.**

**Note:** Some lines (e.g. benefits-in-kind, living accommodation, CP38, zakat via salary, tax-exempt allowances) show *"not tracked"* — that means AI HR doesn't capture those items, not that they're zero. If any apply to you, gather those figures separately when filing.

**Note:** A blue information box at the bottom states the year of assessment and the statutory version used to calculate the figures. Keep this for your records.

## Common pitfalls

- **"I can't find a payslip for last month."** Payroll may not have finalized that period yet, or you may be looking at the wrong company row. Check the **Company** column in **My payslips**.
- **"My net pay looks lower than expected."** Open the payslip and read the **Deductions** section — EPF, SOCSO, EIS and PCB are statutory and vary with your earnings. PCB also depends on your tax category and number of dependent children.
- **"My PCB category or children count is wrong."** These come from your profile. On `/me`, open **Bank & tax → Request a change** to submit a correction. Changes here are **approval-gated** — your HR admin must approve them before they affect future payslips and your EA form.
- **"The EA form shows the wrong year."** The link defaults to the current year of assessment. If you need a prior year and don't see it offered, ask your HR admin.
- **"Print shows the website menus."** Use the **Print** button on the payslip/EA page itself rather than your browser's menu — the page hides navigation automatically when printed.

**Tip:** Always download your EA form **before** the tax-filing season rush, and keep each month's payslip PDF as you go — it makes reconciling your annual EA figures painless.
