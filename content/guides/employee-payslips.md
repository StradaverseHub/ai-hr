Open [My Workspace](/me) to find the payslips and annual tax statements available for your employment. Payroll records are personal; another employee's link does not grant access.

## Read a payslip

1. Choose the period and company. If there is more than one cycle, check which payment the document represents.
2. Review earnings, deductions, employee statutory amounts, net pay and any employer contributions shown separately.
3. Open or download the available payslip document. Compare the payment date and net amount with your bank record.
4. Report a discrepancy to payroll with the period, company and line concerned. Do not submit a new expense claim to correct a payslip.

A generated payslip does not prove that the bank has paid it. Payment processing is a separate employer workflow. A later correction may require payroll to reprocess or adjust a run; editing your profile does not rewrite an already processed payslip.

## Annual EA statement

Choose the correct assessment year and employment/company from your personal tax statement section. Review identifying details and annual amounts before using the statement for your own filing. Ask payroll about missing years, amendments or figures that differ from your records.

## Tax information

Use [Tax relief](/me/tax-relief) for the available TP1 self-service declaration. Review the year, categories, evidence and submission state. A submitted declaration is not a promise that every amount qualifies or will affect the next payslip; payroll must use the applicable data and review process.

Keep bank/tax changes within the profile change workflow. Send sensitive supporting documents only through the intended application process or the channel your HR team specifies.
