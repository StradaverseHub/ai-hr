Configure [Claims](/admin/claims) before employees submit expenses. Check the company, eligibility, claim types/items and required receipts.

## Claims setup and processing

1. Review the forms/items under [Claim forms](/admin/claims/forms).
2. Configure [Limits](/admin/claims/limits) and [Mileage](/admin/claims/mileage). Check effective rules and amounts before changing an existing policy.
3. Review claims through the authorized approval workflow. Inspect the expense date, amount or distance and receipt.
4. Use [Reimbursements](/admin/claims/reimbursements) for the available reimbursement process, or consolidate eligible approved claims into the intended draft payroll run.
5. Verify the resulting paid/linked state and payroll inputs before repeating a handoff.

A saved draft is not a submitted claim; an approved claim is not automatically a bank payment. Do not mark direct reimbursement and also pay the same expense through payroll.

## Employee loans

Use [Loans](/admin/loans) to create and review supported employee loan schedules. Confirm the employee, amount, installment terms and effective period. Review the outstanding balance and installments before consolidating deductions into payroll.

A payroll input should be traceable to the intended loan installment. After a payroll correction or reset, inspect the source and target states before trying to collect it again. Use the supported loan actions rather than editing unrelated salary fields.

## Troubleshooting

Rejected claim submission: inspect limits, receipt requirements and current employment. Missing consolidation: check approval state, company, payroll period and existing links. An unexpected loan deduction needs the loan reference, installment period and payroll run to reconcile it.
