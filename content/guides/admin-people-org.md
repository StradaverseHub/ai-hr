Use [Organization](/admin/organization) and [Personnel](/admin/personnel) to maintain the workforce. Select the correct company before making a change.

## Add people and organization data

1. Create the company structure and reference lists before importing people. Review employer statutory identity under the company's statutory page.
2. Configure [Hiring defaults](/admin/settings/hiring-defaults) and [Employee numbering](/admin/settings/employee-id) if needed.
3. Create the employee or open [Personnel import](/admin/personnel/import). Use the current template, preview the mapping and errors, and commit only after reviewing the selected company and records.
4. Open the employee's profile and review personal details, employment, assignment, compensation, bank details and statutory information. Complete the hire workflow; an unfinished hire draft is not an active employment.
5. Record reporting lines so the intended supervisor can see their team and receive the workflows that use that relationship.

## Changes over time

Use the relevant dated change action for salary and assignment changes. Check the effective date rather than replacing a historical value to make the current screen look right. Payroll resolves compensation for the selected period; a future salary change must not be treated as today's salary. Grade and compensation do not necessarily share the same effective-dating model.

Use the movement or termination workflow for employment changes. A resignation request, an offboarding checklist, an employment end date and account access are separate records; completing one is not proof that the others are complete.

## Malaysian tax and payroll details

Review tax circumstances, dependants and the employee's employment-specific statutory selections. Use the structured TP1, TP3 and zakat screens where appropriate. Prior-employer TP3 figures and imported payroll history are different inputs; do not enter the same earnings in both without reviewing the migration treatment. Use payroll's calculation detail to verify the resulting treatment.

## Give the employee access

Group administrators open [Users](/admin/users). Eligible employees without an existing login appear under **Pending activation**. An invitation or manual account setup creates the login binding; creating the employee alone does not create credentials. Multiple employments for one person do not require multiple logins. Rehiring does not automatically restore an archived account.

## Troubleshooting

- Missing employee: check company scope, employment status and whether the hire is still a draft.
- Missing approver: review the reporting relationship and the workflow's own approval configuration.
- Duplicate person: investigate using [Duplicate records](/admin/personnel/duplicates) before creating another login.
- Blocked deletion: use the supported archive or correction action; referenced history is retained.
