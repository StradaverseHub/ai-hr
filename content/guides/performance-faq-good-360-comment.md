Write about behaviour you have actually seen. If you rate a colleague highly, your overall remark (about 100 words) should back it with concrete evidence, because a high rating feeds their potential (future-growth) score, which is considered at calibration. The STAR method helps: the Situation where you saw it, what they were responsible for, what they specifically did, and the effect it had. Keep it about the work, never the person, and never include anything that identifies you -- 'Developing' with a concrete example is more useful than a vague 'Demonstrate'.
