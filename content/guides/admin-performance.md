Use [Performance](/performance) to manage the review cycles available to your role and company scope. Performance reviews and Rewards compensation reviews are separate workflows.

## Run a cycle

1. Create or open the cycle, review its dates and configuration, and confirm the included employees and reviewers.
2. Check goals and competencies or other review content configured for the cycle.
3. Open the cycle when ready for participation. Track self-reviews, manager reviews and any requested peer feedback.
4. Resolve missing assignments or incomplete work before advancing the cycle.
5. Complete calibration/release actions only where offered by the current workflow. Verify what employees can see after release.
6. Track acknowledgements and close/archive according to the cycle's supported state transitions.

## Keep responsibilities clear

A self-review is the employee's input. A manager assessment, calibrated outcome and acknowledgement represent different actions. Review the current stage before editing or submitting. Do not assume a released rating can be edited without a supported return/reopen action.

Use [Reports](/reports?cat=performance) for the available performance reporting and [Analytics](/analytics) for authorized workforce summaries. Confirm filters and access before distributing exports.

## Rewards handoff

If Rewards is enabled, set up its cycle, policy, membership and reviewed employee facts separately under [Rewards configuration](/admin/rewards). A performance rating does not automatically approve a salary increase, promotion or payroll payment. Assigned reviewers must complete the Rewards route, then an authorized payroll reviewer posts the approved result.

## Troubleshooting

Missing employee/reviewer: check company scope, employment, cycle inclusion and assignment. Missing self-review: verify the employee's linked account and cycle stage. Wrong outcome: use the appropriate review correction path and retain the reason instead of overwriting unrelated personnel data.
