Open [Loops](/control-tower/loops) in AI Centre, under Operations. A loop watches one part of HR work, prepares what a person needs to do, and checks on its next run whether it was done. There are five: Probation ending, Payroll inputs ready, Missing employee data, Missed clock-outs and Passports expiring. Each row says what "done" means for that loop, its level, whether it is switched on, when it last ran and how many cards it prepared in the last 30 days.

## Prepare, never act

Every loop works at the Prepare level. Each night it reads only what the administrator who switched it on may see, prepares a card for the right person (an HR administrator, payroll or the employee) and changes no record itself. The person sees the card on Today and finishes the work on the usual page, with that page's own checks and approvals. On its next run the loop looks again: when the work is done it closes the card, and if the same thing comes up again it reopens it. A loop prepares at most 200 new cards a night for each company and never calls an AI model.

No loop acts on its own. Pay, employment status, access, discipline and statutory filing always need a person's decision: a loop only prepares the work and points to the page where a person decides it.

## Switching a loop on or off

Every loop is off until a group administrator switches it on. Switching a loop on or off applies to every company in the group, and the group administrator who switches it on becomes its sponsor. If the sponsor loses administrator access, the loop stops. The company stop switch stops every loop at once.

## Pausing a loop for one company

Open a loop from the Loops list. Under Pause for a company, an administrator of that company can choose Pause or Resume. A paused loop neither runs nor shows its cards in that company; the other companies are not affected. To stop a loop everywhere, a group administrator switches it off instead.

## Asking AI Centre about loops

An administrator can ask AI Centre in chat which loops are on or paused, when each last ran and how many of its cards are open. The answer gives counts and states for the companies you administer, never who a card is for, and never changes a loop: switching and pausing are done on the Loops page.
