Use [Claims](/claims) to submit expenses when the Claims module is enabled and your account is linked to an active employment.

## Create and submit

1. Choose the claim type and item offered for your company.
2. Enter the expense date, description and amount. For mileage, enter the distance and review the configured calculation rather than inventing a rate.
3. Attach a receipt when required. The upload accepts PDF, JPEG and PNG content; renaming another file does not make it valid.
4. Use **Save draft** if you are not ready. Review saved drafts before submitting them individually or with **Submit Selected**.
5. Submit and check the resulting status. If validation fails, correct the stated field or receipt; do not assume the claim was filed.

## Drafts, limits and payment

A draft is not an approval request and does not establish entitlement to reimbursement. Limits and required evidence are checked at submission. A bulk submission can be refused as a whole if the selected drafts together exceed a limit.

Follow the saved claim to see approval or rejection. Use cancellation only when the current state allows it; draft deletion and cancelling a submitted claim are different actions. Approval is not proof of payment: reimbursement may follow the employer's direct-payment or payroll process.

## Troubleshooting

No claim types: ask HR to check module access and company configuration. Missing receipt: attach a supported file and resubmit the existing draft. Unexpected limit: ask HR which limit period, type and usage apply. Unpaid approved claim: provide the claim reference and ask which reimbursement route is being used.
