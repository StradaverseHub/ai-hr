A justification is your OWN case for the rating -- the evidence your line manager and the management team point to when they back it. Cover four things -- STAR: the SITUATION (the context, and what made it hard), the TASK (the target you were working to, or what you were responsible for), the ACTION (what YOU specifically did -- not the team, not what merely happened), and the RESULT (the outcome, with a number where you can). Lead with the number, then the driver: 'Beat the 12m GP target by 1.9m (16%) by renegotiating three supplier contracts during a raw-material spike' beats 'did well on GP'. Two or three concrete sentences is enough.
