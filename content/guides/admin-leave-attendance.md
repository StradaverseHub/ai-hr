This guide covers leave setup and its attendance/payroll dependencies. Work within the selected company and review the effective policy dates before generating balances.

## Configure leave

1. Open [Leave entitlements](/admin/leave-config/types). Review leave types, eligibility and entitlement tables.
2. Review [Company rules](/admin/leave-config/company-rules) and [Approval workflow](/admin/leave-config/approval-chains). Check who approves, which cases route to HR, and the applicable rules for each company.
3. Maintain [Holidays](/admin/holidays) and the employee's work schedule. Non-working days and holidays affect deductions; a date range is not necessarily the number of days deducted.
4. Use [Generate entitlements](/admin/leave-config/generate) and [Earned accrual](/admin/leave-config/earned-accrual) for the relevant cycle. Review previews and results before repeating an operation.
5. Use the separate history and brought-forward import screens under Leave Data Import. Imported usage and opening entitlement represent different facts.
6. Review [Employee balances](/leave/employee-balances) and [Balance health](/admin/leave-config/balance-health). Investigate unexpected figures through their ledger entries.

## Requests, corrections and leave credit

Employees apply from [My Leave](/leave). Review the deduction preview, duration and any unpaid portion before submission. Half-day or hourly choices depend on the selected type and rules. Approval, rejection, withdrawal and cancellation are separate states. Cancelling an approved request may require approval and can cover selected dates; use the action offered by that record.

Use [Adjustments](/admin/leave-config/adjustments) for an authorized balance correction with a reason. Do not invent a leave request to correct an opening balance. [Leave credit](/admin/leave-config/leave-credit) and employees' replacement-leave requests follow their own eligibility and approval rules; working extra hours is not automatically a credited balance.

## Attendance and payroll

Check schedules and attendance records when leave deductions look surprising. Approved unpaid leave can affect payroll, but a processed run is not evidence that a later leave change has been incorporated. Review and use the payroll correction/reprocess workflow for the affected run.

## Troubleshooting

A zero or masked balance can reflect eligibility, a shared pool, cycle setup or visibility. Inspect the explanatory text and ledger before regenerating or importing again. Check the selected employment when someone has more than one. Use the team tracker for availability and the employee inquiry for an authorized balance investigation.
