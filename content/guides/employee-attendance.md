Use [Clocking](/attendance/clock) to record work and [My records](/attendance/my-records) to inspect the result. Your company's clocking rules determine which location and device checks apply.

## Clock in and out

1. Open Clocking using your own signed-in account.
2. Allow location access when requested and make sure you are at an approved location if geofencing applies.
3. Review the current state, then clock in or clock out.
4. Check the recorded result before trying again. A disabled action or validation message needs attention; it is not confirmation that a punch was saved.

The mobile live experience can also offer clocking. A design preview demonstrates the interface and does not create real punches. Use the clearly identified live mode for actual attendance.

## Check your schedule and records

[My roster](/attendance/my-roster) shows your planned schedule. In My records, open the date to review punches, breaks and calculated hours. Worked hours and payable overtime are different: the schedule, day type, rounding, eligibility and approvals can affect the final result.

## Missed punch or correction

1. Open [Attendance requests](/attendance/requests).
2. Select the request type offered, the date and the correct times or details.
3. Explain the change and submit.
4. Follow it under [Request status](/attendance/request-status). Your request does not immediately replace the attendance record; approval is required where configured.

Use the withdrawal/cancellation action offered for a pending record instead of submitting a contradictory duplicate.

## Planned overtime and travel

Use [Plan overtime](/attendance/plan-ot) for an overtime application and follow its approval status. A plan is not a clock event or a guarantee of payroll payment. Use [Travel](/attendance/travel) for the available travel workflow. Check actual attendance after the work is done.

## Troubleshooting

If location access is denied, check browser/app permission and try at the approved location. If the record remains wrong, give HR the date, displayed time, location/device error and request reference. Do not use another person's account or a design-preview action to record work.
