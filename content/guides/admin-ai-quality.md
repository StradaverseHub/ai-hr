[AI quality](/ai-quality), under AI Centre › Insights, is for HR and group administrators, for the companies they administer. Choose the last 30 days, this quarter or this year, and one company or all of them.

## The figures

- **Answers marked helpful**: the answers people marked helpful, out of all they marked helpful or not helpful under a reply.
- **Prepared actions confirmed unchanged**: actions AI Centre prepared that people confirmed as prepared, out of all they decided. The edited share is shown with it.
- **Prepared actions discarded**: cards in chat that people discarded, out of the same decided actions.
- **Open gaps**: the kinds of question AI Centre could not answer or do whose status is still open.

Each figure is a percentage with its counts. The chart shows the confirmed-unchanged share by month, from 0 to 100%. A conversation about a sensitive topic stores no words and is never counted.

## Gaps and corrections

**Biggest gaps** groups the questions AI Centre could not answer or do by what was asked. Examples are redacted, so names, ids, figures, dates and contact details become placeholders, and an example is removed with the chat it came from. Mark a gap planned, won't do or a duplicate, or set it back to open.

**Corrections to review** lists prepared actions opened to edit or discarded, recommendations not followed and answers marked not helpful, by role and date, never by name. Make one an **eval candidate**: write the question with fictional details and what a good reply does. The text is redacted again when you save it. Approve the saved text, then export it for a developer, who reviews it before it joins AI Centre's evaluation. Nothing leaves your company before you export it.

## Proposals

**Proposals** lists what the loops propose from your own decisions, read-only here. Each is decided in [Payroll cycle settings](/control-tower/loops/payroll-cycle/settings): a proposal applies only when an administrator approves it there, from the next run, and an applied one is changed back there as a new version. Each loop's corrections by month are shown beside it; a loop's level changes only on its own page.

Learning never changes calculations, permissions or what AI Centre may show.
