Open [Performance](/performance) and select the cycle or review assigned to you. Your managerial role and review assignment determine what you can see and change.

## Complete a review

1. Check the cycle dates, employee and current stage.
2. Read the employee's self-review, goals and the feedback available to your role.
3. Enter ratings and comments against the configured review criteria. Use concrete work examples and explain important differences from the self-review.
4. Save work where offered and submit when complete.
5. Follow the cycle's calibration/release process. A submitted manager review may still require further review before the employee can see it.

An employee acknowledgement records receipt through the workflow; it is not a replacement for a discussion or a manager decision.

## Goals and feedback

Review goal progress through the employee's performance record. Peer feedback can have different visibility rules from a manager review. Use only the content the application provides for your role and do not promise anonymity beyond the stated workflow.

## Pay and promotion

Use [Rewards](/rewards) only when explicitly assigned to that review. It has separate recommendation, approval and payroll-posting stages. Finishing a performance review does not itself change salary or grade.

## Troubleshooting

If a review is missing, ask HR to check reviewer assignment, employment/company scope and cycle inclusion. If a submission is refused, reload the latest review state; another action may have advanced the cycle. Use the available return/correction action rather than creating a parallel review outside the system.
