Open [My Leave](/leave) to check eligibility, balances and apply. For colleagues' availability use [Team leave tracker](/leave/tracker); it does not replace your personal balance record.

## Apply

1. Confirm the employment/company shown.
2. Select the leave type and dates. Choose full-day, half-day or hourly duration when the type offers it.
3. Review the deduction preview, non-working days, balance and any shared-pool or eligibility explanation. A multi-day date range does not always deduct one day per calendar date.
4. Complete the required reason or supporting information. Review any proposed unpaid portion and consent before submitting.
5. Submit and check the saved request in [Leave history](/leave/history). Submission is not approval.

## Change or cancel

Open the request and use the actions available for its current state. Pending requests may offer edit or withdrawal. Approved leave uses the cancellation process and can require a further approval; selected-date cancellation is different from cancelling the entire request. Wait for the recorded outcome before assuming a balance has been restored.

Requests can have partial outcomes. Read the approved/rejected dates and remaining request rather than relying only on the original total.

## Leave credit and details

Use [Leave credit](/leave/credit) to request eligible replacement credit and follow its decision. Actual work, an overtime plan and a leave-credit award are different records; submitting one does not automatically create the others.

[Leave details](/leave/details) explains your balance movements. Pending usage, approved usage, accrual, brought-forward amounts and adjustments have different meanings. Masked or unavailable figures may reflect the leave type or shared pool rather than a fault.

## Troubleshooting

If dates or balances look wrong, send HR the leave type, employment, requested dates and the preview/result shown. If approval is stuck, check the request's status and ask HR to verify the configured approver. Do not submit duplicate requests while the first is still pending.
