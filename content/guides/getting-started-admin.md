Use this checklist when setting up a customer or adding a company. You need the relevant administrator permissions and company scope. Menu entries also depend on the modules enabled for your tenant.

## Set up in this order

1. Open [Organization](/admin/organization). Create or review the company, departments, positions, grades and cost centres. Complete the employer identifiers and addresses needed by your country pack.
2. Open [Personnel administration](/admin/personnel). Configure hiring defaults and employee numbering, then create employees or use [Personnel import](/admin/personnel/import). Review the import preview and errors before committing. An employee record is separate from a login.
3. Open [Users](/admin/users). Group administrators can activate eligible staff from **Pending activation**, or manage existing users and grants. Choose employee access unless the person needs an explicitly approved higher role. A company-scoped administrator does not automatically have group-wide account administration.
4. Configure [Leave](/admin/leave-config/types), company rules, approval chains, entitlement generation and opening data. Review [Balance health](/admin/leave-config/balance-health) after imports or policy changes.
5. Configure [Attendance settings](/admin/attendance/settings), schedules, shifts, assignments, work locations and card assignments. If importing punches, save a file mapping and test its preview first.
6. Open [Payroll setup](/payroll/setup). Review company payroll configuration, cycles, pay components, statutory configuration and employee compensation. Import historical payroll separately from prior-employer TP3 figures.
7. Configure the enabled Claims, Performance, Lifecycle and Rewards modules. Each has its own configuration and approval requirements.
8. Test a representative employee and manager journey: sign in, submit a request, review it with the intended approver, and check the resulting record. Review a draft payroll before processing or closing it.

## Check access and delivery

A role, a company grant and a module entitlement answer different questions. If a screen or employee is missing, check all three before adding broad access. [My Team](/manage/my-team) also depends on the reporting relationship and the viewer's scope.

[Workflow email](/admin/settings/notifications) needs tenant policy, recipient consent and an active installation relay. Hiring somebody or saving a policy does not prove that an email was delivered. Installation email configuration has a separate capability.

## Keep guidance current

Use the [Knowledge Base](/knowledge-base) for system guides and company-specific policies. HR administrators publish new articles and upload files. System guides are refreshed by releases; keep your own policies as separate articles so a system update does not replace them. If a guide describes a module you cannot see, ask the tenant administrator to check access and entitlement.
