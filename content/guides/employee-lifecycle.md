Use [My lifecycle](/lifecycle/me) for the checklist available to you and [Asset requests](/lifecycle/asset-requests) to follow equipment requests. These pages require the Lifecycle module and the relevant account access.

## Complete your checklist

Sign in with your own account, open the checklist, and follow the steps shown. If the checklist is missing, ask HR to check the case's contact/account match. Do not use another employee's link or account.

After equipment handover, the device acknowledgement becomes available only when its prerequisites are complete. Read the details and submit once. This is a signed-in acknowledgement; it is not a public acceptance link or a claim that an electronic-signature document has been generated.

## Request equipment

1. Open [New equipment request](/lifecycle/asset-requests/new).
2. Select the available catalogue item, quantity and required details.
3. Submit and keep the request reference.
4. Follow its approval and fulfillment state. Approval is not the same as delivery.
5. Use the offered cancellation action if your needs change and the request still permits it.

IT and People staff have separate assignments for approval and fulfillment. Your ordinary supervisor may not be the designated approver.

## Leaving or changing role

Follow HR's offboarding instructions and return equipment through the recorded process. A completed checklist does not itself end employment or change your login access. Ask HR/IT to verify those steps separately.
