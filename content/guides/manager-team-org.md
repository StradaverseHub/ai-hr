Use [My Team](/manage/my-team), [Team leave tracker](/leave/tracker) and [Org chart](/org) for different questions: who you manage, who is away, and how the organization is connected.

## Review your team

1. Open My Team and check the active company/scope and filters.
2. Find the employee and inspect the information your permission allows.
3. Use the tracker for the relevant dates when planning cover.
4. Use the org chart to understand reporting structure; appearing on the chart does not give permission to edit someone or see their pay.

## Reporting lines

The system can distinguish administrative, functional, project and dotted relationships. They are not interchangeable approval grants. Ask HR to maintain the intended relationship and its effective dates, especially after a transfer or manager change.

An employee with multiple employments can have a different company or reporting context for each. Review the employment associated with the request before deciding who should act.

## Corrections and absence cover

Use [Delegations](/delegations) for temporary workflow cover rather than permanently changing a reporting line to solve a short absence. For a real transfer or reorganization, HR should update the authoritative employment/assignment records.

## Troubleshooting

A missing employee may be outside your current scope, no longer report to you, or have a relationship that does not confer the expected access. Send HR the employee, company, expected reporting relationship and relevant dates. Do not create duplicate employees or broaden your own role to repair the view.
