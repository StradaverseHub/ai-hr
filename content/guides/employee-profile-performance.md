This guide is for employees using **My workspace** to keep their own details current and to take part in performance reviews. You'll learn how to update your contact information yourself in seconds, how to request changes to pay-affecting details (bank account and PCB/tax category) that need an approval first, and how to complete your self-review and acknowledge your manager's review once it's released.

Everything here lives in one place: in the left sidebar, click **My workspace** (the `/me` page). Scroll down to the **My details** card and, lower down, the **My reviews** table.

## Updating your own profile

Your profile is split into two halves by risk. Low-risk **contact** details you can edit directly. **Bank & tax** details affect your pay, so they go through an approval request instead. You'll find both side by side inside the **My details** card.

### Contact details you can edit directly

On the left of the **My details** card, under the note **Contact updates apply immediately**, you can edit:

- **Phone**
- **Address**
- **Date of birth**
- **Emergency contact** (name)
- **Emergency phone**

To update them:

1. In the left sidebar, click **My workspace**.
2. Scroll to the **My details** card.
3. Type your new values into any of the fields above. You can change one or several at once.
4. Click **Save details**.

That's it — the change is saved straight away. There's no approval step and nothing to wait for.

**Note:** This form only ever touches your contact fields. Your name and email are part of your identity record and can't be changed here — ask HR if those need correcting.

**Tip:** Keep your **Emergency contact** and **Emergency phone** filled in. It's the first thing HR reaches for in an actual emergency, and it's easy to leave blank when you join.

### Bank & tax details (these need approval)

On the right of the **My details** card you'll see your **Bank & tax** summary, marked **Changes need approval**. It shows:

| Field | What you'll see |
| --- | --- |
| **Bank** | Your bank name, e.g. `Maybank` |
| **Account** | Masked for safety, e.g. `•••• 1234` |
| **PCB category** | Your tax category — `Single`, `Married, spouse not working`, or `Married, spouse working` |
| **Dependent children** | The number of children claimed for tax relief |

These are read-only here because they feed your salary and your PCB/MTD tax withholding — an admin has to approve any change before it takes effect. To request one:

1. Under the **Bank & tax** summary, click **Request a change** to expand the form.
2. Fill in only the fields you want to change:
   - **Bank name** and **Account no.** — for a new payroll account.
   - **PCB category** — leave it on **No change**, or pick `Single`, `Married, spouse not working`, or `Married, spouse working`.
   - **Dependent children** — the number of qualifying children.
3. Leave any field blank to keep its current value (the form reminds you: **Leave blank to keep current**).
4. Click **Submit for approval**.

Your request then appears in the small list under the form with a status pill: `pending`, `approved`, or `rejected`. While it's `pending`, your live details are unchanged. Once an admin approves it, the new values are written to your statutory profile and the summary updates.

**Common pitfalls**

- **Changing a bank account is all-or-nothing.** If you fill in a new **Account no.**, you must also fill in the **Bank name** (and vice versa). Submitting one without the other is rejected with a message asking for both — this stops a new account number being saved against the wrong bank.
- **Empty request.** If you submit without entering anything, you'll get a "Nothing to change" error. Enter at least one value.
- **Approver isn't your manager.** Bank and tax changes are an **admin** (HR admin or super admin) decision, not a manager's, so don't be surprised if your direct manager can't action it. There's nothing extra for you to do — just wait for the `pending` pill to turn `approved`.

**Tip:** Sort out your bank and PCB category *before* the next payroll run. A change only affects your pay once it's approved, so a request left `pending` over the cut-off won't be reflected in that month's payslip.

## Your performance reviews

When you're part of a review cycle, a **My reviews** table appears further down **My workspace**. Each row shows the **Cycle**, a plain-English **Status**, the **Overall** rating (once released), and a link on the right telling you what to do.

The status text is written from your point of view:

| Status you'll see | What it means | Your action link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Self-review | It's your turn to fill in your self-review | **Start self-review** |
| With your manager | You've submitted; your manager is writing theirs | *No action needed* |
| Awaiting release | Manager finished, not yet shared with you | *No action needed* |
| Ready to acknowledge | The review is released — read and acknowledge it | **Acknowledge** |
| Acknowledged | All done | **View** |

Click the action link to open the review page (`/performance/review/...`). A **Back to My workspace** link sits at the top so you can return easily.

### Completing your self-review

1. In **My reviews**, click **Start self-review** on the relevant cycle.
2. On the review page, find the **Self-review** card.
3. Fill it in:
   - **Your rating** — required. Pick a value from the rating scale; each option shows its number and label (for example `4 · Exceeds expectations`).
   - **Comments** — optional. A few lines on your wins, challenges, and how the period went.
   - **Goals referenced** — optional. Note the goals or objectives you're reflecting against.
4. Click **Submit self-review**.

After you submit, the status moves to **With your manager** and your self-review becomes read-only — you'll see your rating and comments displayed back to you. The **Manager review** card below will say it's still pending until your manager finishes.

**Note:** You won't see your manager's rating, comments, or any pay recommendation while the review is with them or merely awaiting release. The manager section stays hidden from you until the review is formally **released**.

### Acknowledging your manager's review

Once your manager finalizes and the review is **released**, the **My reviews** status changes to **Ready to acknowledge**.

1. In **My reviews**, click **Acknowledge** (or open the review and scroll to the bottom).
2. Read the **Manager review** card — you'll now see the **Manager rating**, the **Overall rating**, and any **Comments**. The **Overall** number also shows in your **My reviews** table.
3. In the **Acknowledge** card at the bottom, click **Acknowledge** to confirm you've seen and discussed it.

Acknowledging records that you've read the review — it doesn't mean you have to agree with every point; that's what the conversation with your manager is for. After acknowledging, the status becomes **Acknowledged** and the row's link changes to **View**, so you can reopen the review any time for reference.

**Tip:** If a row shows **Awaiting release** or **With your manager** with *No action needed*, there's genuinely nothing for you to do yet — check back after your manager wraps up. You'll know the review is ready when an **Acknowledge** link appears.

## Troubleshooting

- **My workspace says my account isn't linked.** If you see a message that your account isn't linked to an employee profile, your login hasn't been connected to an employee record yet. The profile and review features only appear once HR links your account — contact HR.
- **I don't see a My reviews table.** It only shows when you have at least one review in a cycle. No cycle, no table.
- **My bank/tax change still shows the old values.** Check the request list under **Request a change**. If the pill still reads `pending`, it's waiting on an admin; if it reads `rejected`, submit a new request (and double-check you included both bank name and account number together).
- **The Acknowledge button isn't there.** The review hasn't been released yet. Wait for the **My reviews** status to read **Ready to acknowledge**.
