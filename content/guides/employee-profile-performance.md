Use [My Workspace](/me) for personal information and [My performance](/me/performance) for goals, reviews and requested feedback.

## Update personal information

1. Open your personal details and review the editable fields.
2. Save ordinary contact changes through the form provided.
3. For fields that require review, use **Request a change**, provide the requested information and submit for approval.
4. Check the request outcome before assuming the authoritative record has changed. Bank and tax changes can have payroll consequences and may need HR review.

Do not create a second account to change employment details. Ask HR to correct the existing person/employment record or login link.

## Goals and performance reviews

Open the assigned cycle/review from My performance. Read the dates and review stage, complete the self-review and goals requested, save work where available, and submit only when ready. Submission and acknowledgement have different meanings; acknowledging a released review does not change its rating.

Keep goal progress current through the goal actions offered. If asked for peer feedback, follow the invitation's scope and submit or decline through the provided action. Confidentiality depends on the workflow; do not assume every note is private to you.

## Resignation and handover

Use the resignation action in your workspace when available. Review the dates and reason before submitting. A request remains subject to the employer's decision; it is not proof that employment has ended. Use withdrawal only when the current state permits it.

If an onboarding or offboarding checklist is assigned, open [My lifecycle](/lifecycle/me). Complete the tasks and acknowledgement offered there. Handing back equipment and an employment termination are separate steps.

## Help

For a missing review, ask HR to check cycle inclusion and your account link. For an incorrect employment, salary or job assignment, ask the authorized administrator to make the appropriate correction. Use [Feedback](/feedback) for product issues without copying passwords or unnecessary personal data into the report.
