Open [Notifications](/me/notifications) to read in-app messages and manage the delivery preferences available to you. Use [Notices](/notices) for published company notices.

## Follow a notification

Open the linked request or record, review its current status and act only if the workflow assigns you responsibility. A notification describes an event; the underlying record may have advanced since it was sent. Marking a message read does not approve its request.

## Optional workflow email

Where supported, choose your employment, categories and preferred email language, then save your consent. Enabling email requires the supported verified sign-in identity. HR also needs to enable that category and the installation needs an active relay and worker.

Opting in applies to eligible future events; it does not replay all historical notifications. Turning off email does not remove in-app history or change your approval responsibilities. Invitations and password recovery are separate identity messages, not ordinary workflow notification preferences.

If email is missing, first check the in-app record. Ask HR to verify category policy and delivery status, and check the destination/spam folder. A sent status means the relay accepted the message, not that you read it.

## Company notices and help

Read the notice's audience, dates and content before following instructions. Only the publishing administrators can change official notices and knowledge-base articles. Use [Feedback](/feedback) to report a product problem with clear steps and the relevant page/request reference; never include passwords or activation links.
