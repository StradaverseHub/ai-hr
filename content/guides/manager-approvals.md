Open [Approvals](/approvals) for requests assigned to you and [Approval history](/approvals/history) for previous decisions. The inbox can contain different workflows; read the request type before acting.

## Review and decide

1. Open the item and check employee, company, dates, requested change and attachments or explanations.
2. For leave, review deduction details, available balance, coverage and any partial-date outcome. A cancellation request concerns previously approved leave and is a separate decision.
3. For attendance or overtime, compare the request with the roster and available attendance evidence. Approval of a plan does not record the employee's clock times.
4. For claims, review expense date, type, amount/mileage and receipt against the configured policy.
5. For profile changes, review the exact fields requested. Use the authorized HR route when the item is outside your responsibility.
6. Choose an offered decision, enter the required reason and verify the resulting status.

A multi-step workflow may remain pending after your approval because another approver still needs to act. Do not describe your decision as final unless the record shows the completed state.

## Delegation and separation of duties

Use [Delegations](/delegations) for temporary cover. The system checks current authority at submission, so an old browser tab does not preserve access after a grant or delegation expires. Self-approval and other separation rules vary by workflow and must not be bypassed by changing the request owner.

Lifecycle and Rewards have their own assignments and stage rules. Follow the linked module record when an inbox item takes you there.

## Troubleshooting

If an item disappeared, check history and its current state; someone else may have acted or the requester may have withdrawn it. Refresh after a stale-data error. For a routing issue, give HR the request reference and expected approver so they can inspect the relationship and approval configuration.
