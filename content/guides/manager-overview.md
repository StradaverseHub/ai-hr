Start with [My Team](/manage/my-team) and [Approvals](/approvals). A manager role does not grant unrestricted company HR or payroll access. Visibility depends on live reporting relationships, company scope, delegated responsibility and each workflow's rules.

## Daily workflow

1. Check [Approvals](/approvals) for work assigned to you. Open the request and its supporting context before deciding.
2. Review [Team leave tracker](/leave/tracker) for availability and overlapping absence.
3. Open My Team to investigate the employees you are authorized to manage.
4. Review team performance tasks through [Performance](/performance).
5. Check [Notifications](/me/notifications) for changes and follow the linked record.

## Coverage while away

Use [Delegations](/delegations) for the available cover arrangement. Review delegate, dates and scope. A delegation is time-bounded workflow authority, not a permanent role or access to all of your employees' data. Confirm pending requests are assigned as expected.

## Other modules

Claims can arrive in the approval inbox. Lifecycle uses its own assigned module staff and approval levels; being a line manager alone does not make you an IT approver. Rewards requires an explicit assignment to the review and stage. Open those modules only when your responsibilities require it.

## When something is missing

Ask HR to check the administrative reporting line, its dates, company scope and the workflow's approval chain. Do not request group-administrator access merely to recover one missing request. If the app refuses a stale decision, refresh the record and review its current status before trying again.
