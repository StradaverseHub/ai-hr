This guide is for **people managers** — anyone with the `manager` role (and it applies to `hr_admin` and `super_admin` too, who see everything a manager sees and more). It walks you through what changes once you can manage a team: the new **Manage** section in the sidebar, how the **Dashboard** and **Org chart** give you a live read on your people, and a simple daily/weekly rhythm so nothing waiting on you slips through.

## What the Manage section unlocks

Once your account has the manager role, a new group appears in the left sidebar under the heading **Manage**, sitting between the everyday pages (Dashboard, My workspace, People, Org chart, Leave, Attendance) and the admin-only tools. It contains two items:

| Item | Where it goes | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Approvals** | `/approvals` | One inbox for every request awaiting your decision |
| **Performance** | `/performance` | Reviews awaiting you, plus review cycles (admins) |

**Note:** If you don't see the **Manage** heading, your account hasn't been granted the manager role yet. Ask an HR admin to set it under **Admin → Users & roles**. The two pages are gated — opening them without the role redirects you back to the Dashboard.

**Tip:** The sidebar can be collapsed to an icon-only rail using the **Collapse** control at the bottom. Hover any icon to see its label. Your choice is remembered between visits.

### Approvals — your decision inbox

In the left sidebar, click **Approvals**. This page gathers everything that needs a yes/no from you into one place. The header tells you how many items are waiting (for example, *"3 items awaiting a decision"*), or *"Nothing waiting on you"* when you're clear. It's organised into cards:

1. **Leave requests** — each row shows the **Employee**, **Type** of leave, the **Dates** (start → end), and the number of **Days**. In the **Action** column, click **Approve** or **Reject**.
2. **Attendance corrections** — when someone asks to fix a clock-in/out, you'll see the **Employee**, the **Date**, the **Proposed** times, and their **Reason**. Again, **Approve** or **Reject** in the **Action** column.
3. **Profile changes (bank / tax)** — this card only appears for HR admins. It lists proposed changes to sensitive details like bank or tax info.

You only ever see requests for people within your scope — the list is trimmed automatically to what you're allowed to act on.

**Note:** You can't approve your own requests. If a row is your own, the action buttons are replaced by the label *"your request"* — someone else has to decide it. This self-approval ban is by design.

**Tip:** An empty card simply reads *"No pending leave."* or *"No pending corrections."* — that's the all-clear, not an error.

### Performance — reviews and cycles

In the left sidebar, click **Performance**. The subtitle reads *"Review cycles, appraisals, and merit recommendations."* What you see depends on your role:

- **As a manager,** the key section is **Reviews awaiting you** — an amber-highlighted card listing each direct report's review, tagged with the cycle name and a count badge. Click **Review →** on a row to open that person's appraisal and complete it.
- **As an HR admin,** you additionally see the **Review cycles** table (Cycle, Period, Status, Progress, Pending pay) and a **+ New review cycle** form to launch a cycle group-wide or for one company, optionally with a merit pool.

The **Progress** column uses the legend *ack / done / total* — acknowledged, submitted, and total reviews — so admins can see at a glance how far a cycle has run.

**Tip:** If the **Reviews awaiting you** card isn't showing, there's nothing assigned to you right now — it only appears when you have pending reviews.

## Seeing your team at a glance

Two pages give you situational awareness before you even open the inbox.

### The Dashboard

The **Dashboard** (the first sidebar item, or click the **AI HR** logo) is your morning overview. Across the top sit four stat cards — each is clickable and jumps straight to the relevant page:

- **Headcount** → People
- **Pending leave** (*Awaiting approval*) → Leave
- **Companies** → Organization
- **Latest payroll** → Payroll

Below that, the **Planned absences** timeline is the manager's most useful view: a 14-day strip showing who's off and when, so you can spot coverage gaps before approving more leave. Use the prev/next arrows or the date picker to move the window.

Further down you'll find:

- **Onboarding** — who recently joined (last 60 days).
- **Upcoming events** — public holidays ahead, with how many days away.
- **Attendance** — today's split of **Present / Late / Half day / Absent** as counts and a colour bar.
- **Recent activity** — a live feed from the event log of what's been happening across your scope.

**Tip:** The **Pending leave** card is the fastest sanity check each morning — if it's non-zero, head to **Approvals**.

### The Org chart

In the left sidebar, click **Org chart** to see your reporting structure as a tree. The header shows the headcount in view. Each connector colour encodes a different reporting relationship: **direct** (administrative), functional, project, and **dotted-line**. A person has one direct manager (the tree) but may have several cross-cutting lines.

You can **search** for a person, **zoom in/out**, reset the zoom, and **expand or collapse** a manager's reports to focus on one branch. It's the quickest way to confirm who reports to whom before a leave decision or a review.

## Your daily / weekly workflow

A simple rhythm that keeps you on top of things:

**Each morning (2 minutes)**

1. Open the **Dashboard**. Glance at **Pending leave** and the **Planned absences** strip.
2. If anything's waiting, click **Approvals** and clear the **Leave requests** and **Attendance corrections** cards — **Approve** or **Reject** each, checking the **Planned absences** view first for coverage clashes.

**During a review cycle**

3. Check **Performance** → **Reviews awaiting you**. Open each via **Review →** and complete it before the cycle closes.

**Weekly**

4. Open the **Org chart** to confirm your team structure is current — flag any gaps to an HR admin.
5. Scan **Recent activity** on the Dashboard for anything unexpected in your scope.

## Common pitfalls

- **"I can't see Approvals or Performance."** You don't have the manager role yet — ask an HR admin (Admin → Users & roles).
- **"My own leave has no buttons."** Correct — you can't approve your own requests; the row shows *"your request"* instead. Someone else must decide.
- **"The Approvals inbox looks empty."** Empty cards mean nothing is pending — that's good, not a bug.
- **"I don't see the Profile changes card."** That card is HR-admin only; managers approve leave and attendance corrections.
- **"A request I expected isn't there."** You only see items within your scope. If it's genuinely missing, confirm the person reports to you on the **Org chart**, or check the company switcher in the top bar.
