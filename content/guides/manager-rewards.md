Open [Rewards](/rewards) when you are explicitly assigned to a review, or follow the item from [Approvals](/approvals). A line-manager title alone does not give access to a compensation review. The guidance below applies where the relevant controls are available in your installation; ask an authorized administrator if a required control is unavailable.

## Find your review

The review list shows your role, the current stage and responsible reviewers where permitted, due dates, salary and bonus budget percentages, and blocker and exception counts. These figures summarize the review; they do not expose confidential employee rows or grant decision authority.

Use the available year, company, business-unit and stage filters, or **Waiting for me**, to narrow the list. Clear filters if an expected review is missing. Access also depends on effective assignment dates and current People and login eligibility; ask an authorized administrator to inspect Rewards access if your assignment is suspended. Open the review and recheck its current state before acting.

## Review the assigned stage

1. Confirm the cycle, company, business unit, review kind and current stage.
2. Inspect the employee rows, eligibility basis and aggregate budgets available to your role. Check that the selected configuration and saved version are the intended ones. Restricted rows and your own record may be excluded.
3. Review recommendations, justification, source facts and any warnings. For promotions, compare the current and proposed role summaries and complete required acknowledgement.
4. Choose the available submit/approve/return/reject action. Include a reason or exception acceptance note when requested.
5. Check the new status and next responsible stage.

Submitted inputs are frozen. Use return to draft for corrections instead of expecting to edit a submitted snapshot. If the page reports a stale revision, refresh and re-review the current data before deciding.

## Read the eligibility and budget basis

Shared configuration holds group rules, while Group Rewards maintains each review's PAT, target bonus months, multiplier override, branch ratings and budget-cap exemption separately. A later shared configuration version does not automatically update an existing review.

Check the saved eligibility dates, salary-increase exclusion window, bonus minimum rating and notice/ER rules shown for the review. A legacy version may identify snapshot-based status or an unspecified minimum; do not assume a rule that is not recorded. Employees whose basic salary changed in the exclusion window are excluded from the salary increase and its pool base, but can still receive bonus on the adjusted basic salary under the separate bonus rules. A lateral transfer without a basic-salary change is not excluded for that reason.

Read the bonus-pool status as well as its amount:

- **Not set** means required valid inputs are missing or unsuitable, rather than an approved zero budget.
- **Provisional (9+3)** uses the forecast basis. A review cannot progress to CTRO or EC approval while provisional.
- **Final (Actual)** uses the actual basis. When it changes, check the previous pool, new pool and difference, and recheck utilisation before deciding. Submission cannot exceed the final pool.

A saved legacy basis stays in place until Group Rewards explicitly adopts the unit inputs. An exemption needs independent confirmation and does not bypass the final-pool submission check. Only Group Rewards can see the underlying PAT figures; other authorized approvers see the resulting pool and multiplier. If those figures need correction, return the review to the responsible team rather than trying to edit protected inputs.

## Review Senior plan cases

Senior plan membership is handpicked from JG 21–23 and independently confirmed by a second Group Rewards user. Unselected employees in those grades remain on the standard matrices. Selected members' salary base and awards are outside the standard increase and bonus pools, and they do not receive the lump-sum token.

Group Rewards records the offline-approved salary and bonus, reference, date and supporting evidence, then obtains a different Group Rewards user's confirmation. Missing or unconfirmed decisions block payroll transfer. Later changes need a reason and fresh confirmation.

Only Group Rewards, CTRO and EC can see Senior amounts and separate totals. Other authorized reviewers see **Senior plan — handled separately**. Do not infer amounts from a hidden record or request an export outside your permitted audience. A confirmed offline decision is still separate from the final payroll posting.

## Understand the outcome

Annual reviews use BU, BG, GR, CTRO and EC stages. Ad-hoc and mid-year reviews have their own routes and draw on a posted annual review's available funding. Submission can reserve funds; approval and posting are separate transitions.

Only the authorized payroll reviewer posts the completed review into the payroll/compensation workflow. Your approval is not a payslip or a bank payment. Contact HR if the employee source data changed or the proposed effective date no longer fits the available posting action.
