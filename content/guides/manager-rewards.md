Open [Rewards](/rewards) when you are explicitly assigned to a review, or follow the item from [Approvals](/approvals). A line-manager title alone does not give access to a compensation review.

## Find your review

The review list shows your role, the current stage and responsible reviewers where permitted, due dates, salary and bonus budget percentages, and blocker and exception counts. These figures summarize the review; they do not expose confidential employee rows or grant decision authority.

Use the available year, company, business-unit and stage filters, or **Waiting for me**, to narrow the list. Clear filters if an expected review is missing. Access also depends on effective assignment dates and current People and login eligibility; ask an authorized administrator to inspect Rewards access if your assignment is suspended. Open the review and recheck its current state before acting.

## Review the assigned stage

1. Confirm the cycle, company, business unit, review kind and current stage.
2. Inspect the employee rows and aggregate budgets available to your role. Restricted rows and your own record may be excluded.
3. Review recommendations, justification, source facts and any warnings. For promotions, compare the current and proposed role summaries and complete required acknowledgement.
4. Choose the available submit/approve/return/reject action. Include a reason or exception acceptance note when requested.
5. Check the new status and next responsible stage.

Submitted inputs are frozen. Use return to draft for corrections instead of expecting to edit a submitted snapshot. If the page reports a stale revision, refresh and re-review the current data before deciding.

## Understand the outcome

Annual reviews use BU, BG, GR, CTRO and EC stages. Ad-hoc and mid-year reviews have their own routes and draw on a posted annual review's available funding. Submission can reserve funds; approval and posting are separate transitions.

Only the authorized payroll reviewer posts the completed review into the payroll/compensation workflow. Your approval is not a payslip or a bank payment. Contact HR if the employee source data changed or the proposed effective date no longer fits the available posting action.
