Use [Statutory forms](/payroll/statutory-forms), [Payroll reports](/payroll/reports) and [Payment submission](/payroll/payment-submission) after reviewing the source payroll. Access is limited by company scope and enabled modules.

## Before generating an output

1. Select the correct company, period or assessment year and the relevant run where required.
2. Check employer name, address and registration numbers under Organization. Check employee identity and statutory details.
3. Review processed payroll and any historical imports, amendments or signed statutory adjustments included in the output.
4. Read missing-data and eligibility warnings. An empty field does not establish that the legally correct value is zero.

## Choose the correct form

The Statutory Forms area provides the implemented Malaysian outputs, including EA, PCB II, CP39, employer contribution forms and employment-event forms such as CP22, CP22A and CP21. Select the form from its current screen and use the fields and validation offered there. A form being available does not establish that it applies to every employee or event.

For EA, select the company and assessment year, review each employee's annual figures and any manual overrides, then preview/download. Keep amendments traceable. For monthly contribution or tax outputs, reconcile the employee population and totals to the intended payroll period. Use the form's own preview before exporting.

## Submit and retain evidence

Download the format required by the receiving bank or agency. Follow that recipient's current submission process outside the application. Record receipts or acknowledgement details where the application provides them, including PCB II receipt records. Do not mark a submission complete merely because a PDF or upload file was generated.

## Business reports

[Reports](/reports) groups master, attendance, leave, payroll, claim and performance reporting. Choose filters and the offered export format, then verify company scope, dates, row count and totals. A report-builder view is a saved report definition, not a new source of payroll data.

## Troubleshooting

Missing rows: verify company, date range, employee selection and the source run's state. Wrong annual values: inspect payroll history and overrides before adjusting the form. Missing bank data: correct the employee's payment details and regenerate the intended output. Escalate uncertain statutory treatment to the responsible payroll reviewer with the source period and calculation detail.
