THE SITUATION TODAY

Apply OT Plan already works out the duration while you enter the two times, but it shows decimal hours. The current form also offers a reason category alongside the required written reason. We checked the current code; we have not yet reproduced this screen on production.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- Rename the readout to Overtime Hours and show hours and minutes: 2h30m, 2h or 45m. Until both times are entered it will show —. The helper will say: Worked out from Start Time and End Time.

- Support overnight plans. An end time earlier than the start means the following day, so 22:00 to 01:30 shows 3h30m. Equal start and end times will remain invalid rather than silently becoming a full day.

- Use the same hours-and-minutes display in This month, the monthly-limit comparison, the Self Overtime list and the superior's approval view. The monthly comparison will keep its existing figures and limit, with no extra h after a value that already includes its unit.

- Remove Reason category and its explanatory hint. Reason remains mandatory. Previously saved categories will stay on record but will no longer appear in overtime lists, approval views or any overtime report or export that currently includes them.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

An overtime duration you can read directly, including overnight work, and one clear place to explain the request. Everything else on the form stays as it is today.

GOOD TO KNOW

- Existing saved plans remain available. We will keep their hours, approval decisions and recorded categories intact.

- This does not change the monthly limit, which plans count toward it, how approvals work, pay calculations, attachments or who may see a plan. Supporting overnight plans changes the planned-time check only; it does not change clock-correction rules.
