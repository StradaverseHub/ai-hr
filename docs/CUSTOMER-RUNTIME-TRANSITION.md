The runtime/configuration maintenance grant is separate from an app-only deployment or failed-install recovery approval. Main/live remains disabled.

## Failure and rollback boundaries

Before activation, preserve the failed attempt and only quarantine verified owned unactivated resources. Never forge successful-arrival cleanup authority. Restore old service/configuration/build only against their actual preserved identities and after checking database/audit state; do not manufacture a new successful deployment result.

After migrations or application writes, automatic database rollback is forbidden. Keep applied OneHR privacy policy/functions and all new records. Do not turn audit protection off if history has begun. A fallback build/source must be checked for compatibility with the retained schema/privacy rules; otherwise keep the bounded maintenance state and use reviewed forward recovery. Do not promise exact old environment rollback if it would disarm new chain history. Failure recovery requires fresh observations, not assumptions based on an earlier healthy build.
