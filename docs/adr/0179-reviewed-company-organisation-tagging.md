# Reviewed dated Company organisation tags

Company is the source of independent Personnel Business Unit, direct parent Company and root Business group. A null parent is a valid standalone root. Business Unit is selected by UUID through its canonical Criteria availability; its creation company and label do not imply ownership. No mappings or employee changes are created by migration installation.

Before first explicit publication, stored legacy assignment Business Units remain authoritative. The first mapping activates pending classification for any unmapped company and therefore requires HR authority over the complete tenant company footprint. Later proposals include affected descendants and source/destination authority, with durable role proof against both current and proposed trees. Native ancestry always reads published mappings on the transaction's Malaysia business day, never an arbitrary history request date or an unpublished lease. Existing company-tree consumers use the same current parent projection. No expanded permission subtree is stored in the session.

An HR doer prepares a frozen dated preview. A different login and Person, acting as scoped HR or eligible Group Rewards for every affected company and both old/new Business Units, approves its exact digest. Group Rewards review exposes only that run and its frozen report, not the HR company editor. Both actors, scopes, source epoch, identity binding and every employment/assignment/classification source digest are rechecked. A conservative tenant source epoch also catches newly added population. Changes require a fresh preview.

Processing stages bounded checkpoints without changing visible employee data. The original HR doer publishes in one transaction: canonical locks, exact source recheck, private transaction lease, invisible candidate mappings, canonical assignment projection, classification/audit reconciliation, then visible publication. Failure rolls the whole publication back. A retry observes the existing published run. Assignment projection preserves exclusive interval bounds and existing history; inactive re-tag records receive classifications without invented active intervals. Reports retain changed, unchanged, skipped and failed outcomes and frozen before/after labels, IDs and effective dates.

Ordinary native writers derive tags inside their own existing transaction. Managed-mode assignment guards cover raw inserts, updates and reversals without changing immutable superseded before-images. Direct parent edits require the reviewed owner after first publication. Rewards/payroll must report pending classification explicitly rather than silently omit employees.

Verification is hosted only: service database fixtures cover scope/tenant/date/independence, stale and staged retries, inactive history, atomic rollback and ancestry; component fixtures capture desktop/mobile in six locales. A passing source fixture is not a live operation or reporter acceptance. No staging or production re-tag is performed by deployment.

Account admission is separately owned by the canonical encrypted-credential bridge. The engine migration installs a fail-closed private placeholder; the following bridge migration must replace it and the server must refresh cryptographically bound proofs before use. Native roles alone never establish that a suspended or expired login may approve or publish.

Already-entered future hire intervals appear in the frozen report with their proven projection date. Projection starts at that existing interval, before the next independently published mapping boundary. No interval is invented for a future or inactive employment.
