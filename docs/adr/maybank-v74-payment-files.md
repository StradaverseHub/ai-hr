# Maybank2E-RC V7.4 payment files

Status: candidate implementation; the reporter’s hashing-arrangement question is posted on feedback 566a19dc. Not installed or bank-accepted.

## Owners and admission

CountryPack.bankPaymentFiles remains the jurisdiction seam. The existing bank export owner supplies ready saved payroll runs, immutable generated bytes, source digest checks, request identity, tenant/company authorization and generation audit. This extension selects a separate Maybank writer; OCBC layouts and bytes remain separate. A downloaded file records generation only. It neither settles a payment nor recalculates salary, contributions or tax.

Salary uses the company’s primary MYR account. Statutory categories use the existing company_statutory_authority bankProfileId and payment reference for EPF, SOCSO, LHDN or Zakat. Every Maybank preview and generation rechecks the exact company/category/account. No account is borrowed from another company. The existing OCBC account-selection flow is preserved.

A Maybank profile must explicitly select V7.4. Hash mode defaults to unconfirmed and refuses generation. The private bank document describes domestic hashing as conditional and statutory hashing as optional, but instructs readers to obtain the formula from the bank; no formula appears in the supplied attachment. The only implemented alternative is an administrator’s explicit record that the bank does not require hashing. A hash-required arrangement remains unsupported pending the bank-issued algorithm. No guessed hash or silent zero is emitted.

Bank payee residency is a separate nullable fact on the existing personnel banking record, maintained by its canonical scoped HR bank writer and audit. It applies to the actual payee, including a custom holder. It is not inferred from tax residency or EPF citizenship. Missing residency refuses salary generation.

## Format decisions and limits

The private reporter attachment’s revision history identifies V7.4, dated 6 August 2026; its cover retains an older V4.5 footer. Relevant tables, rather than that footer, govern this writer. Domestic files preserve 29 header, 337 detail and 29 footer fields. Statutory files preserve 42 header, 136 detail and 28 footer fields, with distinct record types and amount locations. Records use pipes and CRLF with printable ASCII. Unsupported text, delimiters, quotes, line breaks, overlength fields and unsafe amounts refuse the whole file; no salary or contribution is truncated or rounded. The upload ceiling is 10 MB.

Salary supports IT book transfer and IG interbank payment under the existing IBG arrangement; RENTAS requires additional bank fields and is refused. Receiving identities use bank-published BIC data, with documented Islamic account distinctions where the shared legacy bank picker collapses variants. Sources verified 8 November 2026: [RHB bank BIC appendix](https://www.rhbgroup.com/myreflex/premium/articles/article/bulk_csv_file/index.html), [JANM bank code/account distinctions](https://terengganu.anm.gov.my/images/JANM/AO/Terengganu/07-Aktiviti/2025/Kod_Bank.pdf), [Maybank SWIFT identity](https://www.maybank2u.com.my/maybank2u/malaysia/en/personal/faq/accounts_and_banking/foreign_telegraphic_transfer.page). These verify receiving identities, not Maybank acceptance of this file.

Statutory payments retain saved EPF employee/employer/wage amounts; their required whole-RM constraint causes refusal when recorded cents are incompatible. LHDN retains its full grouped tax reference and separate PCB/CP38 amounts. SOCSO preserves its separate V7.4 Lindung 24 Jam field and includes that saved employee contribution in reconciliation. Zakat uses the documented state/body and classification combinations, including Sarawak’s distinct classes. Registration, ASNB, EIS and bill/PRN tax payments are outside this five-category request.

The header’s generic employer-reference length conflicts with its Zakat-specific remark. The specific 20-character Zakat remark is used; other categories keep their category lengths. No bank-imposed payment filename convention is specified for these standalone uploads (the document’s filename rules describe optional supporting documents). Generated names retain the existing immutable date/sequence identity. Native field/count and fictional-data reconciliation checks establish implementation behavior; only a real authorized Maybank validation can establish acceptance. The restricted PDF and private derived specimens stay outside this public repository and Actions artifacts.
