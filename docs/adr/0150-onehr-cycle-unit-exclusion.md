# ADR-0150 — Units and departments not taking part in a cycle (the cycle exclusion rule)

> Decision record at its recorded date; implementation/status observations are historical. Verify current source and later amendments. [Branch/release policy](../REPOSITORY-AND-ENVIRONMENTS.md) governs operations.

- **Date:** 2026-10-28
- **Status:** Owner decisions recorded (D-P1-22, D0–D16, D5 as amended by APR-022). Built on a migration branch (`0667_onehr_unit_exclusion.sql`, service functions only); not released. The administration screen (X5) waits for its design decision (D14).
- **Amends:** ADR-0141 (skip-level section: gap → built), ADR-0103 (panel archive by rule).
- **Related:** ADR-0080 (pack ownership), ADR-0103, ADR-0141, ADR-0142, WP-B2 leader seats (0663), WP-C department view (0664).

## Context

The source system lets an administrator leave a whole unit (a company here) or some of its departments out of one review cycle. That rule is the only trigger for its "one level up" competency and Career Plan review. AI-HR had per-person participation overrides only, so it matched the source for one person and lacked the unit rule (ADR-0141, skip-level section).

## Decision

1. **One owner of the fact.** `performance_assessment_unit_exclusion` holds (cycle, company, optional department). There is no business-group key (D1). A department rule covers that exact department, never its sub-departments (D2). Membership is derived from the employment's assignment in force today (Malaysia), its company falling back to the employment's company (`app.assessment_unit_placement`). Nothing is copied into the participation snapshot or into `performance_review.reviewer_employment_id`.
2. **Derived on every read (D3):** participation, leader rights, the one-level-up reviewer, calibration grants. **Written once at save:** live 360 rows archived, untouched KPI lines moved one level up. Bring back ends the rule and restores only the derived part.
3. **Subject precedence:** terminated, then the unit rule, then the per-person override, then the opening snapshot (`app.assessment_review_participates`; also `app.assessment_review_eligible` for the department view). A review with a frozen result is never hidden or changed (D7). A per-person "add" is refused while the unit is out; "remove" and "clear" stay allowed and persist across Bring back.
4. **Leader-off reads the unit rule only** (a person removed individually still leads), **per acting employment** (D9): `app.onehr_manager` (the stored reviewer), `app.onehr_moderator` (each approval step), seat holders in `app.onehr_calibration_seat_users`, and the HOD in `app.onehr_department_view`. A login's explicit calibration grant is off only when every live employment of it in the cycle's scope is out (`app.onehr_user_unit_excluded`). Administrators are never stopped. Leader-off applies to frozen reviews too (O4).
5. **One level up, as amended (D5, APR-022):** only competency review and Career Plan review move, live. `app.onehr_effective_reviewer` is the stored reviewer while it takes part, else the walk above it; it replaces `onehr_manager` only in the competency and plan predicates (competency sections, competency and plan-fit AI sources, plan reviewer and remark writer, career profile access and validation, skill history, review header, the review assistant's competency and career operations). The 360 inside the competency review moves with it (O2): `app.onehr_assessment_inputs` admits the effective reviewer with the same anonymous aggregates the stored reviewer sees, without the PM band, score or submitted KPIs. `onehr_manager` is not widened: band confirmation, the manager panel and the PM remark rule stay with the stored reviewer and go to nobody while it is out. The completing classic KPI lock stores the band only while the stored reviewer is out (O1); the journey lock already did.
6. **One walk for preview and runtime (D6):** `app.onehr_unit_step_up` climbs the live administrative line at most six managers, stopping at a missing line, a terminated manager or the subject's own person. The source's "administrator intermediate" stop has no employment-level meaning here (grants are not employment attributes) and is not implemented (O3). The walk starts from the stored reviewer, a snapshot taken when the cycle opened, and climbs live lines: a known hybrid (plan R7).
7. **Written once, through the existing single writers.** The save archives every live 360 row where someone in the new scope is the rated person or the rater, through the keeping panel writer's body (time, reason naming the rule, per-row audit; the 0540 trigger revokes a released anonymous report). There is no status filter: submitted ratings are archived too and leave scoring. Frozen or closed reviews are skipped and counted (D7). Untouched KPI lines move through the moderator writer's body: a line qualifies when it is not HR-filled, not locked and no approval step has acted (setting approvals do not count) and its subject still takes part; each approver who is out is replaced by the walk above that approver; the chain is de-duplicated in order and written whole with `moderatorSource='matrix'`; no notice is sent (D10). A line whose approver has nobody above does not move and its subject is listed. A save that would touch the saver's own assessment is refused.
8. **Preview equals save.** One plan function (`unitExclusionPlanTx`: the `app.assessment_unit_exclusion_facts` projection, computed with the proposed scope before any write, and the pure `planUnitExclusion`) serves both. The save locks the cycle, requires the previewed digest and the cycle revision token (`performance_onehr_cycle.revision`, bumped by every save and Bring back), and refuses either when stale. An identical active rule makes the save a no-op. A whole-company save ends that company's department rules as `subsumed`. A scope with nobody in it today is refused.
9. **Authority and history.** Tenant-wide HR administrators write (D4); company-scoped administrators and performance partners read the rules and previews of their companies. Writes happen only in open or calibration cycles (D11). Rules are ended, never deleted (D12): no DELETE policy, DELETE and TRUNCATE revoked. Events: `review-cycle.unit-exclusion-set` (rule, scope, counts, digest) and `review-cycle.unit-exclusion-ended` (rule, why: `brought_back` or `subsumed`).
10. **Closed cycles (D8, O5):** the close transaction writes `performance_assessment_unit_exclusion_pin` (the employments the active rules covered on the close day) through `app.assessment_unit_exclusion_pin`. A closed cycle reads only its pin, so history does not drift with later moves.
11. **Consumers.** Plan, career and evidence writes need a participating subject (O7, which also closes the per-person gap). The panel builder and import drop raters whose unit is out (E3). Tasks, the workspace, the home team card, the manager command reader and reminder routing follow the effective reviewer; reminders drop moderators who are out. Admin readiness counts and the rating-record "missing" count follow participation. The participants list reports `unit_out`. The cached PM insight source gains the participation arm (U11).
12. **Definer reads under FORCE row security.** Both new tables force row security. Each read policy keeps a definer arm (`current_user<>'ai_hr_app'`, the 0547/0559 convention) so the predicates work when the owner is not a superuser; the application role reads only what its own arm admits.

## Consequences

- With no active rule the fast path is one probe of the partial unique index, and `onehr_effective_reviewer` equals `onehr_manager` (pinned in tests). Per-row cost with rules active needs measuring on staging (plan R2).
- Known, business-exact behaviour: one-time effects are not re-run for movers (source O5); lines already acted on stay with an approver who is out and stall (E6); Bring back restores no archived row and moves no line back; the builder never restores an archived pairing.
- The kernel `/performance` queue (`managerPendingReviews`) still lists a OneHR review under an excluded stored reviewer; the pack's task projection filters it. The kernel cannot read the pack rule (ADR-0080).
- Open for the screen (X5, D14): labels for `unit_out` in six locales, the preview layout, the two-step confirmation, whether the review screen names the one-level-up reviewer (O6), and the review screen's competency and plan forms for the `reviewer` role.
- The held two-step release (WP-E) will need the same leader-off and effective-reviewer arms where it gates on `onehr_manager`, `onehr_moderator` or calibration grants.
