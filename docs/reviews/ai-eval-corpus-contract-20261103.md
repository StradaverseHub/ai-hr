# Evaluation corpus and outcome contract

## Rubric contract

This records the existing source-owned fictional corpus and its observable scoring limits. It is not a new human
approval of every answer, a price/capability certification, a model-quality result or deployment authority.

The ceiling profile freezes three repeats of20 baseline,38 complex,16 parity,16 explainer and90 sensitive turns:
540planned outcomes. The review profile freezes those180 plus58 reviewer-authored turns across five personas:
238planned outcomes. These checked-in questions are now known regression corpora; they are not an undisclosed
future holdout. No budget-driven prefix, implicit repeat reduction or omitted sensitive slice qualifies as complete.

Existing baseline ideals, reviewed alternatives and sensitive expectations remain unchanged. Observable behavior
uses the existing case-specific required reads/actions and hard-defect rules, not the model's own label alone.
Negative safety checks require no forbidden private reads or effects. Sensitive rules separately check native routing,
crisis/workplace-harm fixed replies and their zero-model-call conditions. Actual native HR facts are checked for
unintended mutation within the explicitly listed snapshot domains; AI custody/accounting/proposals are separate.

Tool presence, an answer label or a substring does not establish factual faithfulness, correct clarification or complete
explanation. Positive factual criteria remain human-review-required even when observable behavior is met. Unseen
required/forbidden terms and expected modes are useful observations, not independent factual adjudication. Thai
and Tamil corpus rows still require native-speaker review. No aggregate quality percentage has been newly invented.

Every case/repeat has exactly one terminal outcome. A failed continuation predecessor makes the dependent case
unavailable; it does not become an equivalent fresh conversation. Errors, aborted, unrun and budget-refused cases
remain visible and prevent complete measurement PASS. Complete execution, safety, factual quality, human review
and budget verification are separate results. Ordinary no-model CI proves only that the carrier is inert.

The original native spend resolver, opaque proof validation, finite policy and receipt owner remain authoritative.
An additional evaluation lifetime allocation irreversibly reserves each full reviewed request bound before dispatch;
unknown/failed charges never become zero or replenish it. Prices/metadata alone cannot mint a reviewed capability.
The currently available weekly key limit is not a verified USD15 evaluation allocation. Real calls remain held until
approved financial configuration is available. Budget fit must be established before choosing a billable run; an
incomplete bounded run is diagnostic evidence, not qualified model quality.

Source plans bind raw corpus, fixture, scoring and this review document. `test/eval-plans/refresh.py` only performs
static source maintenance after review; it executes no app/model/compiler/test and grants no approval. Hashes and
cardinality guards detect drift; they do not replace independent rubric review or native permission evidence.
