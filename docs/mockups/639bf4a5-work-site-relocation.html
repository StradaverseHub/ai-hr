A place your company operates from is master data, not an attendance setting. It is also the only screen where a work site can be created or edited at all — which is why we kept it when you asked us to remove it, and why it belongs somewhere findable. Moving it is right.

Nothing is migrated, because nothing needs to be. Every work site already belongs to a company and is already only visible to administrators of that company. The move changes where the menu item sits, not what the data is or who can see it.

⚠️ The reason you gave is out of date

Your request says:

“A location can set a state that overrides the public-holiday calendar used by Leave and Attendance.”
That stopped being true two weeks ago, and you approved the change. We removed the work site’s hold on public holidays entirely: the state control is gone from the screen, the holiday-calendar field was dropped from the database, and the sentence that described this behaviour was deleted from all six languages — because once the behaviour went, the sentence was a lie.
Public holidays now come from the employee’s own work state, which is what you approved. We verified at the time that not one employee’s holidays moved.

So the move is still right, for a different reason: a work site is where people work, it carries the branch mapping, and the clocking points hang off it. Those are organisational facts. It is just no longer anything to do with holidays.

One thing to decide, and one to watch

Do the clocking points move with it? Clocking points are grouped under their work site on that same screen today. If the site moves to Organization and the clocking points stay under Attendance, you would be maintaining one thing in two places. Our recommendation: the site moves, and the clocking points move with it, with a link from Attendance so the old route still finds them.

The menu entry belongs to the Attendance module today, so moving it is a small piece of plumbing rather than a rename — it changes which part of the system owns the screen. Nothing you can see changes beyond where you click.
