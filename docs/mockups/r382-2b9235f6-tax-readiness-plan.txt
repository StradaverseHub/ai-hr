THE SITUATION TODAY

The REP calculation is available, but the current reprocessing message does not explain whether an employee's approval information is missing, revoked or outside the selected payroll period. One employee's incomplete tax information also stops the other selected employees. We have confirmed these weaknesses in the processing workflow; we have not yet verified Employee C's actual approval information.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- Recognise the employee's configured tax category separately from whether the supporting approval information is ready for the selected employer and payroll period.
- Show the category, applicable employer and period, and a clear readiness result for each selected employee. Reuse an existing applicable approval declaration instead of asking HR to enter it again.
- Where information is genuinely incomplete, identify the reason and provide a direct route to the relevant tax configuration. Explain that recording the approval reference, effective period and HR confirmation records an existing approval; AIHR does not grant the approval.
- Allow employees with complete information to reprocess while reporting an incomplete tax configuration against the affected employee only. Distinguish this from the separate Skipped – Nothing payable result in the related request.
- Keep an unsuccessful employee in the run, with their existing payslip, transactions and payroll history unchanged. Do not release transactions or replace their tax category with an ordinary resident category.
- Record each selected employee's outcome and reason in the processing results and audit trail. A successful REP calculation will identify REP consistently on the calculation screen, preview and downloaded document.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

HR can reprocess a group and see which employees succeeded, which need tax information, and which had nothing payable. An applicable recorded REP approval permits REP calculation without repeat entry. A missing approval is explained clearly without preventing other employees from processing.

GOOD TO KNOW

- HASiL's REP calculation applies to approved individuals. Selecting REP alone does not establish that approval. The approval reference and HR confirmation are AIHR's way of recording the evidence; they are not a separate approval issued by AIHR.
- A declaration must apply to the employment, employer and payroll period being processed. If an employment has moved to another employer, the configuration route retains the selected run's employer context and the same employee-record permissions: HR must administer both the payroll employer and the current employer.
- This change does not invent approvals, change previously saved tax calculations, or silently correct older payroll records. Employee C can succeed when the required applicable information is present; if it is absent, the result will identify what must be completed.
