THE SITUATION TODAY

The Atelier theme already opens each module's sub-menu as a flyout to the right of the menu, as you asked last time. But we built it as a second column that sits beside the menu, so the page is pushed to the right and loses that width while the sub-menu is open. Tables, forms and configuration pages get noticeably narrower.

Atelier also has no way to collapse the menu. Classic has a Collapse button that shrinks the menu to icons and gives the page the space; Atelier always shows the full-width menu.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- The sub-menu will float over the page, the way Classic's does, instead of sitting beside the menu. The page keeps its full width and does not move or reflow when the sub-menu opens or closes.

- The floating sub-menu keeps Atelier's look: the same headings, links, icons, counts and order as today. It closes when you choose a link, click anywhere outside it, press Esc, or press its close button. Clicking another module switches the sub-menu in one click.

- A Collapse menu button is added at the bottom of the Atelier menu, with a tooltip. Collapsed, the menu shows icons only, with each module's name on hover, and the page widens to use the space. An Expand button brings the full menu back.

- With the menu collapsed, clicking a module's icon opens the same floating sub-menu, so every page stays reachable.

- Your choice is remembered on that browser. It is the same remembered setting Classic uses, so collapsing in one theme keeps the menu collapsed if you switch to the other.

- Tablets get the same floating sub-menu. Phones keep today's Menu button at the top of the screen, which already gives the page the full width.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

More working space on every screen: the sub-menu no longer squeezes the page, and the menu can be folded away entirely when you want the widest view, with Atelier's styling kept throughout.

GOOD TO KNOW

- This changes only how the Atelier menu is laid out. No menu item, link, permission, page or workflow changes, and the Classic and Monolith themes stay exactly as they are.

- The sub-menu covers the left edge of the page while it is open. That is what floating over the page means; it disappears as soon as you pick a link or click away.
