THE SITUATION TODAY

The misleading EPF Bonus 2012 message has already been fixed. An EPF Bonus 2012 message now appears only when EPF Bonus 2012 is actually ticked.

For Benefit in Kind (BIK) and Value of Living Accommodation (VOLA) items with Subject to PCB ticked, Remuneration Type is fixed at Normal. The monthly tax deduction (PCB) calculation can only treat these benefits as normal monthly remuneration. That is why BKBOP cannot be saved as Additional.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- On BIK and VOLA pay items, Remuneration Type can be chosen as Normal or Additional. Your choice is saved and stays the same when you reopen the item. Nothing is changed automatically when you change Payroll Category.

- When Additional is chosen, PCB treats that benefit exactly as it already treats other additional remuneration, such as a bonus. It uses the existing additional-remuneration formula, with no new formula. Because a benefit has no EPF, no EPF is deducted for it.

- When Normal is chosen, nothing changes from today.

- The same choice is followed everywhere tax is worked out: normal payroll, additional payroll runs, reprocessing, the Pay Item import, payroll history import and income-tax recalculation.

- The PCB breakdown on the payslip detail shows the benefit under Additional or Normal, matching your setting.

- Payroll months already processed or closed keep the method they were calculated with. Closed payroll, payslips and statutory records are never changed. A change to the setting applies the next time an open month is processed or reprocessed.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

BKBOP saves as Additional, and its PCB is calculated as additional remuneration for the month it is paid. BIK items you keep as Normal behave exactly as before.

GOOD TO KNOW

- We're not changing PCB tables, rates or the order of the calculation. EPF, SOCSO and EIS are not affected, because benefits in kind are not part of those wages.

- The LHDN PCB calculation guide we work from describes BIK and VOLA as normal remuneration. Choosing Additional is therefore your company's decision for each item. Normal stays the default for new items.

- Before release we will check one employee's month worked three ways: with BKBOP as Normal, as Additional, and by hand from the LHDN additional-remuneration formula. All three must match to the sen. If you have a worked example of your own, attach it to this request and we will check against it too.

- EA form reporting of BIK and VOLA (sections B3 and B4) does not change.

- This is a larger change because the tax calculation, its history and its checks all need to agree. We will release it as one complete piece, not in parts.
