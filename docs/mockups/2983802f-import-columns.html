Multi-company already works too. Fill in the Company column per row and each employee is matched to that company by legal name, label or registration number — and their department, position and cost centre are checked against that company. The preview shows which company every row resolved to before anything is written. One file, many companies, no need to import company by company.

The new work is the shape of the import, not the fields in it.

Attendance, Leave and Career Movement are deliberately not in this plan. They are transaction records with their own rules about periods, approvals and what may be changed after the fact — not employee information. They need their own design, and folding them in here is the fastest way to get all of it wrong.
