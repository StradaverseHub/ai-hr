Figures are illustrative. What is not illustrative is which columns are shaded: those four are the ones nothing in your system can fill today.

Why the shaded columns are empty, and why we will not print a zero in them

What the form asks forWhere it would come from
MTD amount, per monthYour payroll. Already recorded on each payslip.
CP38 amount, per monthYour payroll. Recorded separately from MTD, with the LHDN instruction reference it came from.
Receipt / bank slip / transaction number Nowhere. That is the reference LHDN gives you when you PAY. The only acknowledgement field in the system sits on the monthly CP39 return, and all six of your CP39 returns are still drafts with no reference and no filing date recorded.
Receipt / transaction date Nowhere, for the same reason.

On a statutory form an empty box says “we are not telling you this”, and a 0.00 says “the money does not exist”. Those are different statements to LHDN, so we will print the first and never the second.

The one thing we need from you

Tell us where the receipt numbers and payment dates should come from. Either you key them in once per month when you pay LHDN — we would add those two fields to the monthly PCB return you already have — or the form is issued with those columns blank for you to complete by hand. We are not going to guess a receipt number onto a tax form.
