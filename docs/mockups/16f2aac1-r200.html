These were deliberately shortened when the form was first built because the full wording ran past its column and printed over the next one. 9Mostly there Kaedah Pembayaran with its four tick boxes, the No. Cek / Kiriman Wang field and the employer signature and telephone boxes are all already on the page. We will add "(Sila tandakan (x))" to the heading, put the four options in your order (Tunai, Kiriman Wang, Cek, Lain-lain), and split the signature box into three: Tandatangan, Nama, and No. Tel & Cop Majikan. All stay blank — they are yours to fill in, never taken from payroll.

3Two things we want to be straight with you about

The attachment. The reference we built this form against last time was not a PERKESO document — it was a completed Borang 8A printed by another payroll system, with its filled-in values highlighted. That is why the form carries the footer saying it is our layout and not PERKESO's own page. Your new attachment may well be the genuine blank PERKESO form; we have not opened it yet. The first thing we will do is establish what it is. If it is an agency-published blank form we will say so on the shelf and re-word that footer; if it is another system's output again, the nine corrections still get made exactly as listed, and the footer stays. We will not claim it is PERKESO's own page unless we can show it is.
Nothing about the numbers changes. This is a layout and wording job only. The contribution month stays the payroll month itself with no shift forward (the EPF Borang A is the one that shifts by a month, and that difference is deliberately protected by a check that fails if the two ever agree). The payment deadline stays the last day of the contribution month. The per-sheet total, the grand total, the automatic page splitting and the figures themselves are untouched.

The review screen you use before downloading (company, year, month, then the schedule with a Status box per person) keeps its shape. Its column headings will be re-worded to match the printed form so the screen and the document never disagree, and the plain-text version of the form is updated in the same pass.
