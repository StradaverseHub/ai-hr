Two captions, two different facts. The form’s item 8 asks for the number of children who qualify. The screen’s “Qualifying children (PCB)” is not a number of children — it is what those children are worth, expressed in whole-child units. Nothing on the screen says which of them the form prints.

Item 8 is the only box on the whole form that states a number of children. If it printed 9, the statement the employee signs and files would say they have nine children.
