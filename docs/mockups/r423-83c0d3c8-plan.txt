VERDICT: Partly there already   ·   SIZE: Large

WHAT YOU ASKED FOR

You asked for clocking locations to be set up in one simple flow: create the location first (name, address, map point, geofencing radius, Active or Inactive), then tick which company or companies may use it. On the employee's Attendance tab, one switch, "Use Geofencing for Clocking", decides the rest. Off: no location is needed and the location field is hidden. On: a location must be chosen, and only Active locations open to the employee's company are offered. When the switch is on, clocking checks the phone's position against that location's radius, accepts inside, refuses outside, and keeps a record of the result.

THE SITUATION TODAY

1. Attendance > Locations is a list of companies and their work locations. Clocking locations are not listed there. They sit inside each work location's own screen, plus a separate screen for clocking locations not attached to any work location. That is why the setup feels company-first.

2. A clocking location has no company at all. Since September every clocking location is shared by every company, so the employee's location picker offers every location in the group, whatever company the employee belongs to.

3. Only a group administrator can create or change a clocking location. Company administrators can view them. This stays.

4. The employee's Attendance tab already has a Location assignment list (one or more locations), a separate "Enforce geofencing" tick box, and a "Radius override" box.

5. Something you should know: that tick box does not control the employee's own Clock in / Clock out screen (the one with the photo). That screen always checks the phone's position against the employee's assigned locations, ticked or not. An employee with no assigned location cannot clock in there at all today.

6. Already working and kept: the map and address search on the location form, a radius on each location, and the record of every check. Each accepted clocking saves where the phone was, which location matched, the distance and the allowed radius. Each refused attempt is written to the audit trail.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

Attendance > Locations

1. Becomes the list of clocking locations, with columns Location Name, Address, State, GPS Coordinates, Geofencing Radius, Companies, Status and Actions (View, Edit, Deactivate, or Activate for an inactive one). It has a search box (name or address), a Company filter, a Status filter (All, Active, Inactive) and a New Clocking Location button for group administrators.

2. A company administrator sees, read-only, only the locations open to companies they administer, and only those company names.

3. The company-based work location list moves to its own menu row under Attendance, called "Work Locations", otherwise unchanged. It still records where an employee works, which shows in their employment history. Clocking locations are no longer shown inside it.

New and Edit Clocking Location

4. One page, in the order you listed: Location Name; Find an address; Address; State (optional, from the list of Malaysian states); Latitude and Longitude; the map with the radius circle; Geofencing Radius in metres; Companies (tick one or more, at least one required); Status (Active or Inactive). Report label and Remarks stay at the bottom as optional, because the Attendance report already prints the report label.

5. A company cannot be unticked while employees of that company are assigned to the location. The message says how many, so they can be moved first.

6. Deactivate asks for confirmation and says how many employees are assigned. An inactive location cannot be chosen for anyone and does not accept clocking.

Employee > Attendance > Location Assignment

7. One switch: "Use Geofencing for Clocking". It replaces the "Enforce geofencing" tick box.

8. Off: the location field is hidden and nothing needs choosing. Saving with the switch off clears the employee's assigned locations; the change history keeps the old list.

9. On: the Clocking Location field appears, and Save is refused until at least one Active location is chosen. The picker offers only Active locations open to the employee's company. More than one may be chosen, because some staff work at two sites, but one is enough.

10. The "Radius override" box is removed. Each location's own radius is the only radius.

11. If a location assigned earlier is no longer usable (inactive, or not open to the employee's company, for example after a transfer), its row says so. It does not count towards the one required location and does not accept clocking.

Clocking

12. Switch on: the phone's position is compared with the assigned location or locations. Inside the radius, the clocking is accepted and saved with the location, distance and radius. Outside, or with no usable location, it is refused with the distance shown, and the refused attempt goes into the audit trail. This is what happens today.

13. Switch off: the employee can clock in and out on the Clock screen without being at any location. The photo and the registered phone are still required, as today. The phone's position is still saved with the clocking, marked "geofencing not required", so HR can see where it was made. It is simply not compared with a location.

14. Clock times HR types in by hand on the Employee Attendance daily list are not checked against a location, because a typed time has no phone position. Today that entry is refused for any employee whose geofencing box is ticked.

WHAT HAPPENS TO WHAT IS ALREADY SAVED

1. Every existing clocking location keeps its name, address, coordinates, radius, report label and status. Its companies are set to the companies whose employees are assigned to it today, plus the company of the work location it is attached to, if any. A location nobody is assigned to is given every company, so it stays selectable exactly as today; narrow it with Edit.

2. Every employee with at least one assigned location gets the switch On, whatever the old tick box said. The Clock screen already checks them against those locations today, so nothing changes for them when they clock in.

3. Employees with no assigned location get the switch Off. Today they cannot clock in on the Clock screen at all. After the change they can, with their position recorded but not checked. If any of them should be geofenced, turn the switch on and choose a location.

4. An employee whose old box was ticked but who has no location stays On and is still refused at clock-in, as today. Their tab says "Choose a location: this employee cannot clock in until one is assigned".

5. An employee with a radius override is judged by the location's own radius from now on.

6. Past clockings and their records are not touched.

7. I have not counted how many employees fall under points 2 to 5. They are counted on the live system just before the change, and the numbers go in the round's notes.

WHAT IS NOT DONE

1. No new clocking method. No company setting chooses between clocking methods today. The Clock screen with photo and registered phone remains the way employees clock.

2. Creating and editing clocking locations stays with group administrators, because one location can serve companies another administrator does not manage. Company administrators still assign locations to their own employees.

3. State on a clocking location is for display and filtering only. It does not change public holidays.

4. The Work Locations list itself is not redesigned.

5. The map stays the free map in use today, not Google Maps.
