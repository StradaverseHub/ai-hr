3. The grey, and one thing we want to check with you

The grey goes. White cards on a light page, with lines rather than filled blocks separating things — the same direction we took the payslip this week.

We are not removing the expand behaviour everywhere. Your Group structure panel is also a collapsed section, and it collapses because a company group can be long. We are changing Statutory Payment Setup, which has a fixed handful of authorities and should just show them. If you want the group panel opened out too, say so and we will do both.

No company data changes, and no existing code, employer number or bank routing is touched.
