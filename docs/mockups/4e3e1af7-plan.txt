VERDICT: Partly there already   ·   SIZE: Moderate

THE SITUATION TODAY

The unit amount box treats a blank as "use the suggested amount", while RM 0 withholds the token for everyone in the BU. The two look almost the same, so clearing the box or typing 0 by mistake could withhold the token for a whole BU.

Some safeguards exist already: when the amount is RM 0, approvers see a review note that the lump sum is withheld, and an amount above the suggestion raises a warning. Each save is recorded, but without the old and new amount or a reason.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- The box becomes a clear choice of three: "Use suggested amount" (shows today's suggestion), "Set amount", or "Withhold for this BU".

- "Set amount" requires an amount above RM 0. Typing an amount above the suggestion shows a warning straight away that it is more than the remaining budget supports.

- "Withhold for this BU" asks you to confirm, shows how many people will not receive the token, and needs a written reason.

- Approvers see a review note with who withheld it, when, and the reason.

- Every change keeps a record of who made it, the date and time, the old and new setting, and the reason.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

Withholding the token for a whole BU can only happen on purpose, with a reason that approvers can see.

GOOD TO KNOW

- No change to existing records - reviews that already have an amount saved keep it exactly as it is.

- A reason is required for withholding. For "Set amount" a reason is optional, but it is recorded when given.

- Who can change the amount stays as today: the BU CEO and Group Rewards while the review is in Draft, and each approver during their own approval step.

- A companion request (headcount, total cost and balance beside the amount) changes the same card. We will build them together.
