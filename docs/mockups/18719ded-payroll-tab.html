2What is already there, and what actually changes

The request says these sections need building

All three already exist on the tab today — Basic Salary, Pay Items and CP38 each have their own card, their own Add control and their own list. Nothing here is new functionality.

What is genuinely inconsistent

The three cards present records differently from each other, the Add controls behave differently, and the tab does not use the full width. That is what this fixes.

Nothing about pay is touched. No salary, no pay item amount, no CP38 instalment, no tax figure and no effective-date rule. If a payroll figure moves, the change has gone wrong and gets reverted.
