What we are not doing, and why. The entry is taken off the profile, not wiped from the system. The employee’s pay history, their EA form and every closed payroll run are built from that same record — once a line can be erased, none of them can be defended in an audit. This is the shape you chose on 28 July; the only thing changing now is that you no longer have to look at it.
