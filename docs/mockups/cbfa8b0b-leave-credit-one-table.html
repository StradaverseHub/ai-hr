The time columns stay empty and disabled on a day type that does not use them — a rest day, off day or public holiday earns its credit from the day itself. They are all in one table, as you asked; nobody has to decide which section they are in.

Each row is checked against your own roster as you type. A day type that disagrees with the roster is flagged on the row and names what the roster actually holds, rather than failing after your manager has approved it.
