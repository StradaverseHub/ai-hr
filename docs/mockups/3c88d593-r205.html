Transaction Detail — one line per company, not one line per month

Employee 100814, August 2026. Entity Transfer from Fiamma (Chin Hin Property) to ISEDECOR BINA SDN BHD, effective 11 August 2026. Payroll already produces the two separate results correctly — this sketch is only about how Transaction View shows them.

Today

Payroll › Transaction View › Transaction Detail

This screen shows one employee's payroll year — pay items down the left, months across the top. There is one August column and one line per pay item, so both employers' figures land in the same cell and add together. Nothing on the screen says a transfer happened, and there is no way to tell how much belongs to Fiamma and how much to ISEDECOR BINA.

The August cell is the problem. RM 3,100.00 is the whole month added up across two different employers. Both companies are inside one figure, and the screen offers no way to pull them apart — which is exactly what was reported.

After this change

Payroll › Transaction View › Transaction Detail

A Company column is added, and each pay item gets its own line per company. August now reads as two lines — Fiamma for 1–10 August and ISEDECOR BINA for 11–31 August — with the annual total for each employer on its own line. Every other employee, who has one company all year, simply sees the same company name repeated and their figures unchanged.

After this change

Payroll › Transaction View › History — the two results, side by side

The History list already shows one line per payroll result, so August 2026 already appears twice for this employee. What it does not say is which days each employer paid for, or why there are two lines. Both lines gain the covered dates and a Split marker, and each still opens into its own full breakdown — earnings, deductions, employer contributions and statutory — for that company alone.

This sketch deliberately shows no statutory amounts: the only money figures on the page are the ones the request itself worked out (RM 3,100 ÷ 31 days × 10 days, and × 21 days). Real amounts come straight from each company's payslip and are not recalculated by this change.

What this change does not touch

No calculation, no proration rule, no statutory working and no payslip is altered. Every figure shown is read from the payslip each company already produced, which is what keeps Transaction Detail, the payroll result and the payslip in agreement. An employee who never changed company sees exactly the screen they see today.
