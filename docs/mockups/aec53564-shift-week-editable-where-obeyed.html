What changes, and what does not

No data changes. Not one shift’s stored week is edited, cleared or moved. This only decides whether the form lets you type into a box whose value it will then ignore.

The days follow the Schedule Group box live. Pick a group that sets a week and the days grey out as you pick it; clear the group and they come back. You never have to save to find out which you got.

We were wrong when we raised this, and we would rather say so than quietly build something else. We told you the shift’s copy of the week “stops being used” and recommended deleting the day fields. We then read the engine: the group only wins when it actually declares a week, and two of your shifts have no such group — for those, these fields are the only thing deciding when people work. Deleting them would have taken the controls away from the shifts that still need them.
