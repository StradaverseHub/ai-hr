THE SITUATION TODAY

When HR continues a Draft employee, the Employment, Bank & Statutory and Payroll tabs look and work differently from the same tabs on an active employee's profile. The Employment tab says "Hire date" and "Reporting manager", always shows a Provisional End Date box, and lists every candidate in one long dropdown. Bank Information has no custom payment holder options, Statutory (Malaysia) is laid out differently and some settings are missing, and there is no Payment Method or Payroll Policy, so HR has to open the employee again after activating to finish them.

One good piece of news: the date typed into "Hire date" already becomes the employee's Group Join Date when the draft is activated, and the Join Date for the current company starts on that same day. So the rename moves no date; only the wording changes.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

Employment tab

- "Hire date" is renamed "Group Join Date", with a note that the Join Date for the current company starts on the same day.

- The end date box only appears when the Employment Type needs one: for Contract it reads "Contract End Date", for Intern "Internship End Date". It is hidden for Full-Time and Part-Time.

- For Full-Time, the end of probation is worked out from the Probation Period on this tab (Group Join Date plus the period), and shown on the tab as a line saying when probation ends, so you can see it. The Probation Period starts filled in with your company's default length (3 months unless your hiring settings say otherwise). New full-time hires still show as "Probation" until it ends, as today. Part-Time hires carry no end date.

- "Reporting manager" is renamed "Direct Superior", and the long dropdown becomes the same search box used on the active profile: type an Employee ID or part of a name and pick from the matches. It offers the same people the list offers today.

Bank & Statutory tab

- Bank Information gets the same "Use a custom holder name" and "Use a custom holder identification" ticks as the active profile. Ticking one opens its box, and a ticked box must be filled in before the employee can be activated.

- Statutory (Malaysia) is rearranged into the same three sections as the active profile (EPF contribution, SOCSO & EIS information, and Income Tax (PCB)) with the same fields, choices and wording. That adds the EPF rate and amount boxes, Subject to SKBBK, Employer bears tax contribution and the full list of income tax residency choices. The training levy (HRDF) choice moves into its own Others Statutory section, as on the active profile.

Payroll tab

- Payment Method is added (Not specified, Bank Transfer, Cheque, Cash), the same as "How This Employee Is Paid" on the active profile. As there, Bank Transfer needs the bank and account number on the Bank & Statutory tab; until both are there, the draft's "still needed" list says so.

- Payroll Policy is added, offering the same list as the active profile: your company's active payroll policies, or the Monthly default. It appears only where Payroll is switched on.

For all three tabs

- Everything entered on the draft, including these new fields, is carried onto the active profile when you press Activate. Every check is made before the employee is created, so Activate never stops halfway with half an employee saved.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

One design for the same information whether the employee is a draft or active, a Direct Superior found by searching instead of scrolling, and bank, statutory and pay setup finished once, on the draft.

GOOD TO KNOW

- We will deliver this in three steps, each going live when it is ready: the Employment tab first, then Payroll, then Bank & Statutory. This one approval covers all three.

- The four Employment Types (Full-Time, Part-Time, Contract, Intern) are fixed in the system today, so the show-or-hide rule is set for each of the four.

- Drafts already in progress keep everything typed so far. A full-time draft that already holds an end date keeps that date when it is activated.

- Two Income Tax figures on the active profile, the qualifying-children count and the relief amount, are worked out from the employee's saved family record, so they appear once the employee is activated. The choices that drive them are all on the draft.

- If you would rather keep the end date box visible for Full-Time instead of working it out from the Probation Period, reject this plan and say so.
