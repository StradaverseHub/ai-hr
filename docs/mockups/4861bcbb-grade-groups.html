What happens to a rule when its group is gone

Today
“Grade 16-19 permanent, 3 days or more → Head of Department, then HR.”
Applies to the people in that group and nobody else.
→
With no grade group
“3 days or more → Head of Department, then HR.”
Applies to EVERY employee — a rule with no scope matches anyone.

All 205 become rules that match everybody, and they then compete with each other. The eight groups carrying 20 rules each collapse onto identical scopes — which the system already refuses as duplicates when you try to create them by hand.

So we have not removed them, and we would rather you decided this knowing the number. Grade groups no longer have anything to do with entitlement — that part is genuinely gone. What is left of them is approval routing, and it is most of yours. If you still want them removed, tell us which of these you want and we will do it:
  • Remove the groups and the 205 rules together, and re-key approval routing from scratch alongside the entitlement setup.
  • Keep them, renamed if the name is what bothers you — they are grade bands for approvals now, not benefit schemes.
