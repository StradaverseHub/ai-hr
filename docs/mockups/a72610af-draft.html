3Three things in the list we are not adding, and why

You listedWhy not
Division, Branch, Region, Line, Section, Team, Base, CategoryThese are read-only on the activated profile too. They move only through a recorded Movement, so there is no dropdown behaviour for the draft to copy. Adding editable ones would make a draft looser than the record it becomes.
Addresses and Emergency contactsThese are lists rather than fields — an employee can have several. They need the same staged treatment Family already has, which is real work rather than layout. Worth doing, but as its own request.
Payroll policy, TP3, pay items, zakatSame reason: they are records attached to an employee, not fields on a form, and they cannot exist until the employee does.
And one sentence from your request we are deliberately not acting on: that Draft and Active should share one underlying structure. A draft is kept apart precisely so a half-entered person can never appear in a headcount, a payroll run or a leave report. That separation is doing real work.
