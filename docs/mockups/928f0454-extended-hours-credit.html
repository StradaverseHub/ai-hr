⚠️ The part that is not in your request — the employee is paid less

Taking a Leave Credit for a day cancels that day’s overtime pay — it is one or the other, never both. On a rest day or public holiday that is a fair swap. On an ordinary working day the arithmetic is different:

8 extra hours on an ordinary working dayWhat the employee gets
Paid as overtime today (1.5×)12 hours of pay
Your proposed credit (≥ 8 hours → 1.0 day)8 hours of leave
Differencethe employee loses a third
This is why the system refuses it today. The block is not an oversight — it is a named rule, and the code says in as many words that a plain working day’s overtime is cash only. For staff earning under the statutory ceiling, overtime pay is an entitlement; swapping it for less time off is not something we will let a setting do quietly.
The good news: you already have the machinery for doing it properly. For rest-day and holiday credits the system already requires both the company switch and that individual employee’s recorded agreement before it will swap statutory overtime pay for time off. The new working-day credit will ride that same gate rather than go round it — so an employee who has not agreed keeps their overtime pay, and one who has agreed gets the credit.

And there is a fairer setting already built. The system can bank the extra time uplifted by the overtime rate — 8 hours worked becomes 12 hours banked, so nobody loses anything by choosing leave. Your flat 0.5 / 1.0 day tiers cannot express that. We will build your tiers as you specified, and leave the uplifted option available, so you can choose per company.

What we will build, and what we will not

We will build it: the toggle and four thresholds per company, hours counted from shift end only, rounded down to your tiers, one credit per employee per date, and no credit at all under the half-day threshold.

We will not let it bypass consent. Where the law makes overtime pay an entitlement, the credit needs the employee’s recorded agreement — the same gate rest-day credits already use. If you want that changed, it is a decision for you and your advisers, not a setting we will add quietly.

Nothing changes until you switch it on. Default off, per company, and every existing credit, balance and payslip is untouched.
