My Attendance – Web Clocking Improvements for Clocking Record Management, Working Hours, Location Tracking and Attendance Photo

Full plan · Open sketch

VERDICT: Partly there already   ·   SIZE: Large

THE SITUATION TODAY

Web Clocking already has a live time counter and tries to capture location without geofencing. Today's Log can hide captured coordinates, Optional photos require another choice, and employees lack a controlled Remove action.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- Keep the layout. Add Remove for eligible clockings, showing the selected date, direction and time in a confirmation dialog with a mandatory remark.
- Route removal through attendance correction approval. Show Removal requested while pending; only final approval excludes the clocking from calculations. Preserve original evidence and history.
- Refuse stale, duplicate or payroll-protected removals. If removing one clocking creates a broken sequence, explain why and direct the employee to HR for a coordinated correction; never silently remove another record.
- Label the counter Elapsed time today, explain that it is not payable time, and preserve its live updates and recovery after reopening.
- Show actual recorded coordinates and any existing location description separately from geofencing. Retain denied, unavailable or timed-out capture explanations for new clockings; do not guess missing historical locations.
- Automatically open the camera for Required and Optional photos. Required must succeed; Optional permits Skip and retry; Not Required bypasses the camera. Preserve review and retake.
- Retain duplicate-submission protection, including checking an uncertain clocking's result before retrying.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

Controlled correction requests, clearer elapsed time and location, and photo capture started directly from the clocking buttons.

GOOD TO KNOW

This uses the existing approval route, not a new immediate-removal policy. Pending removal leaves calculations intact. Geofencing and photo requirements remain enforced independently.

Attendance Applications History – Improve Filtering, Application Type Display, Bulk Approval and UI/UX

Full plan · Open sketch

VERDICT: Partly there already   ·   SIZE: Large

THE SITUATION TODAY

Applications History has organization and application filters and individual approval/rejection. It still shows technical references and raw attendance dates, lacks subtype labels and bulk actions, and places filters above the table.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- Add a collapsible left filter panel following Individual Attendance. Include one/all employees, Company, Department, Job Grade, Business Unit, Division, type, subtype, status and attendance Start/End Date. Keep submission-date filters separately labelled.
- Preserve filters after viewing or deciding a request. Provide Apply and Clear, plus a compact filter drawer on smaller screens.
- Remove the visible reference column and retain View. Show recorded Absent, Short Hours and Incomplete Entry subtypes, Self Overtime and an honest fallback for unknown historical subtypes.
- Add checkboxes and Select all eligible on this page, with a selection count. Changing filters or pages clears selection; other pages are never silently included.
- Confirm Approve Selected or Reject Selected before processing. Rejection requires remarks. Check each request's current permission, status and approval stage independently.
- Report each success, failure or skipped request and its reason, including further approval still required. Record the actual administrator, time and remarks.
- Use dd-mmm-yyyy attendance dates in list and detail views, white field-label and table-heading backgrounds, readable text and usable wrapping for long names.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

Faster filtering and controlled review of selected applications with clear outcomes.

GOOD TO KNOW

Existing access, self-approval and workflow restrictions remain. Bulk approval only completes the stage the administrator is permitted to decide.
