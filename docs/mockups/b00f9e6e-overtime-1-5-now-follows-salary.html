4Deliberately not drawn — and where the 2× and 3× already live

No “Overtime 2.0” and no “Overtime 3.0” rows. We are not creating them, so drawing them would promise something this plan does not deliver. If you want standing items at 2× and 3× built the same way as your 1.5 one, reject and say so.
The 2× and 3× are settings you already control. Rest-day and public-holiday overtime rates sit on your Attendance Policy screen and read 2 and 3 today. The ordinary working-day rate sits on your Payroll Policy screen and reads 1.5; the law stops you going below it, not above it.
The one thing being built has no screen. A tool we keep for copying an overtime item into higher-rate versions currently succeeds even when the item it is copying has been switched off. It will be made to stop and say so. There is nothing to look at, which is why it is written here rather than drawn.

And a correction we owe you. The earlier plan said nobody had ever been paid a cent from Overtime 1.5. Two one-off entries already existed when we wrote it — RM 5.00 on 3 August, which never went into a payroll run, and RM 2.00 on 4 August, which was paid on a completed July payslip. We checked whether the item was attached to anyone permanently and never checked whether anyone had keyed it as a one-off. Both are left exactly as they are.
