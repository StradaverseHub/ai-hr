THE SITUATION TODAY

Import Payroll History can already replace months that were loaded through the import screen. But any month that was processed in AIHR, or is linked to something already produced from it (a payment file, a statutory return, or later payroll in the same year), is rejected with one of the messages you quoted. There is no way to put corrected historical figures over it.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- Processed and Closed payroll can be overwritten by import. When your file matches an employee, company, month and Pay Cycle that already has payroll on file, processed or imported, the row is marked "Overwrite confirmation required" instead of being rejected.

- Each row is shown as one of three things: New record, Overwrite confirmation required, or Rejected. Every row shows the Employee ID and name, company, year and month, Pay Cycle, and the codes from your file. Rejected rows show the Excel row, the code and the exact reason.

- For each overwrite row you can open a side-by-side view of what is on file now and what your file says, item by item, with changed items highlighted.

- You tick the records you want to overwrite. Only ticked records are replaced; unticked ones are left exactly as they are. A Select all box is provided.

- Overwriting is a straight replacement. Your file's earnings, deductions, net pay and every statutory amount (EPF, SOCSO, EIS, PCB, Zakat and the others) become the figures on file, exactly as written. Nothing is recalculated with today's rates or settings, no payroll is run, and no payslip is generated.

- Before you confirm, the screen lists what has already gone out for the ticked months: a bank payment file, EPF, SOCSO, EIS or tax returns already generated, and later months in the same year whose tax was worked out from the old figures. These are shown as warnings for you to acknowledge, not as reasons to refuse.

- Every overwrite is recorded: the original values, the new values, who did it and when. The original payroll is never erased; it is set aside. The whole import can be undone from Log File, which puts the original figures back.

- A file with any Rejected row still has to be corrected and uploaded again before anything is imported. This keeps half a file from being loaded by accident.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

Corrected historical figures can be loaded over payroll that was already processed or closed, with a clear comparison, your explicit choice of which records to replace, a full record of what changed, and a way back.

GOOD TO KNOW

- The payslip an employee was already given is not changed or regenerated. In My Payslip they keep seeing the payslip they were issued. HR screens, payroll reports, the EA form and later statutory reports use the overwritten figures.

- Nothing that already went out is redone for you. Bank files are not re-sent, and EPF, SOCSO, EIS and tax returns are not regenerated or resubmitted. If an amended return is needed, HR generates and submits it as usual. Tax already deducted in later months stays as it was paid.

- Payroll that is still in draft (not yet processed) cannot be overwritten by import. Finish or discard it in Payroll first, so two versions of the same month are never being edited at once.

- Only people who can import payroll history for that company can overwrite. Overwriting processed payroll changes money figures on record, so please check the comparison before confirming.
