What we will NOT do, and why it matters to you

We will not write the attendance row directly. Corrected clock times change overtime, and overtime is paid — so every correction goes through the same calculation the approval path uses, and leaves the same record. What an administrator skips is the WAIT, not the trail. You still get, for every corrected day: who changed it, when, what it was before, and why.
