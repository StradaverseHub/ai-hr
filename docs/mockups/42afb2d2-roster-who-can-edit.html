3What this sketch does not show

  • The alternative. If you would rather a supervisor could change their own team's future dates, that is a real change to who may change the roster, and the plan asks you to reject and say so. A picture cannot show a choice, so it stays in the words — along with the two things we would need to agree first: which supervisors, and what should happen to a day that has already been attended.
  • Who appears in each list. Both panels above deliberately carry the same six people. On your data today no reporting lines are in force at ISEDECOR BINA SDN BHD and no department has a head recorded, so there is no real supervisor-to-team list we could have drawn honestly.
  • The company picker at the top left, which appears when your login covers more than one company. It is the same for both and we did not measure it for a supervisor login, so it is named rather than drawn.
  • The Edit panel itself, the Upload preview, the Audit trail rows, and the rows inside "This week's exceptions". All real; none of them changes with this decision.
  • Twenty-five of the month's thirty-one dates, and eight of the fourteen people. Everything drawn is stored; nothing drawn is invented.
One line of the plan you approved was not delivered. It said dates from tomorrow onwards could be changed by a supervisor as well as by an HR Administrator. The first half — today or earlier is an HR Administrator's, after a confirmation — is live and is drawn above. The second half was not built, because the right to change a roster day sits deeper than the screen and setting a day there has only ever been allowed to an administrator. The plan recommends leaving it that way; approving is how you say yes.
