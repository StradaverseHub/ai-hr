Two things in your screenshot that are deliberately not drawn above

  • The Entitled tick. On this system, a claim type with no limit set means unlimited, not blocked. An Entitled tick would flip that meaning for every claim type that has no limit today, so it needs its own round rather than being slipped in with these five points.
  • The third level (group, then claim, then sub claim). This system has two levels — a claim type and its items — and the limit is held at the claim type, with items sharing it. Adding a third level, and a separate limit at each level, is a bigger change than the five you sent back.
