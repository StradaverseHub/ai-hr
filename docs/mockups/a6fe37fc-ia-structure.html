The six things that read as arbitrary today, and where each one goes
What you find todayWhy it reads as arbitraryProposed
Earning method, rounding and carry forward are in General setting. The step called Entitlement holds the day tables. Two halves of one question — how many days — two steps apart. Both in Tab 3 · What they are entitled to.
Per-date approval is in General setting. It is an approval behaviour, four steps from anything else about approval. Tab 5 · Who approves it.
Send to payroll module is a per-leave-type setting on a different screen. Every other per-type setting is on the leave type. Tab 1 · The leave type.
The company-wide half-day deduction card renders inside every leave type’s Condition step. One company decision, drawn 25 times. Company rules, once.
“Who qualifies” is answered in three places: the type’s own criteria, the entitlement eligibility rules, and the approval assignment scope. Three surfaces, one question — the complaint that started this item. One question, two views of the same rows: Tab 2 for the type’s gate, Tab 3 for the rules that reach it.
Leave application rules holds ten cards, and one of them — the balance card, three sub-forms — is about balance, not about applying. The card grid is a bucket, not a category. The balance card and its three sub-forms move to Tab 3.
