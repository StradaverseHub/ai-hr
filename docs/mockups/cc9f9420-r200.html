3Two things worth knowing before you approve

There is no blank KWSP 6 for us to copy. The specimen this form was built from is a completed Borang A produced by another payroll system's report engine, not by KWSP — the agency publishes no blank page to print onto. So this plan draws a faithful replica of the layout you described, but the sheet will keep saying, in small print at the foot, that it is our drawing and not the agency's own page. That sentence is what keeps the claim honest, and it stays.
The CATATAN notes and the penalty paragraph are agency words, not agency structure. We can place them exactly where you asked; we cannot quote sentences from a document we do not have. They will print in our own plain Malay and English wording, marked as ours. Send a KWSP 6 page and we swap the text word for word — no box moves, nothing is redrawn.
The sheet gets fuller, so it holds fewer names. The payment band, the two stamp boxes, the eight notes and the warning take up roughly a third of the page. A sheet holds about 22 names today and about 14 after this. Nothing is lost — the carry-forward rows already handle it — but a 33-person return that prints on two sheets today will print on three.
Nothing about the numbers changes. The wage, the two contribution columns, the totals, the “contribution month is the payroll month plus one” rule and the review screen that names anyone missing an EPF member number all stay exactly as they are. The i-Akaun upload file is untouched. This is a drawing change only.
