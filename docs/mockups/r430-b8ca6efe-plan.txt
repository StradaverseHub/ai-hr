VERDICT: Partly there already   ·   SIZE: Large

WHAT YOU ASKED FOR

On the employee's own Web Clocking page: remove the Switch link that lets someone change their Current Shift; let Clock In and Clock Out both be pressed at any time, in any order; add a per-employee Attendance Photo setting (Required, Optional, or Not Required) under Personnel Management; let the photo step use a computer's own camera, not only a phone's; and remove the "register this device" step and message.

THE SITUATION TODAY

The Switch link lets an employee page through other shifts that happen to run on today's weekday - not necessarily their own - and change only what the screen shows, never what actually gets recorded. It is confusing and serves no purpose.

Clock In is greyed out once clocked in, and Clock Out is greyed out until clocked in, so a forgotten Clock In can only be fixed by asking HR, exactly the problem you describe.

A photo is always required, every single clock-in and clock-out, for every employee, with no way to turn it off. The step already works with a laptop or desktop's own built-in camera as well as a phone's - that part already works today, it is simply hidden behind the always-on photo requirement.

Before any clocking, the employee must register the browser or phone they are using (a "Register this device" button and a message confirming it is registered). This step is required on this screen for every employee today, with no setting to turn it off.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- Remove the Switch link entirely. The Current Shift shown is always the one actually assigned to the employee through their Shift or Schedule Group - nothing the employee can change from this screen.

- Clock In and Clock Out are both always clickable. Clocking out with no clock-in beforehand, or clocking in again after already clocking out, are both allowed and saved. Any day that does not pair up neatly (a clock-out with no matching clock-in, for example) is saved as an incomplete day and flagged for a manager to look at - the same way a forgotten clock-out is already flagged today. Nothing changes about how a completed, paired day's hours or overtime are worked out.

- A new "Attendance Photo" setting is added for each employee, under Personnel Management, on the employee's Attendance tab, right next to the existing "Use Geofencing for Clocking" switch, in a clearly labelled "Web Clocking" section. Three choices: Required - a photo must be taken or the clocking is refused; Optional - the employee may take a photo or skip it; Not Required - no camera step is shown at all. Every employee starts on Not Required, and HR sets it per person.

  Said plainly: today everyone must take a photo, every time, with no choice. Once this ships, nobody is required to take a photo unless HR turns that specific employee's setting to Required or Optional. Switching that off is HR's decision to make per person, same as geofencing already works.

- The camera step keeps working exactly as it does today, which already supports a laptop or desktop's own webcam as well as a phone's camera, through the same single capture screen (live preview, Take Photo, then Retake or Continue) for both Required and Optional. Optional additionally offers a plain "Skip Photo" choice next to "Take Photo".

- The "register this device" step, its button, and its message are removed from this screen entirely. An employee who logs in and opens Web Clocking can clock in or out straight away, with no device step at all.

  The honest tradeoff, stated plainly: today, every clock-in photo is tied to the one specific browser or phone that was registered to take it, and if that one device is lost or compromised, HR can switch off that single device on the Attendance Policy screen without touching any of the employee's other devices. After this change, that one specific lever is gone from this screen - if HR needs to cut someone off, it has to be done through their login, not a single device. Registering a device today takes one tap with no approval needed, so it was never a strong lock to begin with; the photo and the location already recorded with every clock-in and clock-out remain the real safeguards, and neither of those changes.

WHAT HAPPENS TO WHAT IS ALREADY SAVED

Every clocking already recorded, every attendance day already worked out, and every past photo stay exactly as they are.

Devices already registered are not deleted. HR can still see every employee's registered devices and switch any one of them off from the Attendance Policy screen - that screen does not change. Only the Web Clocking page itself stops asking for a device.

Nothing about how a completed day's worked hours or overtime is calculated changes - only which clockings are allowed to be recorded in the first place, and how an incomplete day is flagged afterwards.

WHAT IS NOT DONE

A separate, smaller "quick clock" button that appears elsewhere (outside this Web Clocking page) is older, takes no photo, and is not touched by this round. If your company has separately switched on "Require Registered Device", that smaller button will still ask for one; this round only removes it from the dedicated Web Clocking page.

There is no phone app version of clocking today, so nothing changes there.

The Attendance Policy screen that lists and manages registered devices is not changed or removed - it still works, it is simply no longer consulted by this one screen.

Pictures attached: the Web Clocking page with the Switch link gone and both buttons always available; the camera step showing the live preview and the captured-photo review; and the new Attendance Photo setting on the employee's Attendance tab in Personnel Management.
