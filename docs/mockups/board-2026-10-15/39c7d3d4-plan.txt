VERDICT: Partly there already   ·   SIZE: Moderate

THE SITUATION TODAY

Payroll already saves the processing month separately from a transaction's Effective Date. However, its entry screens and saving checks require that date to fall inside the selected month. This prevents entering work from an earlier month for payment in the current run.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- Allow a valid Effective Date outside the selected Payroll Month when adding or editing an Individual Transaction, including Day by Day entries.
- Keep Payroll Month, Pay Cycle and Effective Date independent. Changing one will not silently change another.
- Show the saved Effective Date when viewing, editing and reporting the transaction.
- Continue checking required values, employee access, compatible payroll items, genuine date rules and locked payroll runs.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

HR can enter September work for payment or deduction in October while keeping both the original work date and the intended processing period clear.

GOOD TO KNOW

This does not change payroll formulas, statutory rules or transactions that have already been processed. Automatic Attendance-to-Payroll transfers keep their existing ownership and protection rules.
