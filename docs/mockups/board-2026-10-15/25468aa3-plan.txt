THE SITUATION TODAY

Full Flexi already uses required working duration for several attendance judgments, but its form still requires fixed clock times. Breaks already have a per-row Flexible choice, and shift colours currently come from a small fixed palette. Overtime windows and shift premiums are still edited on the Shift page.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- Make Full Flexi working-time setup show only required normal working hours. Hide fixed start, end and half-day clock fields for this type.
- Judge completion from qualifying recorded working time after applicable breaks. An unfinished clocking session stays incomplete, and a different clock time alone does not make Full Flexi late or early out.
- Keep each Flexible choice, duration input and visible Minutes label together on one line, with responsive layout and existing break validation.
- Move the existing overtime-window and shift-premium editor to Work Schedule & Overtime Rates, preserving every saved value when basic shift details are edited.
- Add a visual colour picker, HEX entry and preview. Use the saved colour consistently in existing shift indicators with readable text and icons.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

Full Flexi setup will describe a duration-based shift clearly, and HR can maintain breaks and colours without unrelated pay controls crowding the page.

GOOD TO KNOW

Clocking does not automatically check an employee out after the required hours. Open elapsed time is provisional. Fixed and Semi-Flexi shifts keep their existing rules, and existing saved colours remain recognizable.
