THE SITUATION TODAY

Attendance already resolves each employee's schedule and applicable holiday calendar. It does not yet automatically assign an employee-specific replacement when a public holiday falls on a rest day.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- Detect public holidays that coincide with an employee's scheduled rest day using their effective schedule and applicable holiday policy.
- Search subsequent dates in order until the policy permits a replacement working day, skipping other holidays, rest days and previously assigned replacements.
- Retain the original holiday and rest day, and show the replacement date with its original holiday and reason.
- Apply the same effective holiday result to attendance, calendars, leave and the overtime/payroll calculations that already depend on holiday day types.
- Make repeat processing safe and keep a history of changes when schedules, calendars or policies are corrected.
- Send unclear rules, unresolved dates and changes affecting locked payroll history for HR review instead of silently choosing a date or rewriting past pay.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

Straightforward holiday/rest-day clashes are handled automatically for each employee. HR can trace every replacement and review cases that need a policy decision or a protected-period correction.

GOOD TO KNOW

Employees can have different replacement dates because their schedules differ. The configured jurisdiction and company policy determine entitlement; a rule from one region must not be assumed to apply everywhere. Off days and half days follow the applicable policy rather than being treated as normal working days by default.
