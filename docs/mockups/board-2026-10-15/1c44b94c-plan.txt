THE SITUATION TODAY

Attendance Allowance already supports multiple shifts, comparisons and Monthly, Weekly and Daily methods. Its current shift box is cramped, and Attendance Conditions use one flat AND/OR choice. Several requested event counts, short-hours measures and leave measures are missing from the current condition builder.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- Use clean white form surfaces in light mode and readable matching surfaces in dark mode, with consistent labels and spacing.
- Replace Applicable Shifts with searchable multi-selection, removable choices and Select all / Clear all. Preserve saved choices for correction if a shift becomes unavailable.
- Add a visual condition builder with nested All/Any groups, add/remove/reorder controls and a readable bracketed preview.
- Offer separate counts and durations for late arrival, early departure, absence, short hours, incomplete entries and approved leave, alongside existing measures.
- Show each measure's unit and the exact calculation period. Validate the complete policy and preview its decision before activation.
- Preserve existing saved policy behavior until HR explicitly saves a new version using the expanded conditions.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

HR can express grouped attendance conditions clearly and check why an allowance or deduction qualifies before transferring it to payroll.

GOOD TO KNOW

Monthly continues to mean a calendar month. Weekly uses Monday to Sunday, attributed to the month containing Sunday; Daily uses each date. Counts mean distinct qualifying dates, while leave duration uses approved day fractions. Incomplete clocking is counted as incomplete, and unavailable time measurements are never invented as zero.
