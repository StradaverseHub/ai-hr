THE SITUATION TODAY

Schedule Groups already contain identity, attendance, overtime and dated rotation settings, but they appear as scrolling sections. A shift's current default group also acts as its allowed-group membership, which prevents safe reuse. There is no separate Enable Exception or Duty Roster Control setting.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- Organize settings into tabs for General, Attendance, Overtime and Working Day / Schedule Pattern, retaining each section's independent save and unsaved edits.
- Rename Rule Name to Schedule Group Name and add a separate Schedule Group Label used in relevant group lists, assignments and roster displays.
- Add Enable Exception, initially Off. When On, exempt the group from Absent, Late In and Early Out exception judgments and their attendance-based deductions from the setting's effective date.
- Let an active shift be allowed in several groups without moving it out of another group. Keep each group's own working week and rotation.
- Exclude archived shifts from new choices while retaining saved historical references visibly.
- Add Duty Roster Control under Working Day / Schedule Pattern. New groups start Off; On permits authorized individual roster overrides, with validation and audit history.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

A clearer setup journey, reusable shifts and explicit control over attendance exemptions and individual roster changes.

GOOD TO KNOW

Enable Exception preserves measured working time, raw clocks, leave, overtime, statutory calculations and existing payroll results. Short-hours and incomplete-entry evidence remains available, and allowance policies still evaluate their configured measured facts. Existing groups will retain their current ability to make manual roster changes during the upgrade; HR can then turn that ability Off explicitly.
