VERDICT: Partly there already   ·   SIZE: Large

THE SITUATION TODAY

Attendance workflows already support several approval stages and employee-group matching. Stage names are entered manually, the available employee criteria are limited, and repeating Direct Superior does not currently walk up the reporting chain.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- Use clean white form surfaces in light mode and readable matching surfaces in dark mode on the company list and View/Edit screens.
- Generate Level 1, Level 2 and later labels automatically. Add Approval Level adds the next level; removing or moving a level updates the sequence.
- Add Position, Employment Type, Branch, Region, Line, Section, Team, Base, Category and searchable employee selection, alongside the existing assignment fields.
- Follow each person's Direct Superior to resolve successive approval levels for Attendance Irregularity and Self Overtime.
- Refuse submission with a useful message when a required superior is missing, inactive, the applicant, repeated or part of a reporting loop. HR must correct the relationship or use an explicitly configured fallback.
- Preserve the actual approvers chosen when an application is submitted and keep existing approval history intact.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

HR can configure standard approval levels once, match the intended employees and see how each level follows the reporting hierarchy. Employees receive a clear explanation when the required chain needs correction.

GOOD TO KNOW

Existing applications retain their assigned approvers. New routing applies to new submissions after the updated workflow is saved. Priority determines which matching workflow applies; conflicting equal-priority rules must be corrected before saving.
