The screens are already fixed. Since last week a leave type you do not qualify for no longer appears on your balance cards or in your Request Leave list. What is left is the history: entitlement that was granted before, to people whose profile does not say whether they qualify.

What we propose — in this order, because the order is the point

1
We send you the list. Every affected employee, which leave type, how many days, and which fact is missing. The report already exists and only reads — it changes nothing.
2
You fill the fields in. For most of these people it is a two-minute job, and it is the only step that actually solves the problem rather than working around it.
3
Then you tell us what a blank should mean — once the number is small enough that the answer barely matters either way.
4
And only then we reverse the Maternity days still standing for anyone who genuinely should not have them. Not before you say so.
Why we are not just changing the rule now. Treating a blank as “does not qualify” would take Caregiver Leave and Natural Disaster Leave away from the 48 people with no marital status recorded — because those two types genuinely restrict by it. We would be fixing one wrong grant by cancelling two real ones, for more people.

Nothing is broken while this sits

The cards and the Request Leave list are already correct. This is about what was granted in the past, and what happens at the next year-open. Nobody can currently apply for leave they do not qualify for.

We will not delete a balance on a guess. Those are days on real employees' records. Step 4 happens when you say it does, and we will show you exactly what it would change first.
