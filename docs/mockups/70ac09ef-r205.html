There are TWO separate blockers on Medical Leave, and this matters: even if saving the earning method did open the year automatically, Medical would still credit 0.00, because the six-table ambiguity stops the calculation regardless of what triggers it. Fixing the trigger alone would change nothing you can see.

7 · So should saving a setting create entitlement by itself?

Automatic — correcting

Adjusting figures people have already been granted. There is a before and an after, the change is recorded against each person, and it can be read back on their record. This is what moved your 35 Hospitalization balances.

Deliberate — creating

Handing a whole year of days to everyone for the first time. It is money and it is time off. It gets a rehearsal you can read before anything is written, and a downloadable log naming every person afterwards. That is Generate Entitlement, and we recommend keeping it as the one place a year is opened.

What this fixes, in order

  • The Generate Entitlement preview names the real blocker per employee instead of a category.
  • The Entitlement Tables screen warns you the moment a leave type has several tables and no rule.
  • A 0.00 on My Leave and Employee Balances is never silent again, including this case.
  • Saving an earning method tells you what it did and did not do, and points at the next step.
  • Once Medical Leave has one table in force, or a rule per table, Generate Entitlement credits everyone in one run — and from the following year it behaves exactly like Hospitalization.
