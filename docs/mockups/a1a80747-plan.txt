VERDICT: Partly there already   ·   SIZE: Moderate

THE SITUATION TODAY

When HR opens Reprocess on a processed payroll, employees who need attention are split across two lists. Reprocess Required holds employees whose payslip is out of date. New / Pending holds employees who have no payslip on this run yet, such as a late hire or someone with a new individual transaction. Both lists already go through the same reprocessing, but HR has to look in two places.

HR must also press Check Tax Readiness before the Reprocess button works. If one employee's tax readiness changes between that check and pressing Reprocess, the whole request is refused and HR has to check again.

The result screen already lists each employee, and one employee's problem already does not stop the others. But employees held back for income-tax recalculation do not appear in the results at all.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- One list, called Reprocess Required. It includes everyone who needs processing: employees with an out-of-date payslip, plus new or pending employees with no payslip yet. Each employee appears once, even if several things changed for them.

- Each row shows a small label, "New" or "Changed", so HR can still tell a first payslip from a recalculated one.

- The Check Tax Readiness button is removed. HR ticks the employees and presses Reprocess. The tax-readiness check then runs automatically, one employee at a time, as part of the reprocessing.

- Employees who need income-tax recalculation are skipped, as today, and the others carry on. One employee is never able to stop the rest.

- The results show every employee with one of four outcomes: Reprocessed successfully; Skipped – income tax recalculation required; Skipped – nothing payable; Failed – processing error, with the reason. Totals at the top show Selected, Reprocessed, Skipped and Failed.

- Skipped employees have a link to Income Tax Recalculation. Once that is done, they appear in Reprocess Required again.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

All employees who need processing are in one place, and one button does the work. HR never has to repeat a check by hand. The results make clear who is done and who still needs action.

GOOD TO KNOW

- We're not touching how anything is calculated, how changes are detected, the tax-readiness rules or income-tax recalculation. Approval, closing, releasing and payment stay as they are.

- Employees with nothing to pay this month still show in their own "Nothing payable" group. They are not mixed into Reprocess Required.

- Because the check now runs at the moment each employee is processed, it always uses up-to-date information. The "Tax readiness changed, please check again" message goes away.
