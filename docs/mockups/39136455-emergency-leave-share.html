2Proposed — each row tells its own story, and the transfer is visible

Read across the Emergency Leave row: no entitlement of its own, 2 days taken, 1 day waiting, and 3 days drawn in from Annual Leave to cover them. Read across Annual Leave: 16 days, 3 of them lent out. The pool still has 13.00, and it is shown once, on the row that owns it.
Emergency Leave's Balance is 0.00 on purpose, and that is the one thing worth knowing before you approve this: Emergency Leave has no balance of its own to carry: it borrows. So the caption on that row names the pool and the days actually left in it, which is the number you want when someone asks "can they take another day of Emergency Leave".
