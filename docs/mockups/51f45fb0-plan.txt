THE SITUATION TODAY

On the Rewards access page, one administrator can add, change or remove access, including their own, and it takes effect as soon as they confirm. AIHR already shows a before-and-after preview and records who made each change and when, but nobody else has to agree to it, and there is no export of the access list for periodic reviews.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- Saving an access change (add, edit or remove) creates a request marked "Pending". Nothing changes until it is approved.

- A "Waiting for approval" section on the Rewards access page lists pending requests with the before and after, who asked and when.

- Another person who manages Rewards access for that company approves or rejects it. Rejecting needs a reason.

- Nobody can approve their own request, and nobody can approve a request that changes their own access.

- A request lapses automatically if the review leaves Draft or the review's access changes in some other way before approval, so an approval always applies to what was actually shown.

- Every request, approval and rejection is recorded with requester, approver, dates and times, and before and after values.

- New "Export access list" button: downloads every assignment in your companies with person, role, review, status and the last change, for periodic access review.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

No single person can grant or widen access to reward data on their own, and you have a ready-made list for access reviews.

GOOD TO KNOW

- A company needs at least two people who can manage Rewards access, otherwise requests cannot be approved. The page will warn when a company has only one.

- When someone creates a new unit review, they are still given the Group Rewards seat on it automatically so that setup can start; this is recorded and shows in the export. Everything added after that needs the second approval.

- The approver is any other person who manages Rewards access for that company. A separate named "checker" role is not part of this plan; if you need one, reject and say so.
