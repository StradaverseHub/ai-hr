THE SITUATION TODAY

Part of this already works: when a person's login is archived, suspended, or their resignation or last working date in People has passed, they automatically lose all Rewards access, show as "Disabled or suspended login" on the Rewards access page, and stop counting as cover for their role.

What is missing: access has no start or end date, there is no proper way to appoint a stand-in for an approver who is away, and someone who has resigned but is still serving notice, or who has moved to another company or business unit, keeps access until their last day or until someone removes it.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- Each assignment gets a "From" date and an optional "Until" date. Access counts only between those dates.

- A proxy can be appointed: choose the role, the person being covered and the dates. The proxy acts in that seat only for that period. The existing "BG CEO / authorised proxy" role uses this.

- Access is suspended automatically, checked every time the person uses Rewards, when People shows they have resigned and are serving notice, have been terminated, or (for a BU CEO) are no longer in the company or business unit of that review.

- A suspended assignment shows "Suspended" with the reason and the date of the change in People. Group Rewards gets a notification.

- If the suspended person was the only one in a required approval role, the review shows that role as missing.

- Only Group Rewards can restore suspended access, with a written reason, and the restore needs a second approval.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

Access to reward data follows people's real employment status and planned absences without anyone having to remember to remove it.

GOOD TO KNOW

- In Rewards a review waits for a seat, not for a named person. So once a proxy or replacement holds the seat, any review waiting at that stage is theirs to action; nothing needs to be moved. Decisions already made stay recorded under the original person's name.

- Checking on every use, rather than once a night, means there is never a gap of up to a day during which a leaver still has access.

- The second approval for restoring access depends on the separate request for second approval on access changes.
