THE SITUATION TODAY

An employee can now open their own attendance day and submit a correction for approval. When it is rejected, the day keeps its original figures, but the employee cannot see why: nobody is asked to record an explanation. The day screens also need a deliberate phone layout.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- Ask the supervisor or HR to enter a reason before rejecting an attendance correction. Keep that explanation with the decision and show it on the employee's day screen and in My Request.

- Show each rejected request separately, with its requested times, decision date and rejection reason. A later request for the same day will not erase the earlier explanation.

- Keep old rejected requests visible with “Rejection reason not recorded” rather than inventing an explanation.

- Make both the employee and HR day screens comfortable on a phone: stack their cards, keep labels beside their figures, let the overtime figures scroll inside their card, and make correction fields and buttons easy to reach.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

An employee can understand a refusal and submit a better correction without guessing what went wrong. The employee and HR see the same attendance figures on a phone as on a larger screen.

GOOD TO KNOW

- Rejection leaves attendance and overtime unchanged. Approval still uses the existing review process and recalculates the day as it does today.

- A person who can already make the decision can record the explanation. This gives nobody extra access to another employee's attendance.

- The rejection explanation belongs to the approver. The employee's reason and optional note stay separate.

- Rejecting several selected attendance or overtime requests together uses the same required explanation for each. Approval requires no explanation.

- Old decisions will not acquire explanations after the fact. Nothing already saved is replaced.

- The request records who asked, when, the requested values and their reason. The day figures before and after an approved correction are recorded when it is applied. This work does not add a separate snapshot of the day when the request was submitted.
