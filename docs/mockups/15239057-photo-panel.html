Why the wording in the request mattersREAD THIS BEFORE APPROVING

The request says the panel “overlaps other screens or UI elements”. It does not. Nothing is drawn over anything — the panel is being cut off by the edge of the area the page scrolls inside. That distinction decides the fix: making the panel sit “higher” than its neighbours would change nothing at all, because nothing is competing with it. It has to be lifted out of the scrolling area instead, and then clamped so it can never run past the window edge.

We have fixed this exact thing once before, on the payroll row menu, and the note left behind on that fix describes the same cause. So this is a known trap with a known remedy rather than a new investigation.

Two things worth knowing. Nothing on the screen is labelled “Upload Photo” — the button you press is a pencil marked “Edit photo”, and “Upload photo” is inside the panel it opens. And the same panel is used by the company logo editor, so this fix improves both screens, not just Personnel.
