One thing to look at while you are here

55 of your 75 active employees have no gender recorded, and 48 have no marital status. The eligibility rules treat “not recorded” as “does not match”, so once the wrong credits are cleared, those employees will not see a Maternity card — not because they are male, but because the field is empty. The same is true of Paternity Leave. Filling those fields in is what makes the rule do what you mean; we can give you the list of who is missing what.
