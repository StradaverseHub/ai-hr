THE SITUATION TODAY

AIHR already has this rule. Each review has a "pay-change exclusion" window that Group Rewards can change in the review settings. Anyone whose basic salary changed inside it gets no merit, promotion or special adjustment, their new basic stays the same, and their salary is left out of the salary increase pools. They stay in the bonus run, and their salary still counts towards the bonus pool.

There are five gaps. The window for new reviews starts as 1 October to 31 January, not 31 December. Only the salary in effect on the snapshot date is checked, so a change dated after the snapshot is missed, and the bonus is then worked out on the old salary. A record that was re-saved without any change to basic salary, such as a transfer, can count as a change. The reason appears only on each person's own page, with no date or type of change. There is no list, no export, no override with a reason, and the pools do not show what was left out.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- New reviews start with the window set to 1 October to 31 December of the reward year. Group Rewards can still change it. Existing reviews keep their current window.

- The check looks at every basic salary change dated inside the window, including future-dated ones, not just the salary on the snapshot date. It runs again whenever the snapshot is refreshed.

- Only a real change in basic salary counts. A lateral transfer (change of company, BU, department or position) with the same basic salary does not, and those employees stay eligible for both the salary increase and the bonus.

- For these employees, the bonus and their share of the bonus pool use the adjusted basic salary, meaning the latest basic salary inside the window as recorded in AIHR.

- In the people list, these employees are tagged "Salary adjusted 1 Oct to 31 Dec: not eligible for salary increase", with the date and type of the change (for example promotion or increment). Where AIHR has no type recorded, it says "Salary change".

- Their merit, promotion and special adjustment show 0% and cannot be edited.

- Under the salary increase pools: "Excluded from base: X employees, MYR Y payroll", with a link to the list.

- Group Rewards gets a list of excluded employees with the change details, and can export it.

- Group Rewards can keep a specific employee eligible with a written reason, for example a correction of a payroll error. The override is shown in the list and recorded with who, when and why.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

The rule follows your 1 October to 31 December policy, catches changes made after the snapshot, and everyone can see who was left out and why. The pools show the amount excluded.

GOOD TO KNOW

- We're not changing who counts for the bonus. These employees stay in the bonus run under the normal bonus rules, and their adjusted salary counts towards the bonus pool.

- The type of change comes from how the change was entered in People. AIHR has types for promotion, increment, confirmation and salary adjustment; counter offers and off-cycle increments show the type they were entered as, or "Salary change" if none was given.

- Your approved request about the snapshot date (refreshing it for year-end rules) works together with this one.
