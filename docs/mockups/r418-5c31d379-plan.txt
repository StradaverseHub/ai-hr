VERDICT: Small fixes to what is already there   ·   SIZE: Small to Medium

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- Borang A, the identity number: it follows the employee's status, the same status payroll already uses to decide their EPF rate.
  - Malaysian citizen: the IC (MyKad) number.
  - Permanent resident: the MyPR number.
  - Non-citizen: the passport number.
  A non-citizen with a passport number is no longer stopped for having no IC. If the number that applies is missing, the message names that number, for example "Passport number". This is the same rule we are putting on Borang 8A (your other request), so both forms agree.

- Borang A, people with no EPF contribution: anyone whose employer share and employee share are both RM0.00 for the payroll cycles you chose is left off the form. They are left off the preview, the PDF, the text copy and the counts. Page totals, the number of employees and the grand total are worked out only from the people who remain. If either share is not zero, the person stays on the form. Payroll records are not changed.

- EPF shelf: the "Borang BBCB — Not built yet" card is removed. BBCD (Borang Bayaran Caruman Bulanan) stays. You confirmed BBCB was the same form.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

Borang A lists only the people who actually contributed, with the right identity number for each of them, and the EPF shelf shows each form once.

GOOD TO KNOW

- The status comes from the EPF citizenship status on each employee's statutory record. If it is wrong there, their EPF rate is also wrong, so it is the one place to correct.

- We hold a permanent resident's MyPR number in the same box as an IC number, because the two cards use the same 12-digit format.

- The i-Akaun upload file follows the same rule for who is listed, so the file and the form always agree. If KWSP needs the people with zero contributions in the file, tell us and we will keep them in the file only.

- We are keeping the name BBCD, the name printed on the form you sent us. We could not find a different official name for it on KWSP's current forms list.

- The column keeps the heading printed on the agency's own form (No. Kad Pengenalan), even on a line that carries a passport number, because that is how the form itself is laid out.

- The KWSP i-Akaun upload file has only an IC number box and no box for a passport number. So a non-citizen with no IC, such as Employee D, appears on the printed Borang A with their passport number, but the upload file names them and cannot include them. We do not know how KWSP wants a passport holder entered in that file. If you can find out (for example from your KWSP officer), tell us and we will add it.

- No EPF amounts change.
