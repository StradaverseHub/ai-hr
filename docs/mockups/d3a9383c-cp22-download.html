The 7 you will still type — every time, and here is why

  1. LHDN branch address (box 1) — which branch you file with is your choice, not a record we hold.
  2. The tax-agent tick (11) — only you know whether that correspondence address is your agent’s.
  3. Passport previously registered with LHDN (18) — never collected.
  4. Spouse’s income tax number (25) — there has never been a place in the system to keep it.
  5. Fluctuating payments and when they will be known (28) — not held anywhere.
  6. Previous employer in Malaysia (29) — the system has never asked for it when hiring.
  7. Your signature (30) — yours to sign.

What the download actually is

LHDN’s own CP22 (Pin. 1/2020) page, unaltered — same layout, same wording, same Malay and English. The typing boxes sit on top of it.

Every box stays typeable, including the 26 filled ones, so you can change anything before you sign. Nothing is locked.

Nothing is saved back. This reads records you already keep and hands you a file.

What this picture does not promise

The ° boxes fill only when the detail is on record. 33 of your 40 staff records hold Malaysian tax details today; the other 7 produce a form with those boxes empty rather than wrong. Your own name and job title (31, 32) fill only when you have your own staff record. Nothing is ever guessed.

This is a map of the page, not the page. Exactly where each box sits on LHDN’s artwork is settled when you see the first real one — if a box lands badly, say so and it moves.

CP22A is not in this round, and there is still no way to mark a notice as filed — an item ages off the list about a month after its deadline. Both are separate gaps this plan leaves open.
