THE SITUATION TODAY

On the Rewards access page, the "Search users" box only filters the people who already have Rewards access. When you click Add access, the "Existing login account" box is one long alphabetical list of every login in the system, with no way to type a name and nothing to show which company, business unit or job each person belongs to.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- In Add access, replace the long list with a search box. Type part of a name, an employee number or an email and matching people appear as you type.

- Each match shows the person's employee number, job title, company and business unit, taken from Ops › People › People Management, so you can tell two people with similar names apart.

- Only active employees appear. People who have resigned past their last day, been terminated or had their login suspended are not offered.

- People who are active employees but have no login yet are shown greyed out with "No login yet", so you know to ask HR to invite them first.

- The "Search users" box above the assignments list will also match employee numbers, not just names and emails.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

You find the right person in a few keystrokes and can see at a glance that it is the right person in the right company and unit.

GOOD TO KNOW

- Rewards access is given to a login, not directly to an employee record, so a person must have been invited to AIHR before they can be given a Rewards role. This is deliberate: the system never creates or links logins automatically.

- Who is allowed to give access does not change, and nothing about existing access changes.
