The question you asked us — answered from the calculation itself

You asked whether “Saturday not applicable” and “Saturday Off Day” mean the same thing. They do not, and the difference is pay.

Saturday not applicable makes it a rest day — working it is paid at rest-day rates, up to 2×  ·  Saturday Off Day makes it a non-working day that is not a rest day — working it is ordinary overtime, 1.5×

Both look identical on a roster. They pay differently. That is why these cannot share one group.
