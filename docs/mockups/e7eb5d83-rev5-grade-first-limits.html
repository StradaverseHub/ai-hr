What a sub-claim limit does, and one thing on your mileage we should fix while we are here

Medical RM1,500 a year, Outpatient RM1,000 of it

Somebody on GRADE 12 claims RM600 of Outpatient in March.
Medical used RM600 of RM1,500 — RM900 left
Outpatient used RM600 of RM1,000 — RM400 left
They can still claim RM900 of Inpatient, but only RM400 more of Outpatient. Both pots are checked when they press submit, and a claim that would break either one is refused there and then.
The part where a sub-claim already comes out of the parent pot works today — it is the sub-claim’s own limit that has never existed.
Your mileage rate is being held in an old place, and it is one click from being wiped.
Every mileage claim in your company is currently priced at RM0.70 per kilometre from a setting attached to the old Claim Limit row. The proper bands you set up — RM1.00 / RM0.80 / RM0.60 — are saved against GRADE 12 (PERMANENT) only, and nobody is on that grade, so nobody is getting them. Re-saving the Mileage limit on the current screen throws the RM0.70 away, and mileage claiming stops for everyone.
What we will do about it. Saving limits will never touch a rate again. And we will put your RM0.70 per kilometre in as the company-wide mileage rate, so everyone is priced the way they already are today, while GRADE 12 keeps its own RM1.00 / RM0.80 / RM0.60 bands. Nobody’s claim changes by a cent — it just stops depending on an old setting.
