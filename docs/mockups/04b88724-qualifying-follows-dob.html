Why an 18-plus child is not set to zero — from the page you sent us

HASiL PCB specification 2026, page 26: “A relief of RM2,000 per child is allowed if the child is unmarried, 18 years of age and above, and is receiving full-time education.”

Page 27 — where a child falls within these conditions, the employee is treated as havingChildrenRelief
Child over 18 in full-time diploma-level study or above, in Malaysia4RM8,000
Child over 18 in full-time degree-level study or above, outside Malaysia4RM8,000
Disabled child certified by the Department of Social Welfare4RM8,000
Disabled child in diploma-level study or above8RM16,000

Setting every 18-plus child to zero would remove all four of these. A parent with one disabled child in university would lose RM16,000 of relief and be taxed on it every month.
