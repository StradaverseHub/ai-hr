The one place that stays silent is the transaction screen itself — which is the screen you look at when you are keying the transaction.

The same two words appear on the transaction history list and in its download, so the two screens cannot disagree about the same line — and the history list can be filtered down to just the transactions that need reprocessing.

4Deliberately not drawn — these stay in words

  • Processing and Failed are not being added. A recalculation finishes while you wait — all fifty-four that have ever run on your system completed, and none failed. Drawing a status nobody could ever see on screen would be promising a screen that does not exist.
  • Reprocessed is not a separate status. Once the recalculation has taken the line in, the line is in the latest payslip — which is exactly what Processed means. A second word for the same fact would be one more thing to interpret, not one more thing you know.
  • Salary and standing monthly items are not flagged. This round covers one-off transactions only. If you change someone's salary or a fixed allowance, no line turns amber and no band appears — you tick that person on the Reprocess screen yourself, exactly as you do today.
  • The band can appear with every line still reading Processed. That happens when the payslip went stale for a reason that is not a transaction — attendance being recalculated, for instance. It is correct: the band is about the employee's payslip, the badges are about their transactions.
