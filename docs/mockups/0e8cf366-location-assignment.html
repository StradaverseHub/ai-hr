2The other card is a different thing, and we recommend keeping it

Location assignment — where they may CLOCK
  • A circle on a map with a radius.
  • Several at once. Add and remove freely.
  • Decides one thing: is this clock-in accepted.
Location — where they WORK
  • One at a time, with an effective date, kept as history.
  • Carries the state and the public holiday calendar they follow.
  • Moves payroll and overtime history when it changes.
If we merged them, an employee would lose the dated work location that decides their public holidays. We propose renaming the clocking card to Location assignment, moving it above the other, and removing the three things that make them look related — then they read as two different questions instead of two versions of one.
