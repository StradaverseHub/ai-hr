Why the total moves: the form's own wording

Box C1  “Total monthly gross remuneration and additional remuneration including allowances / perquisites / gifts / benefits that are SUBJECT TO TAX”

Box C2  “Total allowances / perquisites / gifts / benefits that are EXEMPT FROM TAX”

The tax-exempt amounts are never inside C1. Today we subtract them from C1 anyway. Only the part of a box that goes above its yearly exemption limit is taxable — and because C1 holds only taxable pay, that excess is missing from C1 and has to be added.

Your example 1  — C2(i) keyed 3,600, yearly exemption 6,000

Today

Gross remuneration (C1)60,000.00
Less tax-exempt allowances (C2)− 3,600.00
Less EPF and approved funds (C3)− 4,000.00
Accumulated Net Remuneration52,400.00

Understated by 3,600.00

After this change

Gross remuneration (C1)60,000.00
Plus taxable excess over exemption (C2)+ 0.00
Less EPF and approved funds (C3)− 4,000.00
Accumulated Net Remuneration56,000.00
Tax-exempt, so not taxed at all3,600.00
Exemption still unused2,400.00

Matches your figure: 56,000.00

For the two boxes with no published limit the screen will say no limit is published rather than printing 0.00, because a zero would read as a figure we had checked. The exemption limits are shown and never enforced — the form is captured as keyed, exactly as it is today; nothing you type is altered or refused.
