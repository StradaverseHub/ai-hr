What the picture does not show

When you process an extra payment before the monthly salary, the system works the tax out against the person's normal monthly salary taken from their salary record, and then re-works the whole month once the Monthly payroll is run — so the month's totals land where they would have if you had processed in the conventional order.

That re-working is the large part of this item and it has no honest picture, so it stays in words rather than being drawn as though it were a screen.
