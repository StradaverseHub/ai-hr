Why the two screens cannot drift apart

They are not two copies — they are two doors onto one record. The Personnel screen and this one would call the same underlying function to read and to save, so a change made in either place is the same change, recorded once, with the same history. Keeping them "synchronised" is not something that has to be maintained; it is what happens when there is only one place the information lives.
Personnel Management keeps its copy. You asked for a second entry point, not for the first one to be taken away — an HR administrator working through an employee's record should still find the schedule where it is today. What goes away is being sent there from Attendance.
