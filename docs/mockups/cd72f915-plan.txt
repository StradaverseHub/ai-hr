THE SITUATION TODAY

The launch check you asked for already exists, but it is invisible until the last moment. When the BU CEO presses Submit, AIHR refuses unless every seat on the route has at least one person with an active login: BU CEO, BG CEO / authorised proxy, Group Rewards, CTRO, Executive Chairman and Payroll handoff. The same check runs again at every approval step. So a review cannot stall at a stage with nobody assigned.

What is missing: nobody can see in advance which roles are missing, and only the last Group Rewards person is protected from removal.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- Each unit review gets a coverage status on the Rewards access page: every required role listed as assigned (with names) or Missing, and PBP shown as optional.

- The same "roles missing" badge appears on the review's row in Rewards configuration.

- When the BU CEO tries to submit with roles missing, the message names the missing roles instead of a general error.

- Removing the last active person in any required role is refused, as it is today for Group Rewards. To change the person, use Edit and replace them in one step.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

Gaps are visible and fixed while the review is being set up, not discovered when the BU CEO tries to submit.

GOOD TO KNOW

- A person whose login is disabled, suspended or past their last working day does not count as coverage. This is already the rule and stays.

- A brand-new review starts with only its creator as Group Rewards, so it will show the other roles as missing until access is added. That is expected.
