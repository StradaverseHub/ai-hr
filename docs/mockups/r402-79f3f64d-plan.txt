FIRST, A CORRECTION

The system labels CP22A "tax clearance on cessation". That label is ours, and it is wrong. On LHDN's own forms, CP22A is the notice a private-sector employer sends when someone stops working. CP22B is the same notice for government agencies only ("Agensi Kerajaan" is printed in its title). So CP22B is not a tax-clearance form, and a private company never files it. We will fix the label and build CP22A properly.

THE SITUATION TODAY

CP39 is already drawn to LHDN's CP39 layout, the same layout as your sample. CP22 is filled in on LHDN's own page. Form E / CP8D has a figures screen. But each one opens differently, there is no payroll cycle choice, and CP21 and CP22A are not built. We also found two faults on CP39: the employer number can print "E" twice, and identity card numbers print with dashes where LHDN's own instructions write digits only.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- Monthly form (CP39): Open Form, choose Company, Year, Month and Payroll Cycle, press Continue, see the full form, then download it. This is the same path as EA, Borang A and Borang 8A. Select All is ticked when the screen opens.

- Yearly form (Form E / CP8D): choose Company and Year only, then preview and download. A month or a cycle means nothing on a yearly return, so they are not asked for.

- Forms about one person (CP22 for a new employee, CP22A for someone leaving, CP21 for someone leaving Malaysia): choose the Company, then pick the employee from a list of people who recently joined or are about to leave, with a name search, then preview and download. CP21 lists all current staff, because the system does not know who is leaving the country.

- If there is nothing to report, you stay on the selection screen with a clear message, and no blank form is produced.

- CP39: the employer number prints once, and identity card numbers print as digits only, as LHDN's instructions show.

- New: CP22A, filled in on LHDN's own 2023 form. It covers resignation, retirement and death, and fills in the year's pay so far, tax deducted, zakat and EPF from processed payroll.

- New: CP21, filled in on LHDN's own 2025 form. You type the departure details, and the rest comes from the staff and payroll records.

- The preview shows the actual PDF across the full width of the screen at its true shape. The download is the very same file.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

All LHDN forms open the same way. Each one asks only for what that form needs, and you always see the exact document before you download it.
