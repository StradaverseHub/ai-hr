The two settings answer different questions

Why both exist, and when you can skip the second one entirely.

Set on the Leave Type

May this employee take it at all?

A yes or no for the whole leave type. Fail it and the type is not offered and an application is refused.
  • Gender, marital status
  • Minimum service
  • Probation or confirmed
  • Company, department, grade and the rest
→
Set on the Entitlement Table

Which day table do they get?

Not a yes or no - a CHOICE between tables. Your Annual Leave has sixteen of them, and something has to pick one.
  • Grade 12-15 · Grade 16-19 · Grade H
  • Permanent · Contract · Retiree
  • Part Timer · Special
  • …sixteen in total
"Yes, they may take Annual Leave" plus "their days come from the Grade 16-19 Permanent table". The first cannot express the second, which is the whole reason both exist.
