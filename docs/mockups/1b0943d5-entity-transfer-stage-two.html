The safeguard, and it matters as much as the feature. Before a month with a transfer is committed, the covered periods must tile the whole month — no gap, no overlap — and the halves must add to the full figure: 2,129.03 + 4,470.97 = 6,600.00. If they do not, the run stops and says so instead of quietly paying less. Today's shortfall was noticed only because the run's total happened to drop by exactly the missing amount.
Why this is not just a calculation change. A payroll run belongs to one company and only includes people who belong to that company today — August has eight separate runs, one per company. Employee 100814 is at ISEDECOR now, so Fiamma's run never sees them, however correct the history is. And a payslip has nowhere to record which dates it covers, so neither half can state its own period. Both have to change before two payslips are possible.
