A box holding five months of the year, printed as if it were twelve, is worse than a box we leave alone. The employee files their tax return from this form. An empty box tells them nothing; a box with a confident, wrong number tells them something false, and they act on it.

So the mapping is worth building, and switching it on for a past year is not. We would put the setting back now and have it take effect from a year where we have the detail for every month, rather than produce a 2026 form that is part-itemised and part-guessed.

One more thing to settle before we build. The list of EA lines we can currently choose from has nine entries, and the EA form has twenty-six boxes — with no entry at all for director's fee, which we suspect is the case you have in mind. Widening that list is part of this work, and we would rather agree which boxes matter to you than guess at twenty-six.
