But if we clear the generated entitlement and keep the leave people took, those employees are left having taken leave against a balance that no longer exists, so their balance reads negative. Clearing the taken leave instead would erase leave they genuinely took. There is no way to wipe the balances that satisfies both instructions, so we are telling you rather than quietly picking one.
