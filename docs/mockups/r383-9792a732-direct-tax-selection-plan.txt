THE SITUATION TODAY

Correction to our earlier proposal: the separate declaration card and approval-reference requirement were our app design, not a government-mandated screen or reference format. Income Tax Resident already offers REP and Knowledge Worker, but payroll can still demand a separate declaration. Position C-Suites is not yet available in that selection.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- Make Income Tax Resident the single selection that determines the tax calculation: Normal Resident, Non-Resident, Returning Expert Program (REP), Knowledge Worker (IRDA), or Position C-Suites.
- Remove the Payroll Incentive Declarations card and its processing prerequisite. Saving an incentive category will record HR's decision that the employee meets that scheme's conditions for the employer shown, without another declaration or approval-reference entry.
- Keep the employer clear beside the selection. A transfer will not carry an employer-specific incentive automatically into another employer's payroll, and permissions to view or edit employee records will stay the same.
- Use the selected category consistently in payroll previews, processing, reprocessing and calculation explanations. Show the matching formula and identify a specific incompatible citizenship or tax-residency record if one needs correction.
- Preserve existing REP and Knowledge Worker configurations during the changeover. Reconcile conflicting older settings before switching calculations, so an employee is not silently moved to normal taxation. Retain saved payslips, calculation histories and earlier approval records.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

HR will choose the applicable category in Bank & Statutory and save once. Payroll will use that selection directly, without asking for the removed declaration card. Calculation details will identify the scheme actually used for that saved payroll result.

GOOD TO KNOW

- Selecting an incentive category records HR's eligibility decision; it does not obtain government approval. HR remains responsible for checking the individual and employer conditions and the applicable approval period.
- Previously saved payroll will keep its original calculation details. Changing the selection affects new calculations and an explicitly requested reprocessing attempt.
- For transferred employment, access to an older employer's payroll context still requires the existing employee-record permissions and authority for both employers.
