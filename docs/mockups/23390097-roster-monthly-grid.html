WFO-HQ-FWH Coloured = rostered. Somebody set that shift on that date, and it is one of the 315 rostered days already saved.

WFO-HQ-FWH Grey = the person's usual shift filling a date nothing was rostered on. The whole month reads as covered without rostering every day by hand.

OFF vs RD. RD is a rest day that was rostered. OFF is a day the usual shift does not work at all, so nothing is scheduled. Saturday and Sunday columns are shaded, as on your own screen.

A0005 shows the rest-day warning. Seven days in a row with a shift and no rest day — the EA 1955 s.59 flag the roster already carries, now checked across the whole month.

A0008's month is empty because no usual shift is set for her. The Attendance Policy screen already lists exactly who those people are, and setting it there fills this in.

Edit opens that one person's month, where you tick several dates and set them all to one shift, with a confirmation before it saves. A single cell can still be changed by clicking it.

09/08 is today. Dates today or earlier can only be changed by an HR Administrator, and only after confirming. From 10/08 onwards a supervisor can change them too.

Export downloads exactly what is on screen. Every change — one cell, one row, or a whole upload — is filed in the Audit Trail under Attendance with the shift before and the shift after.

Seven days, never a month. Prev / next move a week at a time, so preparing August means fourteen separate visits to this screen.

The cell prints the shift's whole name, not its code — which is why MB28's 48-character name has to be cut off mid-word.

No Business Group, Department or Employee filter, and no Employee No, Department or Occupation column to sort or read by.

A dash means "nothing rostered on this date" — even when the person has a usual shift. A0001's week is covered by his usual shift and the grid cannot say so; that is what the grey cells in card 1 fix.

No weekend shading, no Edit link, no upload, no export.

The click-to-assign cell stays. Picking an assignment and clicking a cell works exactly as it does today and is not being rebuilt.

What is already underneath, and is not changing. 315 rostered days are saved, and every attendance, lateness and overtime figure is already measured against whatever the roster says for that date. Your 269 attendance days keep working exactly as they do now, and changing a roster date never goes back and re-does an attendance day that was already recorded.

3What this sketch deliberately does not show

  • The Upload Shift Roster preview and the template file. The plan promises both — you will see how many employees and days were read, how many are valid, how many are duplicated, and every rejected line with its reason, before anything is saved. They are separate screens and this sketch is the grid; drawing a preview we have not laid out yet would be promising a shape rather than a behaviour.
  • The Edit panel, the confirmation prompts, and the Audit Trail rows. Described in the plan, not drawn here.
  • Any shift code except MB28 and WFO-HQ-FWH. Those two are yours — MB28 is live on your data and WFO-HQ-FWH is the code in your own screenshot. The rest of your 9 live shifts are counted, not invented, and only MB28's hours are printed in the legend because those are the ones we measured. The built legend prints every code with its own start and end time.
One thing in the plan has no honest picture. The upload file drawn here is the month grid itself — download it, fill it in, send it back. The plan also says that if you would rather upload a plain list instead — one line per employee per date with a shift code — you should reject the plan and say so. A choice cannot be drawn, so it stays in the words.
