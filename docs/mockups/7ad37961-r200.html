Draft → Submitted → Approved or Rejected → Cancelled. Once approved, the amounts are picked up by the monthly tax calculation from the approved deduction month onward. Nothing already approved is ever overwritten — a change is a new version and the earlier one stays readable.
Kept in words on purpose, not drawn:
  • The exact ringgit limits for every one of C1 to C17 are transcribed from the reporter's uploaded LHDN TP1 (2026) document. Where a figure or a frequency rule cannot be read off that document with certainty, the item is still captured and still printed, but with no blocking limit — we do not guess a legal number.
  • C15 and C7 carry frequency rules (once every so many years) rather than a plain annual limit. Those are recorded and shown as guidance next to the item; enforcing them across years is confirmed against the document before it is switched on.
  • The downloadable form follows the official layout and carries every entered figure. Whether it may be used as a filing document rather than an internal record is the company's call, not a system claim.
