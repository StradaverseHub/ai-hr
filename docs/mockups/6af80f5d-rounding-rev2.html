Yesterday we told you the Balance would round too, to 6.00. That was wrong and we are correcting it before building. The Balance is not a display figure — it is the employee’s actual remaining days, read straight from the record of what was credited and taken. Rounding it would make the screen disagree with the record, and the employee would be told they have a number of days that nobody ever gave them.

So Earned to Date rounds, and the Balance stays exact. They can differ by the rounding — which is honest, because they are two different things: one is how much has been earned so far, the other is what is actually left after everything.
