Payroll Settings — Payroll Cycles, Pay Schedule, Payroll Policies

Today's screens, and the proposed company-first redesign. Nothing that is calculated changes — this is navigation and layout only.

Company names below are the ones you used in your request. The cycle names shown (Monthly, Bonus, Off-cycle, Adjustment, Advance Salary, Mid-Month) are the real default set every company starts with. Saved numbers such as cut-off day and pay day are shown as a dash, because the real screen fills them from each company's own saved settings — we have not made figures up.
