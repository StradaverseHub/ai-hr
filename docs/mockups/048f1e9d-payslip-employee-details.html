What we are NOT doing, and why

We are not printing “not recorded” anywhere. A blank on a payslip is quieter than a payslip that announces a missing tax number to the employee. If you want those gaps chased, that is a report, not a line on the payslip — tell us and we will build it separately.

We are not removing any of the six fields. You listed them; they stay. The only change to what is printed is that a lone last number stops sharing its row with a hole.

We are not redesigning the rest of the payslip. Earnings, deductions and the rest already use the heading-bar-over-ruled-grid shape. This brings the one block that was left out into line with them, which is the smallest change that answers “more modern” without us guessing at a new look.

If this is not what you meant, reject it and sketch over this picture — that is quicker for both of us than another round of words.
