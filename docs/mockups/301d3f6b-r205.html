Special Leave: one pool of ten days, shared by six leave types

Item 301d3f6b · what is already true, what is not, and what changes

Where we got it wrong. The plan you rejected said we would “complete the Special Leave setup so the pool is worth ten days rather than nine” without saying how. That left room for exactly the outcome you are warning against — topping each of the six types up to ten days of its own. That was loose wording on our side, and this page closes it.

Where the system is already right. Any day taken on any of the six already counts against the same ten-day Special Leave allowance, and the eleventh day is refused whichever of the six it is asked for — both when leave is applied for and again when it is approved. Nobody can reach sixty days. What is wrong is the figure the employee reads, not the entitlement they are given.

Where the days themselves live

Today the days sit partly in the individual types’ own names, adding up to 9.00 between the six — which is both why the card opens one day short and why it can look as though each type has an allowance of its own. They should all sit in one Special Leave balance of 10.00.

What we will actually do

  • Put all ten days in one Special Leave balance, and set each of the six types to draw on that balance instead of holding days of its own. That is what makes it literally one pool.
  • Move the days those six types are holding today into that single balance, so nothing is stranded and nobody loses a day in the move.
  • Correct the balance card so it follows that link. Today it only counts days a type holds in its own name.
  • Check the finished figures against your example above on the live system, and report what we found either way.

Order matters, and this is why the display fix is not optional. If the setting change went in on its own, the card would count six types each holding nothing and show 0.00 — worse than the 9.00 it shows now. The display fix ships first; the setting change follows in the same round.

What does not change

  • All six types keep their own names on the apply screen and in leave history. An employee still picks the right reason for their leave.
  • The ten-day limit, the maximum per application, and who may see what are all untouched.
  • Leave already taken this year keeps counting against the pool exactly as it does now.
