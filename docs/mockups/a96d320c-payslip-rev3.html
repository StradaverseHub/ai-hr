Two things we want to flag before you approve

We are NOT removing zero statutory rows. You asked for excessive zero-value rows to go. For ordinary pay items we agree and they are already gone. But EPF, SOCSO and EIS at 0.00 is the payslip telling the employee that nothing was contributed this month — delete the row and the document goes silent about a statutory figure. We would rather show 0.00 than say nothing. Tell us if you disagree and we will change it.

We are not building the four-across grid literally. Your suggested structure put Earnings, Deductions, Employer Contributions and Other side by side in one row. On A4 that gives each column about 32mm, and a name like “Construction Project Management Allowance” would wrap to four lines — which is the clipping problem you raised with us before. Two across, in two bands, keeps the pairing you asked for and keeps the names readable.
