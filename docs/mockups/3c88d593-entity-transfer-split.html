Why this is bigger than payroll. The system stores a person's company as a single current value, with no history — so payroll is not ignoring the transfer date, it has nothing to look up. It already keeps dated history for position, department, cost centre, location and business unit, and employee 100814 already has two such rows either side of the transfer. Company is the one thing missing from that history, which is why attendance and leave have the same blind spot. Proration follows each company's own configured method — the 10 and 21 day figures above are illustrative, not a hard-coded calendar rule.
