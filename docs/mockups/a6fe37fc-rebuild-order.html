You asked for the legacy Leave Configuration to be removed and rebuilt. This is how we would do it, and what stays untouched while we do.

The order nothing is deleted until the replacement is proven
1

Prove the safety net

Build the check first: every approval rule, resolved for every real employee, before and after. Nothing else starts until this runs.

2

Agree the structure

We show you the new module’s shape and screens. You approve it before a screen is built. Your own rule.

3

Build alongside

The new screens go in beside the old ones. At every point you have something that works.

4

Move and verify

Your configuration moves across, and the check from step 1 must come back identical.

5

Remove the old

Only now. Old screens, old routes and the code behind them are deleted for real — not hidden.

Step 5 is the one you asked for, and it happens. Steps 1 to 4 are what stop it costing you anything.
