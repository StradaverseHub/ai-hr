How non-cash benefits affect this calculation

Taxable BIK and living accommodation are included in normal remuneration for PCB. These benefits do not increase cash gross salary. The resulting PCB deduction is reflected in the employee's saved net pay.

Pay items marked not subject to PCB remain excluded. Applicable exemptions are recorded with the saved benefit breakdown.
