On a phone
  • The calendar sits on top; tapping a date scrolls to the day panel underneath it.
  • Submit stays pinned to the bottom of the screen, with the hours and the date beside it.
  • Every colour also carries words, so the calendar still reads correctly to someone who cannot tell the shades apart.
  • The same layout reflows at 1440, 1024, 768 and 375 pixels wide.
Deliberately not drawn
  • No OT 1 and OT 2 pair, and no 2100- / 0300+ helper — this plan does not add them.
  • No Draft or Resubmit dot, and no Save as draft button — those states do not exist.
  • No bulk mode and no “upcoming this month” list.
  • Nothing is said about what a rest-day or holiday hour is paid at: the screen does not work that out.
