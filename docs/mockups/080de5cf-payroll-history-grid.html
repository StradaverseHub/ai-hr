2Why a pay item can appear twice

Your reference does this too. In your screenshot Gross appears four times — once each for End-month, Bonus, Advance Processing and OT-Leave — and Employee EPF appears twice.
So do we. A month can hold more than one payroll run, and a bonus run and an end-month run are genuinely different lines. Splitting them is what makes the row totals reconcile against the runs.
One column from your screenshot will not appear. Your old system stores a Transaction ID against each line — WEEK 2, WEEK 4. We do not record a week against a payroll run, so we are not going to draw a column we cannot fill. Everything else in your screenshot maps onto something we already store.

3What this replaces, and what it does not

Today's History stays. One line per payslip with the headline figures, and you can open a line to see what it was made of. It answers “show me the payslips”, and some people use it exactly that way.
The grid arrives beside it. It answers “show me one person's year”. Same stored figures, turned on their side — nothing is recalculated and no payroll run is touched.
