One thing this screen does that a plain list would lose. Your companies are not a flat list — they form a group structure, with parent companies above subsidiaries, and that tree is what several other screens read to decide who can see whom. The listing will show the code, name, registration number and status you asked for, and keep a way to see and edit the parent relationship. Flattening it into rows alone would quietly break the group hierarchy.
