Why this is one job and not three

What we found when we opened the four screens.

Uses the shared collapsible panel. This is the real model.

Loans this request
Plain dropdown today — and it lives under Admin, not Payroll › Transaction.

You asked for Zakat and Loans to “match Fixed Transaction and Individual Transaction”. Those two do not currently match each other — they are two different components. Rather than copy either one twice more, we point all four at the one panel that already exists, so the next screen is a one-line change instead of a fifth copy.
