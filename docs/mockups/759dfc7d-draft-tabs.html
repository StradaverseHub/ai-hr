What is actually left, and why it is queued

The remaining work is the layout: moving the draft from a stepped form onto those tabs. Those tab screens are being rebuilt right now by another request you approved — one Save button per tab, with a change summary and an unsaved-changes prompt. Two builds rewriting the same screens at the same time is how work gets silently lost, so this one follows it rather than running beside it.

A draft keeps everything that makes it a draft: save at any point without filling everything in, nothing validated until you activate, and the screen keeps telling you what is still outstanding.
