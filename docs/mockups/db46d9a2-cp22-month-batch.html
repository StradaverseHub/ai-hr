Said in words, because a picture cannot hedge

A correction we owe you

When we planned the CP22 form we wrote that filing one becomes “downloading it, typing up to seven remaining boxes, and signing”. That is no longer a route LHDN accepts. Since 1 September 2024 the notification must go through e-CP22 on the MyTax portal; posted and hand-delivered forms are not accepted, and a missed notification carries RM200 to RM20,000 or up to six months’ imprisonment.

So we are deliberately not using your reference’s label Download For Submission anywhere on this screen. The button says what it does — it downloads a file — and the line beside it says what the file is for.

The revision we ship on

Your reference shows CP22 [Pin. 1/2021]. The copy you sent us earlier — the one we bundled and shipped, and the one drawn here — is [Pin. 1/2020]. We could not establish which revision LHDN publishes today and we will not quietly swap the artwork of a statutory form on a guess. This work needs nothing from you to go ahead; send us the 1/2021 page whenever you have it and we switch it in a short follow-up, with no other change.

Not drawn, because it is not being built

  • No preview pane inside our screen. Your reference has a View button that shows the forms in the page; that viewer is not in this work. The file we hand you is already unlocked and typeable, so the copy you open on your own machine is the preview. If having it inside our screen matters to you, reject this plan and say so.
  • CP22A, the leaver notice, is not in this pass. It follows the same shape and is the natural next piece.
  • A whole month, not a free date range. A month is what you described, so a month is what we will build. If you would rather have any date to any date, reject this plan and say so.
  • No new required fields. The branch box is optional and the tax reference is not something you pick — we hold one employer number per company and fill it from the company you selected. If it is missing, the amber band tells you before you download.
