This is the section — and for 30 people on any unrostered day, it is the only schedule there is.
What removing it now would do. Those 30 employees would have no start time to be late against, no end time to leave early against, and no working-days list to say whether a date is a working day at all. Lateness and Early Out would go blank, and every unrostered day would stop classifying. The Lateness, Early Out & Overtime Settings cannot fill the gap — they shape overtime and deductions, they do not say when the day starts.
  1. Assign a shift to every employee — a default shift, or roster them. This is the real prerequisite.
  2. Then we remove the section, and it is a safe deletion rather than a silent one.
