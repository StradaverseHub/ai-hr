What the four choices mean for pay.
Full — an ordinary working day.
Half — a working day with half the normal hours, so overtime starts after half a day.
Rest — the statutory rest day. Working it pays half a day's wage for up to half a normal day, a full day's wage beyond that, and twice the hourly rate past a normal day.
Off — a non-working day that is not the rest day. Working it pays overtime at one and a half times the hourly rate.
