THE SITUATION TODAY

When an HR Admin opens an Audit Trail record, the details are shown as a raw list of technical field names and codes, for example "companyId: 98796fcd-...". For edits, changed values show as "old → new", but those values are often codes too. HR Admins and auditors cannot tell which company, employee or department was affected without looking it up elsewhere.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- Technical field names become plain labels. For example, companyId becomes Company, employmentId becomes Employee and departmentId becomes Department.

- Codes are replaced by the business name. Examples: "Company: Sample Holdings (SHB)", "Employee: Sample Employee / EMP0001", "Department: Human Resources". The same applies to positions, grades, business units, leave types, payroll runs, pay items, workflows and users.

- The detail view is organised into clear sections. Action shows who did what, and when. Record shows which record was affected. Changes is a table of each changed field with its Previous and Updated value, side by side.

- If a referenced record has been deleted, or the person viewing is not allowed to see it, the screen shows "Record not available" instead of a code.

- The original codes are still kept. A small "Technical details" link at the bottom of each record shows them for anyone who needs to trace a record exactly.

- Assignment transfers and other records that today show a block of text are laid out the same readable way.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

Anyone reviewing the audit trail can see who did what, to which company, employee or record, and what changed, in plain business terms. Nothing has to be decoded by hand.

GOOD TO KNOW

- This only changes what's shown on screen. Audit records themselves are never changed, and nothing is removed from them.

- Names are shown as they are today. If a department was renamed after the event, the current name is shown, with a note saying so. The system does not keep a history of names.

- Employee names are shown only to admins who are allowed to see that employee. Others see "Record not available".

- The Audit Trail Excel download stays exactly as it is for now, because other teams may rely on its layout. Adding readable columns to the download can be a follow-up request.

- Branch is already stored as readable text, so it already shows correctly.
