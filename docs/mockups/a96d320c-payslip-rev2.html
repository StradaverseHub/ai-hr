The one zero we want to keep

Allowance, Overtime, Bonus and every other pay item disappear when there is nothing in them, exactly as you asked. We would like to keep the four statutory lines — EPF, SOCSO, EIS and income tax — even at 0.00.

There, a zero is a statement rather than clutter: “income tax 0.00” tells the employee no tax was taken this month. If the line vanishes they cannot tell that apart from a payslip that simply did not mention tax — and it is the line they look at for their own tax return. Your request allows this exception; we are asking rather than deciding quietly.
