1Your rounding table — the third decimal, and what it is hiding

“it is still not displayed as 2 decimal place”

NOW — your live table, all three leave types

The only figures here that need a third decimal are 0.401 and 0.701. They are not really thousandths — they are “one notch above 0.400”, typed because a row stops just before its Round To.

And they leave two holes. A part-day of exactly 0.400 matches no row — it is above the first row’s end and below the second row’s start. So it is left alone instead of rounding to half a day. Same at exactly 0.700.
AFTER — two decimals, and no holes
FromRound toResult
0.000.400.00
0.400.700.50
0.701.001.00

From is filled in for you — it is always where the row above stopped, so the ranges cannot drift apart or leave a gap. You type only Round to and Result.

Same rounding, two decimals, nothing uncovered. The one change: a part-day of exactly 0.400 now becomes half a day, and exactly 0.700 becomes a full day — which is what the table was always meant to say.
