VERDICT: Partly there already   ·   SIZE: Moderate

YOUR REQUEST

You asked us to add Bank Name, Bank Account and Payroll Cycle to Employment Info - Payslip Display Fields, so that each works like the existing fields: you can switch it on or off, it follows the order you set, it shows the same way on the Payslip Review screen and in the downloaded payslip, its value comes from the employee's or the payroll's own record, and there is no separate fixed display for it.

THE SITUATION TODAY

Bank Name, Bank Account and Payroll Cycle already print on every payslip, together with Payment Method. They sit at the end of the Employee Profile block, on the review screen and in the downloaded payslip. But they are not in the setup list: they are always added after the fields you chose, so you cannot switch them off or move them. That is the separate fixed display you described.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- Add four fields to the setup list: Payment Method, Bank Name, Bank Account and Payroll Cycle. Payment Method is included because it prints in the same fixed spot today, and leaving it out would keep one line you still could not hide or move.

- Each one can be switched on or off and moved up or down (or dragged) like the existing fields, and prints where you place it, two per row, left then right.

- The fixed lines at the end of the block are removed, so each of these details prints once, only where your setup puts it.

- The review screen, the reviewer's downloaded copy and the employee's own downloaded payslip all follow the same setting.

- The values come from the same records as today: Bank Name and Bank Account from the employee's bank details, Payment Method from the employee's payroll details, and Payroll Cycle is the name of the payroll cycle the run was opened under (or the run type, for example "Regular", when the run was opened without one).

- No payslip changes on the day this goes live. Every company, whether it has saved its setup or not, finds the four new fields switched on and placed at the end of its list, which is exactly where they print today. From then on you can switch them off or move them, and Copy to Companies copies them like any other field.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

Every line in the Employment Info block is controlled from one setup screen, and nothing prints outside it.

GOOD TO KNOW

- The bank account number stays in full on the review screen and on both downloaded copies, as you asked for earlier.

- These details are read from the employee's current record each time a payslip is opened, as they are today. If an employee changes bank account, an older payslip opened later shows the new account. The system has never kept a record of which account each past payment went to, so that cannot be shown for past months; keeping that record from now on would be a separate request.

- The four labels will read Payment Method, Bank Name, Bank Account and Payroll Cycle, with capitals like the other labels in the block (today they read "Bank name" and so on). All six languages are covered.

- We're not touching how anything is calculated. This only changes what is shown and where.
