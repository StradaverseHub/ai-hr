THE SITUATION TODAY

HR can record non-cash benefits against an employee, but the payroll calculation currently leaves those items out before applying their PCB treatment. The PCB calculation detail also has no saved breakdown of taxable BIK and living accommodation. The reported Employee D calculation has not yet been independently checked against the installed payroll records.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- Include the entered monthly BIK and living accommodation values in normal taxable remuneration when the pay item is subject to PCB. Keep items marked not subject to PCB outside the tax calculation.
- Keep BIK and living accommodation separate from cash earnings. They may change the PCB deduction, but will never increase the cash salary paid to the employee.
- Show each benefit separately in the current-month and accumulated sections of PCB Calculation Detail, using the same saved information in the preview and download.
- Carry the applicable saved taxable benefits through the current tax year without counting them twice across payroll cycles or carrying the previous year's values into the next year.
- Keep the existing EA benefit classifications separate from cash gross salary, and identify incomplete or conflicting pay-item settings before recalculation.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

HR will be able to see how a non-cash benefit affected PCB and compare the saved calculation with the entered monthly benefit. Payroll will distinguish the employee's taxable remuneration from their cash earnings throughout calculation and reporting.

GOOD TO KNOW

- This change uses monthly benefit values entered by HR. It does not determine the value of a car, accommodation or other benefit, or introduce automatic annual allocation.
- Existing saved results will not be silently recalculated. An authorised recalculation is needed to replace a result, and the previous result remains in its history.
- If selected reprocessing finds no payable cash earnings, it will retain the saved result and show the existing skipped outcome. A non-cash benefit will not create a fresh deduction against no payable earnings.
