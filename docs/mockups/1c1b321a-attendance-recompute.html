5What this picture does not promise

Four things in the plan cannot be drawn honestly, so they are left in words here.

  • Which shift an older day is measured against. This screen re-measures a past day against the shift the person is on now — right when the shift itself was corrected, wrong when the person was moved between shifts. Your other request, the effective date on the Shift arrangement card, is what fixes that. It is a separate decision you have not made yet, so nothing on this picture depends on it.
  • The alternative behaviour for processed pay runs. The plan offers one course of action: flag the run. If you would rather the recompute refuse to touch any day inside an already-processed run and leave those days alone entirely, that is a different screen and it is deliberately not drawn — reject the plan and say so.
  • Days with no clocking at all. A day shows as Absent because no record exists for it. There is nothing to re-work where nothing was recorded, so those days will never appear in this table. Spotting and creating absences is a separate request.
  • The Audit Trail. Each run also appears in the Audit Trail under Attendance. That is an existing screen and is not redrawn here.
