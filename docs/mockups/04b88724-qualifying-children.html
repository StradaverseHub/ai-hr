Each child is judged on its own: a child under 18 counts as one, a disabled child as four, a disabled child in higher education as eight. These are the corrected figures already shipped for this item — RM8,000 and RM16,000, from page 27 of the 2026 specification you cited.

For 71 of your 75 employees the tax figure is not worked out from their children at all — it is a flat count entered by hand. Those will show Entered instead of Worked out, so you can see at a glance which is which. We will not dress a typed number up as a calculated one.

Moving those 71 across changes real PCB, so it stays a deliberate step. The Child Relief Review report — the one we kept when its button was removed — is the list you do it from.
