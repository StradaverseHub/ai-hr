VERDICT: Partly there already   ·   SIZE: Moderate

THE SITUATION TODAY

Most of these rules already apply automatically, whatever role a person has been given:

- Nobody sees their own reward line, not even an approver.

- People marked "EC confidential" are visible only to Group Rewards, CTRO and the Executive Chairman.

- People marked "BG confidential" are hidden from the BU CEO and the PBP.

- The PBP can read but cannot change or approve anything.

- Unit totals and budgets always include the hidden people.

The gap is how a person gets marked BG or EC confidential: today Group Rewards sets it by hand for each person during data verification or through the data template. Nothing works it out from the reporting lines in AIHR, so a missed marking means a BU CEO could see a BG CEO's direct report. The "What each Rewards role permits" panel also does not mention these rules.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- When a review is created or refreshed, AIHR marks people automatically from their reporting line in People: anyone reporting directly to the person in the BG CEO seat becomes "BG confidential", and anyone reporting directly to the person in the Executive Chairman seat becomes "EC confidential".

- Group Rewards can make a person more restricted than the automatic marking, but never less restricted. The data verification screen shows why each marking was set.

- If the BG CEO or Executive Chairman seat changes person, the markings are worked out again.

- The "What each Rewards role permits" panel gets a second part, "Confidentiality rules", listing the four rules above in plain words.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

The confidentiality rules no longer depend on someone remembering to tick a box for each sensitive person.

GOOD TO KNOW

- AIHR has no separate "EC direct report" flag on the employee record. Using the reporting line to the person in the Executive Chairman seat gives the same result and stays correct when reporting lines change. If someone should be EC confidential without reporting directly to the Executive Chairman, Group Rewards can still mark them by hand.

- Reporting lines must be kept up to date in People for this to work; people with no reporting line recorded stay as they are and Group Rewards marks them by hand.
