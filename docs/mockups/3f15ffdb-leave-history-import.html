4 · What this does not do

The single most important thing on this page.

No leave balance moves. An imported record is history — it appears in the employee's leave history with its original reference, dates, session and remark, and it is already approved so nobody has to re-apply. It does not take a single day off anyone's balance.
Why not. When you migrate a customer you also bring across their balance as it stands, and that figure already has the past leave removed from it. Deducting the imported records as well would take the same days off twice — from a number that gets paid out when someone leaves.

Loading balances is the other tool's job — the one renamed Leave Balance Import in section 1. One file of records here, one file of balances there, nothing counted twice.
