Why some columns already work. The five that respond are worked out from your entitlement setup as at the date you pick. The four that do not are read from a running total of the employee's whole leave record, which carries no date at all — so it cannot answer "as at when".

What a future date will mean. Entitlement already earned by that date is counted, as is leave already booked for days up to it. Nothing is forecast or estimated — only what is genuinely on record for a date on or before the one you choose.

Only what is shown changes. No entitlement rule, accrual, carry-forward or approval is altered, and nobody's actual balance moves. This is about answering "as at when" honestly on a screen that already asks the question.
