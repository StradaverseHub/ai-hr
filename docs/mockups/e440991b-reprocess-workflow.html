One click from the list. The same Reprocess Payroll button also sits at the top of the payroll run screen, next to Close Payroll — so you never scroll to find it. It appears on Processed runs only, and never on a closed one.

Reset Payroll is a different thing and stays where it is: it sends the whole run back to Draft. Reprocess recalculates only the people you pick and leaves everyone else's payslip untouched.
