The two amber pieces are the real work, and they are there for a reason. Today a formula may only divide by a fixed number, never by a live value. That is deliberate: an employee with zero working hours would divide by zero, produce an impossible figure and stop the whole payroll run. Your example divides by Working Days and Working Hours, so we need to allow it — safely. What we will build: if a divisor comes out as zero for someone, that employee's item pays nothing and they are named on the run for you to look at. The batch never breaks, and no one is silently paid a wrong number.
