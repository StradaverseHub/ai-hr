THE SITUATION TODAY

On the Rewards access page each Add access gives one person one role on one exact unit review. Group-level people (Group Rewards, CTRO, Executive Chairman, Payroll handoff) and BG CEOs have to be added again for every unit, every cycle, which means many repeated rounds of add, preview and save.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- In Add access, the review scope becomes a list where you tick as many unit reviews as you need, with a search box.

- Two shortcuts tick reviews for you: "All Draft reviews in a cycle" and "All Draft reviews in a company".

- The preview lists every assignment that will be added (and any that already exist), and one save adds all of them or none.

- New "Copy access from an earlier cycle": pick the earlier cycle and the new one. AIHR matches the unit reviews by company and business unit and proposes the same people in the same roles. People whose login is no longer active are listed and skipped. You review the list, untick anything you do not want, then save.

- When copying or adding, the BU CEO seat is pre-suggested from the business unit head recorded in People. A suggestion is marked "suggested" and only takes effect when Group Rewards ticks it.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

Setting up a new cycle takes a few saves instead of dozens, with a full preview before anything is granted.

GOOD TO KNOW

- Access is still recorded per unit review behind the scenes, so every existing rule keeps working: only Draft reviews can have their access changed, and each review keeps its own history.

- AIHR does not currently record business groups or who the BG CEO of each group is, so there is no "all companies in this BG" shortcut and no automatic BG CEO suggestion. Copying from the previous cycle covers most of that need. If you want business groups added to AIHR, raise it as a separate request.

- If the separate request for a second approval on access changes is approved, bulk and copied changes will go through that approval as one request.
