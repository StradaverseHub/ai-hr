THE SITUATION TODAY

On Payroll > Statutory Forms, the EA form and the PCB 2(II) form each appear twice - once for English and once for Bahasa Malaysia - so the Income Tax shelf shows four cards for two forms. The EA cards also carry a "Blank form and field register" button that you no longer need. When you open a form, the first step sits in a narrow box in the middle of the screen, and Preview opens the form in a separate browser tab instead of inside the system.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- Form C.P.8A (EA) becomes one card, and PCB 2(II) becomes one card, each with a single Open Form button.

- After Open Form, the first step asks for Company, Year and a new Version choice: English or Bahasa Malaysia. English is picked to start with.

- The Version you pick opens the existing form in that language - the same English and Bahasa Malaysia forms you use today, unchanged.

- The "Blank form and field register" button is removed from the EA card, with nothing put in its place. The EA forms themselves and everything they use stay exactly as they are.

- Preview now opens the form inside the system, across the full width of the screen, with the form name and the Version choice along the top so you can switch language without starting again. The form is scaled to fit the width, never stretched, so its proportions, borders and margins stay correct.

- The first step (Company, Year, Version) also uses the full screen width instead of a narrow box.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

Two cards instead of four on the Income Tax shelf, one obvious way into each form, and a larger, clearer view of the form before you download it.

GOOD TO KNOW

- We're not touching how anything is calculated, what data goes into the forms, the Selected Date, the authorised signatory, numbering, the PDF or the Download button. Only the list, the version choice and how the form is shown on screen change.

- Links you may have bookmarked to the old English or Bahasa Malaysia cards still work: they open the merged form with that language already chosen.

- On some phones and tablets a browser shows only the first page of a form inside a page, so the viewer also offers an "Open in new tab" link.

- If you would rather keep Preview opening in its own browser tab as it does today, and only want the cards merged, reject this plan and say so.
