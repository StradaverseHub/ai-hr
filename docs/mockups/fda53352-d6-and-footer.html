Why D.6 reads 4,000.00 — and what the “2” you saw actually is

APARTICULARS OF EMPLOYEE

8. Number of children qualified for tax relief      2   .

Box A.8 asks for a count, and 2 is correct there. The official form carries both boxes: A.8 the number of children, D.6 the ringgit. Their captions read almost the same, which is how the two got crossed. Two qualifying children at RM2,000 each gives the RM4,000 you asked for in D.6 — and where a child is weighted more heavily, for instance a disabled child in higher education, D.6 shows RM16,000 while A.8 still shows 1.
