VERDICT: Partly there already   ·   SIZE: Moderate

THE SITUATION TODAY

When HR saves a Termination with a future date, for example 20-Nov-2026, the system only schedules it. Until that date arrives, the employee still shows as Active, Last Working Date shows Not set, and the Employment tab says nothing will change until the date.

Payroll does not see the scheduled date either. If November payroll is processed before the 20th, it pays the full month and does not list the employee as a leaver. HR cannot check the prorated final salary before the employee leaves.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- As soon as a future Termination or Resignation is saved, the employee's status becomes Active - Serving Notice. This status already exists in the system; it just was not shown for scheduled terminations.

- The Employment tab shows the Last Working Date straight away, and the Resignation Date where one was entered, marked "Scheduled in Movement · takes effect 20-Nov-2026". The termination date stays exactly as HR entered it. It is never changed to today.

- Department, position and direct superior stay as they are until the date arrives. On the date, the employee becomes Resigned, as today.

- Payroll recognises the last working day immediately. November payroll, including the preview, prorates basic salary up to that day, along with any pay items set to be prorated. The employee is listed as leaving in that month, so the usual leaver reminders (such as the leave balance) appear. This is the same calculation the system already uses for employees who have left. The difference is that HR can now run and check it in advance.

- If HR changes the date, the Employment tab and payroll follow the latest saved date. The Movement tab still shows one Termination line with its change history, not a duplicate. If the scheduled Termination is deleted, the dates are cleared and payroll returns to the full month.

- If the month's payroll is already processed but not closed when the date is entered or changed, the existing "needs reprocessing" flag appears. Closed payroll, payslips and statutory records are never changed. The usual closed-payroll notice is shown instead.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

HR can record a resignation as soon as it is known, see Serving Notice and the last working day straight away, and process and check the final month's prorated salary before the employee leaves, without waiting for the date or entering the same information on several screens.

GOOD TO KNOW

- We're not touching how anything is calculated. EPF, SOCSO, EIS and PCB follow the prorated wages exactly as they already do for leavers.

- Leave encashment stays a suggested figure for HR to decide, as today. Leaver tax forms and the final settlement process are not changed by this request.

- This also applies to Resignation events entered with a future date, and to your company's own termination types.
