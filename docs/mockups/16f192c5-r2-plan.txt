THE SITUATION TODAY

Every automated email AIHR sends is plain text with no design: the account invitation, password reset, approval and workflow alerts, attendance copies, assessment reminders and the scheduled Employee Master report. Our first plan offered a shared logo, colour, header and footer plus editable wording. You asked for more: the freedom to overhaul the design completely, like a marketing (EDM) email. This plan does that.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- A new Email templates screen under System Settings lists every automated email: what it is, when it is sent, who receives it, and whether it uses the system design or yours.

- Each email opens in an Email designer. You build the email from blocks: header with logo, banner image, heading and text, image, button with your own link, two columns, divider, spacer, social links and footer. You can add, remove and rearrange blocks freely.

- Every block can be styled: background and text colours, font, size, alignment and spacing. You can upload your own images and logo.

- A design you build once can be saved and reused as the starting layout for the other emails, so all your emails share one look.

- Wording is edited in each of the six languages. A list of details you can insert is shown for each email, such as {first_name}, {company_name} or {organization_code}. A language you have not edited keeps the system wording.

- Each system email has one AIHR block that carries what the email exists for: for example the "Activate my account" button and its link, or a password reset link and its expiry. You can move that block and change its colours, but you cannot remove it, change its link or edit its wording, so the email always works and stays safe.

- Desktop and mobile preview with sample details, Send test to me, and Restore default.

- Every saved change records who made it and when, and earlier versions can be restored.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

AIHR emails that look like your own designed company emails, built and changed by HR without a system change, while every link and security message still works exactly as it must.

GOOD TO KNOW

- Designs are built from blocks rather than by pasting raw HTML code. Blocks are what make the emails display correctly in Outlook, Gmail and phone mail apps, and keep harmful code out. If you also need to paste in an HTML design made elsewhere, say so in your reply and we will plan it as a follow-up.

- Your own buttons and images must use secure (https) links.

- Letters keep their own templates in Letters.

- Only system administrators can change email designs.

- Nothing changes in who receives emails or when they are sent.
