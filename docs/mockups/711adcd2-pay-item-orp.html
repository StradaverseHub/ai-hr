Your example takes 200 off the overtime base to reach 2,800. We can do that — the tick box allows it — but Malaysian employment law works overtime out from the ordinary rate of pay based on wages, and taking deductions off that base lowers overtime pay. So no deduction is ticked by default, and the screen warns you when you tick one for overtime. We would rather it asked than did it quietly.
