2The two smaller points — what we propose, and why

“5+” and “16 days”

We propose keeping 5 – No limit and 16.00.

  • No limit is the wording the editor you type the table into already uses. Writing the same fact two ways on one screen is the exact confusion we have spent this month removing.
  • 16.00 rather than “16 days”: a leave type can be counted in hours, so the word “days” would be wrong on some rows. The two decimals are there so a half day shows as 16.50 and is never quietly rounded away.
If you would still rather see 5+ and 16 days, reject and say so.
