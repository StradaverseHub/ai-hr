THE SITUATION TODAY

The downloaded payslip already separates earnings, deductions and statutory employer contributions. However, it also prints Total Employer Cost, which includes remuneration as well as employer contributions. That broader total can make a Director Fee look like a contribution even though the contribution rows themselves contain statutory items.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- Place employee, employment and payment information in one centred, clearly defined Employment Info card. Move Payment Method, Bank Name and Bank Account into that card and remove the separate Payment Information section.
- Remove Total Employer Cost and the requested explanatory remark from the payslip. Show applicable employer-paid contribution items only, with labels that distinguish employer amounts from employee deductions.
- Keep Director Fee and other remuneration in Earnings. Verify any configured contribution items against their saved classification and processed amounts, rather than treating all company payments as contributions.
- Hide the Basic Salary row when the selected payroll record has no applicable amount or a zero amount. Retain genuine negative adjustments and other earnings.
- Rename the Year to Date column This Run to Current Month, retaining the same saved figures and Year to Date calculation.
- Apply the same information placement and contribution rows in the Individual Payslip preview and both admin and employee PDF downloads using this design. Preserve payment details, payroll figures and the existing employer-paid employee-share disclosures.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

A compact payslip with one clear information card and a contribution section that does not mix company payroll cost with statutory contributions. Director-only payslips will show the actual fee without an unnecessary zero Basic Salary row.

GOOD TO KNOW

- Current Month remains the amount on the selected payslip. If there are several payroll runs in one month, this change does not combine those runs into a monthly total.
- The related Payslip Information Setup request will control which employee fields are shown; this request controls their layout. Payment details remain available in the new card.
- Previously issued payroll amounts will not be recalculated. If reproduction identifies an actual calculation error, it will be reviewed as a separate correction before any payroll amounts are changed.
