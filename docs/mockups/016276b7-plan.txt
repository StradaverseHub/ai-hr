THE SITUATION TODAY

When HR presses Process for a company, the system checks every employee's income tax history first. If even one employee has an unresolved income tax issue, the whole company is marked Failed and nobody is processed. In your example, 2 employees out of 159 needed attention, so 157 employees with nothing wrong were stopped as well.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- When you press Process, employees with an income tax issue are set aside ("held for income tax review") and everyone else is processed as normal.

- The result screen will show three numbers for each company: processed, held for income tax review, and failed. If the only problem in a company is held employees, it is shown as Partly processed, not Failed.

- Under the summary, a new list names each held employee: employee number, name, the month with the problem, and the reason in plain words, with a link straight to Recalculate Income Tax Value for that company and month.

- The payroll run page will keep a clear notice ("2 employees held for income tax review - not yet in this payroll") until they are dealt with, so nobody forgets them.

- After HR fixes and applies the corrections in Recalculate Income Tax Value, a Process held employees button on the run page opens the existing Reprocess screen with those employees already selected. Only they are calculated and added to the same payroll run. The 157 already processed are not touched, recalculated or duplicated.

- If an employee is still not ready when you try again, they stay held and the reason is shown again.

- Their approved claims and loan instalments wait for them. They are not pushed to next month or marked as deducted while the employee is held.

- As today, the month cannot be closed while any employee in the run is still not processed. The close message will now say which employees are held for income tax review. If you decide an employee should not be paid in this run, the existing Remove employee action still works.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

One or two employees with an income tax problem no longer hold up the whole company. The others are paid on time, the held employees are clearly listed with the reason, and they can be added to the same payroll once their tax history is fixed.

GOOD TO KNOW

- We're not changing how income tax (PCB) is calculated, how history is checked, or what counts as a genuine issue. Missing tax information is still never treated as zero. Employees with real issues are still held; they're just no longer holding everyone else up.

- Approval, validation and finalisation stay exactly as they are today.

- This only covers income tax history issues. Other checks that stop a whole company today (for example an employee with no salary record) stay as they are. If you want those handled the same way, please raise it as a separate request.

- The draft payroll screen already shows the employees with income tax issues before you press Process. That stays, so you can still fix them first if you prefer.

- Each held employee is shown only to people who are already allowed to see that company's payroll. The tax details are only shown to people allowed to review income tax.
