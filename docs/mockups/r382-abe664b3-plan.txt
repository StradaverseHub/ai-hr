VERDICT: Partly there already   ·   SIZE: Large

THE SITUATION TODAY

The system already protects employees from a newly calculated payslip with no earnings and statutory deductions. For selected reprocessing, that protection currently stops the whole selection and asks HR to remove the employee. Skipping a calculation must instead keep the employee and their saved records in the run.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- Check each selected employee separately using the same pay rules that calculate their salary, allowances, transactions, attendance, leave and other earnings. An empty basic salary alone will not decide whether they have anything payable.
- Show Skipped – Nothing payable when there are no payable earnings for the period. Continue processing the other selected employees without asking HR to remove anyone.
- Preserve the skipped employee's existing saved payslip, run membership, transactions, reservations and payroll history. Do not create a replacement payslip or new statutory deductions for that attempt.
- Add Processing status and Remarks to the employee results. Show the full skip explanation in expandable details, together with who requested the attempt and when it happened.
- Show a separate Needs attention result for an employee whose required payroll information is incomplete, including a missing applicable tax-scheme approval. Continue eligible employees without applying another employee's settings or silently substituting a different tax category.
- Keep Remove employees from this run as a separate intentional action. Record skipped, successfully reprocessed and validation-failed attempts distinctly in the audit trail.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

HR can reprocess a mixed selection and see an honest result for each employee. A skipped employee remains on the run; their last saved result is explicitly labelled as retained, rather than presented as newly recalculated.

GOOD TO KNOW

- Nothing payable means no payable earnings after the period's pay rules, before statutory deductions. A positive earning reduced to no take-home pay by deductions is a different case and will not be silently skipped.
- A skipped or unsuccessful attempt does not make an outdated saved result current. Where the run already requires correction, that warning remains and HR must resolve it before finalising the run.
- This change does not rewrite historical payroll or invent missing tax-scheme approvals. The separate tax-scheme request shares the same employee-by-employee result handling.
