## Owner direction retained

Build for the anchor group's internal use, stabilize through real workflows, then productize. Prioritize correct multi-entity payroll, statutory compliance, attendance and claims. Payroll operations lead; terminal clock import bridges into native attendance rollout. Historical headcount/entity figures are sizing inputs, not current customer records.

Keep go-live gates explicit: verified statutory sources and accountable sign-off, cross-employer tax correctness, PDPA-compatible audit/erasure, safe configuration and real operational delivery. Derive legal rules from authoritative current sources rather than this proposal.

Extract shared workflow/configuration/role engines from proven module needs; keep country statutory truth governed by versioned packs and reviewed override paths. Effective-date facts that require retroactive correctness, and validate client-supplied attendance evidence server-side. RLS governs authorization, not sensor truth.

The original deferred-module labels and “current → target” inventory are historical. Later ADRs and owner decisions govern expanded performance, rewards, AI, mobile and authentication scope. Product plans are not proof of implementation, activation, customer acceptance or permission to process real data.
