========================================================

SOURCE: docs/forms/cp39-pin-2015-page2-specimen.png — image2.png inside "CP39 page 2 sample.docx" on

THE SHEET HEADER — one line above the table

    No. Majikan   E   <employer number on a dotted rule>                      Muka Surat   <n>

"No. Majikan" and "E" are bold; the page number sits at the far right with a rule under it. **The number
is the DOCUMENT's page number — the front sheet is page 1, so the first schedule sheet is 2.**

THE TABLE — every cell ruled, two-level headings

    ┌─────┬───────────────┬──────────────────────────┬──────────┬──────────┬─────────┬─────────────────────┬─────────────────────┐
    │     │               │                          │          │          │         │  BAGI PEKERJA ASING │ JUMLAH POTONGAN CUKAI│
    │ BIL │ NO. CUKAI     │ NAMA PENUH PEKERJA        │ NO. K/P  │ NO. K/P  │  NO.    ├──────────┬──────────┼──────────┬──────────┤
    │     │ PENDAPATAN    │ (SEPERTI DI KAD PENGENALAN│  LAMA    │  BARU    │ PEKERJA │ NO.      │   KOD    │ PCB (RM) │ CP38 (RM)│
    │     │               │  ATAU PASPORT)            │          │          │         │ PASPORT  │  NEGARA  │          │          │
    └─────┴───────────────┴──────────────────────────┴──────────┴──────────┴─────────┴──────────┴──────────┴──────────┴──────────┘

Two group headings span two columns each — BAGI PEKERJA ASING over (NO. PASPORT, KOD NEGARA), and
JUMLAH POTONGAN CUKAI over (PCB (RM), CP38 (RM)). Every other heading spans the full header height.
All headings are UPPERCASE and centred; the NAMA PENUH PEKERJA caption carries its parenthetical on the
second and third lines.

Row values: BIL and the two money columns right-aligned; everything else left-aligned. The new identity
card number prints WITH dashes (941128-03-5179). Empty is empty — a person with no passport gets an
empty cell, never a dash and never a zero (AGENTS 28).

THE FOOT

Bottom LEFT, small, outside the table:

    Borang CP39 boleh diperolehi di laman web : http://www.hasil.gov.my

Bottom RIGHT, a ruled box of two rows aligned to the two money columns:

    ┌──────────────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┐
    │      JUMLAH      │  <page pcb>  │  <page cp38> │
    ├──────────────────┼──────────────┴──────────────┤
    │   JUMLAH BESAR   │      <grand total>          │
    └──────────────────┴─────────────────────────────┘

JUMLAH is this SHEET's total, split across the two money columns. JUMLAH BESAR is the whole statement's
and spans both. **AMAUN (PCB + CP38) does NOT appear on this page** — it belongs to BUTIR-BUTIR
PEMBAYARAN on the front sheet, where r218 now prints it.
