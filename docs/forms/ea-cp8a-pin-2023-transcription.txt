=========================================================================

⚠️ THE REVISION IN THIS FILE'S OLD NAME WAS WRONG. READ THIS FIRST (r346).
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
This file used to be called `ea-cp8a-pin-2024-transcription.txt` and its first line said Pin. 2024.
There is no such revision. **LHDN publishes Pin. 2023**, and on 2026-09-26 it was still the current
EA at hasil.gov.my: `borang_ea__c.p.8a_-_pin._2023_.pdf` returns 206 `application/pdf`, while the
same path for 2024, 2025 and 2026 returns 404. The agency's own "EA & EC Guide Notes — Remuneration
for the Year 2024" accompany the **Pin. 2023** form: THE REVISION LABEL IS NOT THE YEAR, and reading
it as one is almost certainly how the error was made upstream.

THE SOURCE NOW, AND IT IS THE REAL ONE
--------------------------------------
`docs/forms/ea-cp8a-pin-2023-official-en.pdf` — fetched live from hasil.gov.my on 2026-09-26, sha256
`6b76638173b9d06a…`, 381,770 bytes, **byte-identical** to the reporter's attachment on item fda53352.
Vector text (5 Arial/Times faces, no image XObject), authored in Word 2016, `/Title` "Borang EA
(C.P.8A - Pin. 2023)". `docs/forms/ea-cp8a-pin-2023-official-bm.xlsx` is the Bahasa Malaysia sheet,
docProps `Company=LHDNM`. These are genuine LHDN artwork — the first this board has received.

STRUCTURALLY the traced form and the real one agree box for box: same sections, same numbering, same
BM captions. What differed was the WORDING of our English (26 captions were our paraphrase, corrected
in r346 to the agency's own) and the MONEY COLUMN, which the tracing put at 504.5 → 571.3 where the
agency's own cells put it at 482.3 → 556.6.

The rendered form is still OURS — drawn to the agency's arrangement, on our paper. It may declare
`agency-layout` and may NEVER declare `official`. Holding the agency's file for comparison does not
change what we print.

WHAT WE DREW FROM IT (r219, 2026-08-23)
--------------------------------------
`docs/forms/ea-cp8a-our-output-2026-08-23.png` is what `lib/ea-pdf.ts` produces after the redraw, from a
fully-populated fixture, kept beside the specimen so the two can be held up against each other without
running anything. Every coordinate in `EA_FORM` (lib/ea-pdf.ts) was MEASURED off the specimen PNG by
scanning it for rules — the banner box at 77.3/90.2 pt, the money column at 504.5 -> 572.2, the JUMLAH
double rule at 425.8/435.4, the officer box at 640.8 -> 730.6 — rather than eyeballed, which is the CP39
lesson. The one deliberate departure is the LEFT margin: the specimen sets its rules 16.8 pt from the
paper edge, inside the unprintable margin of most office printers, so everything left-anchored moved to
24 pt. The money column did not move.
