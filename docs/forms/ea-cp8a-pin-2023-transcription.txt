⚠️ THE REVISION IN THIS FILE'S OLD NAME WAS WRONG. READ THIS FIRST (r346).
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
This file used to be called `ea-cp8a-pin-2024-transcription.txt` and its first line said Pin. 2024.
There is no such revision. **LHDN publishes Pin. 2023**, and on 2026-09-26 it was still the current
EA at hasil.gov.my: `borang_ea__c.p.8a_-_pin._2023_.pdf` returns 206 `application/pdf`, while the

it as one is almost certainly how the error was made upstream.

WHAT WE DREW FROM IT (r219, 2026-08-23)
--------------------------------------
`docs/forms/ea-cp8a-our-output-2026-08-23.png` is what `lib/ea-pdf.ts` produces after the redraw, from a
fully-populated fixture, kept beside the specimen so the two can be held up against each other without
running anything. Every coordinate in `EA_FORM` (lib/ea-pdf.ts) was MEASURED off the specimen PNG by
scanning it for rules — the banner box at 77.3/90.2 pt, the money column at 504.5 -> 572.2, the JUMLAH
double rule at 425.8/435.4, the officer box at 640.8 -> 730.6 — rather than eyeballed, which is the CP39
lesson. The one deliberate departure is the LEFT margin: the specimen sets its rules 16.8 pt from the
paper edge, inside the unprintable margin of most office printers, so everything left-anchored moved to
24 pt. The money column did not move.

  ⚠️ CORRECTION (r372, 2026-09-30) — THE TWO SENTENCES ABOVE ABOUT THE LEFT MARGIN ARE WRONG, AND THE
  ERROR IS IN WHOSE MARGIN 16.8 IS. They are left standing because r219 really did reason that way and
  the reasoning propagated; read them as a record of a decision, not as a fact about LHDN.

  16.8 pt is the left edge of the SPECIMEN PNG — a rasterisation of another payroll vendor's rendering
  (item ece6d75d, Producer "Developer Express Inc. DXperience v23.2.5"). It is not a number LHDN
  published. Measured off the agency's own vector file with
  `node --import tsx scripts/measure-ea-official-pdf.ts --rects`:

      150 of the agency's body-table cells begin at x = 39.8, and NOTHING on its page begins left of it.
      The next columns in are 45.2 (the Date caption), 57.1 (156 cells) and 74.8 (214 cells).
      Its outer frame closes at ~557; ours runs to EA_FORM.right = 571.3.

  So the agency's page is NARROWER in content and WIDER in margin than ours: our left edge at 24 sits
  15.8 pt OUTSIDE the agency's, not inside it. There is no unprintable-margin conflict, and 24 is not a
  printability departure to be preserved — it is an un-remeasured leftover of the tracing, like the
  item and sub-item columns beside it. Re-deriving it belongs to the grid redraw (item 795c470c).

THE FILLED BLACK BLOCKS — MEASURED, NOT ESTIMATED (added 2026-09-27, item fda53352)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The reporter's red-boxed comparison showed the one thing that still makes our page read as a
different document: LHDN sets its section letters, its banner and its top-right heading as SOLID
BLACK BLOCKS WITH WHITE LETTERING. We draw plain text.

Measured off `ea-cp8a-pin-2023-official-en.pdf` rasterised at 150 dpi, by scanning for rows and
columns that are >55% ink within the block's own window. A longest-dark-run measure does NOT work
here: the blocks contain WHITE TEXT, which breaks the run and under-reports the height (the first
attempt returned 4.8 pt for a two-line block). Values are points from the top-left of the page.

    block                     top      height     x      width
    PRIVATE SECTOR heading    17.8      20.2     398.9   133.0     (two lines, English edition)
    banner                    78.2      14.9      39.8   516.0     (39.8 -> 555.8)
    section square A          96.5      12.5      39.8    17.3
    section square B         222.7      10.6      39.8    17.3
    section square C         443.0      10.1      39.8    17.3
    section square D         512.6       9.6      39.8    17.3
    section square E         641.8      10.6      39.8    17.3
    section square F         706.1      10.1      39.8    17.3

CONSISTENCY CHECKS, and they are why these numbers can be trusted:
  · EXACTLY SIX squares were found scanning the whole left margin — one per section, A through F,
    and nothing else. A detector picking up stray ink would not land on six.
  · Every square is at the SAME x (39.8) and the SAME width (17.3).
  · The banner spans 39.8 -> 555.8, which is the body's own right edge (556.6, from the table cells)
    to within rounding.
  · 39.8 is the body's left column edge, the same one the item captions sit against.

⚠️ THE HEIGHTS VARY (9.6 - 12.5) AND THAT IS NOT NECESSARILY REAL. A 10 pt square is ~21 px at
150 dpi, so a one-pixel threshold difference is ±0.5 pt. The squares may be sized to their row rather
than fixed. Trust the WIDTH (17.3, identical across all six) and re-check the height against the
drawn output rather than treating the spread above as six different sizes.

⚠️ AND THE LEFT MARGIN IS OURS — BUT NOT FOR THE REASON THIS NOTE USED TO GIVE (corrected r372).
EA_FORM.left is 24 and the blocks above are given in the AGENCY's coordinates, so a build must still
decide per block whether it follows the agency or our margin, and say which. That part stands.

What this note used to say, quoted so the claim is recognisable if it comes back:

    "⚠️ AND THE LEFT MARGIN IS OURS, DELIBERATELY. EA_FORM.left is 24 where the agency's rules start
     at 16.8, because the agency's margin is inside the unprintable area of most office printers."

It is backwards, and it contradicts the table immediately above it, which records every section square
at x 39.8. The agency's rules do NOT start at 16.8 — 16.8 is the left edge of the DevExpress specimen
PNG. `--rects` on the agency's own PDF counts 150 cells beginning at 39.8 and none left of it. Our 24 is
therefore 15.8 pt OUTSIDE the agency's body column, not inside a margin it could not print. There is no
printability conflict; 24 is simply un-remeasured, and item 795c470c re-derives it with the rest of
the grid.
