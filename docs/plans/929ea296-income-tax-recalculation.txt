THE SITUATION TODAY

Changing a Pay Item's tax settings does not correct historical taxable income. Payroll history can be imported and corrected, but there is no dedicated action to review and recalculate only its income tax basis.

WHAT WE'LL CHANGE

- Add Recalculate Income Tax Value under Payroll Settings. Choose one company, year, January through the selected month, and all eligible employees or named employees. Include former employees with history in that company.
- Preview monthly and year-to-date taxable and exempt income, differences, employee counts and the latest saved Pay Item settings. Show the previous treatment where recorded; otherwise say it is unavailable.
- Explain missing information and blocked employees before confirmation. Confirm the exact eligible employees, period and settings reviewed; changing any of them requires a new preview.
- Save an auditable tax-basis correction without replacing original payroll transactions, closed batches or issued payslips. Subsequent payroll calculations and newly generated applicable tax reports use the corrected basis, with its correction reference.
- Show batch history, actor, time, employee results, before-and-after values and reasons for failure. Retrying failed employees cannot duplicate successful corrections.

WHAT YOU'LL GET

Correct supported historical tax values without deleting and re-importing payroll history. See exactly which employees and months changed, with the original history retained.

GOOD TO KNOW

- Salary, allowances, deductions, actual PCB deducted, payments and existing statutory reports or submissions stay unchanged. Correcting or reissuing those follows the separate controlled process.
- Incomplete history is held for correction, never estimated. If changed annual exemptions would affect a later saved month's tax basis, extend the selected period and preview again; later months are never changed silently.
