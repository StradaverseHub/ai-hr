# Bounded recursive improvement for AI HR

21 November 2026 · Source inspection at `f27f355c8` · Research and pilot design

AI HR can use a model to propose improvements to a versioned Assistant task, then use newly observed failures to guide the next round. Start with an offline experiment on one read-only task, such as explaining the current caller’s leave entitlement from the canonical leave service. Keep permissions and HR decisions with their existing owners. A candidate earns promotion through independent evidence and a separate approver. The claim to demonstrate is that the loop produces repeatable gains on this task.

## What the research shows

- **DSPy.** Khattab et al. define language-model pipelines as modular programs and optimize those modules against an explicit metric. Their case studies show improvements on math and multi-hop question answering. DSPy provides a way to express the experiment. Its evaluation establishes performance on the tasks and models studied. [DSPy, ICLR 2024](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/f1cf02ce09757f57c3b93c0db83181e0-Paper-Conference.pdf)
- **GEPA.** Agrawal et al. use structured execution traces and textual failure feedback to propose prompt edits, keeping candidates that do well on different validation examples. The authors' revised paper reports a 6% average improvement over GRPO across six benchmark tasks and up to 35 times fewer rollouts. Reported gains depend on the benchmark and model. The train, validation and test splits in Appendix E.1 illustrate why candidate search and final evaluation need separate datasets. Rollout savings alone do not establish lower total cost. [GEPA, ICLR 2026, revised paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.19457v2)
- **Independent testing.** Dwork et al. show that adaptively reusing a holdout can overfit the holdout itself. Their formal protection relies on a controlled reusable-holdout mechanism. For AI HR, a practical starting point is to keep a fresh, independently maintained release holdout outside the optimizer's access, limit evaluation attempts and stop treating exposed cases as independent evidence. [Generalization in Adaptive Data Analysis and Holdout Reuse, NeurIPS 2015](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2015/hash/bad5f33780c42f2588878a9d07405083-Abstract.html)
- **Limited exposure and recovery.** Google SRE describes canaries as partial, time-limited deployments evaluated against a control. The operating pattern supports a scoped trial and a fast return to a known baseline. AI HR still needs its own evidence on trial admission, measurements and fallback. [Google SRE Workbook, Canarying Releases](https://sre.google/workbook/canarying-releases/)

The papers support trying an offline optimizer that can draft changes from diagnostic feedback. They leave AI HR's quality gains, cross-locale and workflow generalization, spend, latency and effectiveness over multiple releases as experimental questions.

## Pilot protocol

1. **Choose the task and target.** Begin with source-grounded leave entitlement explanations, including questions that require a clarification or a reasoned refusal. Freeze the provider and model configuration, the approved source version, baseline, scoring contract, allowed prompt component, rollout budget, spend cap and run deadline. Use fictional employments, leave policies and questions. Draw expected facts from the canonical fixture and score the explanation against those facts. Let scoped, already-authorized aggregate signals select a failure category. Recreate it as synthetic examples before providing feedback to the optimizer. [ADR-0151 §4.6](../adr/0151-orchestrator-action-layer-and-system-learning.md) · [Source-owned leave explanations](../../lib/ai/assistant-answer-explanations.ts)
2. **Generate a bounded candidate offline.** Compare a manually revised strategy and a GEPA-generated strategy with the same existing baseline. Use the same starting task contract and measure total tokens, spend and time across both candidate generation and evaluation. Give the optimizer search examples, a separate search-validation set and concise diagnostics from source and schema checks. Keep it outside serving, with fixture access and no HR write or promotion credentials. Export a reviewable diff and the run manifest. The current serving contract requires a reviewed, named variant in source or a separate reviewed change to the artifact contract. A free-form GEPA export has no direct import path. [DSPy GEPA optimizer documentation](https://github.com/stanfordnlp/dspy/blob/main/docs/docs/api/optimizers/GEPA/overview.md) · [Conversation strategy contract](../../lib/ai/assistant-conversation-candidate.ts)
3. **Evaluate the frozen candidate.** Preserve the existing suite as a stable regression gate. Add a separately maintained release holdout with fictional scenario families disjoint from search examples and from earlier disclosed holdouts. Use two independent evaluators under the existing harness. The new holdout runs as a supplemental evaluator-owned release check because the harness currently verifies only the fixed-suite digest. Bind the supplemental report to the same frozen candidate, baseline, build and provider configuration. Keep holdout inputs, expected answers and case-level results with evaluators. Return only the promotion decision and controlled aggregate results. Move an exposed case into the regression suite and replace it before reuse as a release holdout. Run the candidate and baseline on the same cases, with comparable model settings and repeated runs where sampling matters. If the holdout compares both GEPA and manual candidates, fix the comparison and selection rule before opening it. [Adaptive holdout research](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2015/hash/bad5f33780c42f2588878a9d07405083-Abstract.html) · [Release harness](../../core/module-agent-releases.ts)
4. **Apply hard gates before quality comparisons.** Require zero security and quality failures on mandated cases, including scope isolation, injection resistance, sensitive-data disclosure, ambiguous inputs and unsupported effects. Then compare the primary task metric, such as the rate of correct answers supported by the approved source, alongside routing or clarification correctness, response latency and cost. Predeclare a minimum meaningful gain, uncertainty criterion and acceptable latency/cost bounds before inspecting results. Report per-locale and workflow counts and paired outcome differences. If the sample cannot resolve the threshold, mark the result inconclusive and keep the baseline. [ADR-0151 §4.6](../adr/0151-orchestrator-action-layer-and-system-learning.md) · [GEPA §4 and Appendix E.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.19457v2)
5. **Rehearse trial and rollback.** A separate authorized approver can admit only the frozen candidate that earned the receipts. Test on the exact staging build with the approved scope, job limit and expiry. Exercise explicit rollback, source or provider mismatch, expiry, exhaustion and runtime refusal. Verify through the next eligible Assistant request that the known baseline is selected, where the relevant guard permits a request, and inspect the recorded candidate and rollback receipts. Review any already-committed HR effect through the owning service's existing correction path. Application deployment and production authorization follow repository policy. [Release harness](../../core/module-agent-releases.ts) · [Repository and environment policy](../REPOSITORY-AND-ENVIRONMENTS.md) · [Google SRE Workbook](https://sre.google/workbook/canarying-releases/)
6. **Repeat from an accepted artifact.** Record the parent strategy, candidate digest, optimizer settings, fixture versions, independent receipts, decision and trial outcome. Feed eligible failure categories back into search for the next round. Compare the next candidate with its accepted parent to measure incremental gain and with the canonical baseline to check cumulative benefit. The current harness compares against its canonical baseline, so the parent comparison is additional offline evidence. Keep the canonical baseline available for recovery. Two completed cycles with independently scored gains, bounded trial receipts and a successful rollback drill would demonstrate repeatability on this one task. If a cycle yields no qualifying gain, retain the approved strategy and record the result. [Release harness](../../core/module-agent-releases.ts) · [Adaptive holdout research](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2015/hash/bad5f33780c42f2588878a9d07405083-Abstract.html)

The pilot should decide whether the optimizer earns a continuing role as a candidate author. Expanding to more tasks requires task-specific metrics, source and actor fixtures, and independent gates through their owning workflows.
