Announcements

A line in the bar for the people you pick: until the cycle opens, while a part is open, or between dates you pick. The first two follow the cycle calendar, so they move if it does. No email goes with it. Each shows on the pages you tick (the dashboard unless you tick more; the AI+ check-in only when ticked) and stays there until it ends. Several share one line, which runs across the bar, round and round, when it doesn't fit.
