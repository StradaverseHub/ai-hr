How the numbers work. A behaviour answered Demonstrate scores {{ '%g'|format(pts[0]) }} at the person's own tier and {{ '%g'|format(pts[1]) }} where it is a stretch to the next one{% if pts[2] or pts[3] %}; one answered Developing scores {{ '%g'|format(pts[2]) }} and {{ '%g'|format(pts[3]) }}{% endif %} (the points set for this cycle). A person's figure is the mean across the assessors who submitted. Where an extreme-assessor correction applies it adjusts the overall score only, so the seven principles can sum to slightly more or less than the figure in the Score column.

Bands. The overall band uses Group People's thresholds (Low ≤ {{ '%g'|format(ctx['lowMax']) }}, Mid ≤ {{ '%g'|format(ctx['midMax']) }}). Per-principle bands are derived by scaling those to each principle's share of the total.

Who is in scope. Everyone with at least one submitted assessment this cycle, excluding JG H. Anyone still short of the minimum submissions shows as withheld, and the per-principle breakdown is withheld with the score.
