Start the next cycle

Starting a cycle makes it the one active cycle and archives every other; people only ever see the active one. Before you start: bring the headcount up to date on People › Import, then build the panels on 360 Feedback › Panels.

The lowest job grade decides who takes part at all: below it, and every letter grade, has no review, isn't rated and doesn't rate others in this cycle. Blank is everyone; change it later on Participants.

The cycle type decides who works on what. The Early Year, after the Year-End closes, is KPI setting and its approval only. The Mid-Year checks in on those KPIs: self, line manager and HOD. The Year-End adds the 360, competency, the BU CEO, BG CEO and the Talent Box, and is when plans are written for the year ahead. The period (opens to closes) holds every part's dates; the opening date also decides who takes part. Malaysia time.

KPIs carry in from: a Mid-Year normally carries in its Early Year's agreed KPIs, for the people taking part. Results, approvals and files never carry over.
