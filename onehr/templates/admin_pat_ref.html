Record each unit's profit after tax (PAT) achievement. It picks the curve its ratings are calibrated against; no figure means the normal curve.

Why this matters

The curve shifts with how the unit actually performed: a unit that missed budget is calibrated against a tighter spread than one that beat it. Leave a figure blank and that unit is calibrated against the normal curve and flagged as not yet entered. Calibration goes business unit by business unit, so a business group has no figure of its own. These curves are guidance, not a quota: nothing is blocked for missing them.
