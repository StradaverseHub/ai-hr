How it works
  • Four steps: choose the file; OneHR checks its quality (what can't go in, what's worth a look); it shows what it would change and asks the super admin anything it shouldn't guess; then the final picture, and you apply that same file.
  • Each row is one KPI for one person, so a person can have many rows. The same KPI can repeat across many people (unit, department or team KPIs).
  • Business group, business unit and department come from the person's own profile, not the file. How the business group scores KPIs (the Scoring setting) decides which columns a row must carry: Rate 1–5 for a rating ladder, or Threshold / Target plus Multiple or Excellence for a threshold-to-target score.
  • One moderator per KPI. Leave Matrix manager ID blank and the person's line manager moderates that KPI; put a matrix manager's Employee ID and that KPI goes to them alone. Once every KPI is moderated, the person goes to the HOD, then the BU CEO, then the BG CEO.
  • A KPI their unit carries as a BU KPI (in BU KPI setup) is flagged for the super admin to decide: the unit's BU KPI, with its one target, or the person's own line.
  • The old Approver 1/2/3 columns are gone. A file that still fills them in is refused, so download the new template.
