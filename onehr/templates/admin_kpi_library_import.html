How it works
  • Each row is one reusable KPI. Its scope comes from the file's columns Business Group, Company, Department, Tier and Job Grade (the library isn't per person). Leave Company, Department, Tier and Job Grade blank to mean “any”: a row blank on all four is the business group's fallback. The most specific match wins for a given employee (job grade, then grade band, then department, then business unit).
  • Each row says what the KPI is: KPI, Strategic Thrust / Category, KPI Description, Unit of Measure and Method (More or Less: which way is better). Perspective (optional) is Financial, Customer, Internal Process or Learning & Growth; blank leaves it as it is.
  • No targets, rates or weights. They are set on each person's line, and a unit's in BU KPI setup. A file that still has Rate, Threshold, Target, Multiple, Excellence, Weightage or Spread columns goes in, and those columns are ignored.
  • BU Level (optional): Yes marks the KPI as one figure Group People fills for the whole unit, No as personal; blank leaves it as it is (a new KPI is personal).

Four steps, all or nothing. The file is checked first and stops (nothing changes) if any row names a business group you don't manage, has an invalid grade band, or is missing its KPI name or method. Then OneHR shows what each KPI does and asks about a new name close to one the library has, or a BU-level change on a KPI people carry; then you apply that same file.
