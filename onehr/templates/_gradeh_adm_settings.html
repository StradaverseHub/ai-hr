Early opening for HODs and BU CEOs

An HOD's Grade H review opens as soon as every Grade H person under them has acknowledged (own sign-in, in person or deemed), and at the latest on the HOD date. A BU CEO's sign-off for a unit opens as soon as every Grade H person in it has finished the HOD stage (or has none), and at the latest on the BU CEO date. Each is worked out per HOD and per unit. Neither opens before its part's first calendar day, and both still close on the calendar's closing day. Leave a date blank for no early opening: that part then opens on its calendar day alone.
