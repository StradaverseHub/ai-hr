How it works
  • One row a person: their Employee ID and the job code of the job they hold, at their own job grade (a JG 14 takes a code ending -14). Assign competencies lists the jobs and their codes.
  • Job scope only where a code has more than one. Left blank, it's the scope of their own placement, else the job with none; the import says when it can't tell.
  • Their job is placed in the job architecture and they get that job's competencies, as Place a job on their page does. Someone who has started their self-assessment, whose manager has reviewed it, or whose potential result is out keeps their list as it is; their job is still placed.
  • A row with only an Employee ID is skipped, so a file filled in part goes in. Columns headed Reference: aren't read.

Four steps, all or nothing. Choose the file; OneHR checks it (a row it can't place stops the file until you fix it); it shows what changes and asks the super admin about anyone whose competency list is already held; then the final picture, and you apply that same file.
