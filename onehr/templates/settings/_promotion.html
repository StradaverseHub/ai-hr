Standing rules

Each rule reads N consecutive years at a talent category or better, with no promotion inside that window. A gap year breaks the run rather than being skipped — an unknown year is not a qualifying year. Rules are checked top down and the first match wins, so put the longer, more forgiving rule first.

Consecutive yearsAt this category or above

Leave the years blank (or set 0) to turn a rule off. Turning all three off is allowed and means exactly what it says: nobody is surfaced automatically, and every nomination is made by hand.

What a rule is not: it does not promote anybody, and it is not a queue. It puts a name in front of the people who decide, with the reason printed next to it. Someone the rules miss can still be nominated; someone the rules surface can still be passed over.

History window

Reading {{ history_years }} years of talent status — the longest rule above, or five years, whichever is more.

Not a separate setting on purpose: a window shorter than the longest rule would starve that rule silently. It would still sit on this page looking configured, and never fire.

How far a nomination may reach

The grades a nomination may propose: the person's own, plus this many above it, capped at the top of the ladder (JG 12–23).

Somebody on a non-numeric grade (H, FW, INTERN) is offered the bottom rungs of the numeric ladder instead — promotion out of one of those lands in the ladder rather than moving along it, so “+{{ grade_reach }}” would mean nothing there.
