What each answer on the 360 earns. A behaviour at the person's current level and one at the next level can earn different points. Why this matters

The points add up to the person's 360 score, which with their competency makes the potential score and band. A next-level behaviour is a stretch, so it usually earns more. Changing points re-scores everyone in a cycle that follows them. Once a cycle's first potential result is released, its points are fixed.
