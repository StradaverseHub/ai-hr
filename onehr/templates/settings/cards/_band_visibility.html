Choose whether employees and managers see the potential band (Low, Mid or High) on their 360 feedback pages.

Why this matters

The potential band isn't part of every round's 360 assessment. When this is off, the band is hidden from the four places employees and managers see it — a person's own My Feedback page (and its printed PDF), a manager's report view of a direct report, Team view and HOD view. Turning it on restores all four exactly as before.

Admin views always show the band whatever this says — the Overview distribution, a person's 360 results, Reports and its PDF, and every Excel export. The calibration pages are also unaffected: deciding the band is their whole point, so their own permissions govern them. This setting changes only what is displayed — no score, band or report release is affected, and it doesn't change who can open a report.
