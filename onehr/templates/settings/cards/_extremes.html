A rater who gave their whole panel the same answer this cycle is flagged on Assessor extremes. Turn this on to correct their scores before each panel average is worked out.

Why this matters

When this is on, a rater who always said No has their own score raised by the bonus (up to the highest possible score), and a rater who always said Yes has theirs lowered by the penalty (down to zero). The correction is made before the person's panel average is worked out. If several flagged raters sit on one panel, each is corrected separately, so the effect adds up. Once on, it applies live everywhere a score or band is shown, including the person's own report, Team view and HOD view.

+

Raises that rater's own score, up to the highest possible.

−

Lowers that rater's own score, down to zero.
