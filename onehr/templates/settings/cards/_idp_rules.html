Why this matters

On requiring all three: 70:20:10 is a description of how people tend to learn, not a quota each goal owes. Requiring all three turns a prompt into paperwork and tends to produce a padded course line rather than a better plan. It's here because some organisations genuinely want it — but at least one is the default for a reason.
