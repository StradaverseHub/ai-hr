Why this matters

A calibration can quietly turn into re-banding most of a unit, or leave a unit with far more Stars than its results support. These limits keep the spread honest. The moves limit counts people whose talent category changes in a session; a move inside a category, or back to where someone started, is free. The Stars limit counts who sits in Star and Super Star now, against the figures for the PAT curve the unit follows. When a limit is on it is hard: the box shows it to BG CEOs and Group Chiefs, refuses a move past it, and won't lock a unit over its Star or Super Star limit. You can change any figure here.
