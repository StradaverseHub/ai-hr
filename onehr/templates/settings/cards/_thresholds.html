Where the Low, Mid and High potential bands start and end. A person's potential score (the 360 and the competency, weighted as above) is read on the 360's own scale, 0–{{ '%g'|format(sample_max) }}.

Why this matters

Each rater scores a person out of {{ '%g'|format(sample_max) }} (a Demonstrate earns {{ '%g'|format(pts360.dc) }} at the person's current level and {{ '%g'|format(pts360.dn) }} at the next; a Developing earns {{ '%g'|format(pts360.vc) }} and {{ '%g'|format(pts360.vn) }}, set under Points per answer). A person's 360 score is the average of the submitted ratings. That is then combined with their approved competency score by the weighting on this page and read on the same scale. Moving a cutoff moves everyone near it: a band read from a score is read again on the new numbers, while a band someone set in calibration, or one the BG CEO locked, stays.
