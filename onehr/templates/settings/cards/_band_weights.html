Why this matters

Each half is taken as a share of its own maximum, then weighted by this split and added up on the total of both maximums — on {{ '%g'|format(sample_max) }} + {{ '%g'|format(sample_tc_max) }} points, the 360 can add up to its share of the total and the competency up to the rest. The AI insight in the department review shows each part. Weighting the shares (not adding raw points) is what makes the split you set the split that counts.

Someone with no line-manager-approved competency assessment is banded on their 360 alone — the missing half is never counted as a zero.
