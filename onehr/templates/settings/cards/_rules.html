The default rules for panel size, when a person's score counts, and the job-grade bands. Saving applies them to the current cycle and every future one; past cycles keep their own rules.

Why this matters

These are the defaults for the whole organisation. A cycle that needs different rules can have its own override, set at the foot of this card. Each grade band is rated on its own set of behaviours, so the ranges below decide which behaviours a person is rated on.
