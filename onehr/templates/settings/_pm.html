Settings › Scoring

How each business group scores a KPI, and which band a score falls in. Pick a group to see its settings. A group uses one method, never a mix. Where each band starts belongs to a cycle: this page shows the cycle in the top bar.

{% if not can_edit %}

Read only for you. Scoring rules are the super admin's: changing a method or a cut-off re-scores every scorecard in the group. They are shown here because they score your people.

{% endif %}
How the three methods differ

5-point rating: every KPI carries its own Rate 1–5 table, and the weighted average maps to a label through one table shared by every 5-point group (its starts are the cycle's own).

Achievement %: every KPI carries a threshold and a target, and the weighted achievement % maps to a label through that business group's own cutoff table, set below for the cycle. The two forms differ only above target: one keeps scaling, the other stops at the excellence figure. A unit, or chosen departments of it, can start the bands elsewhere (a rule); a department's rule wins over its unit's, and a unit's over the group's.
