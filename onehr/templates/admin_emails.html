When a KPI or competency changes

Super admin

When Group People adds a KPI line or a competency after someone has started or submitted, or swaps their competencies, that person gets one email naming what changed and what to fill in. Only the new item opens for them: everything they already submitted stays as it is. Changing a target, a method, a unit or a weight, taking a line off, and a Reopen send no such email. A save that reaches many people goes out in waves.
