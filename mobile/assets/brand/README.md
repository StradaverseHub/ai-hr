# Approved OneHR+ mark

The owner approved the flat mint vector on 2 November 2026: HR, superscript + at the upper right, extended sideways numeral 1 behind HR. All four paths inherit the single opaque fill #A6F2D5; background #080B09. No gradient or texture belongs in the mark.

`onehr-logo.svg` is the editable source. The app icon is a 1024px opaque PNG. Android foreground and monochrome assets have transparent, padded canvases; the monogram occupies about 61% of the adaptive canvas width. Splash and favicon exports use the same geometry. Exports were rendered from this SVG with @resvg/resvg-js, rather than recoloring a generated raster. Local exploratory images under `assets/brand-review` are not runtime inputs and are excluded from EAS uploads.
